title: The iPhone and iPad app opens on home
line: The app opens on what needs you across your machines, boards and sessions, then each board with its lane bar and lead, then your channels and DMs.
---
Home is the current project's conversations, sorted by what needs you, as the
desktop's Overview reads them: a pane that asks or failed on any of your
machines, a board's question for you, a PR whose checks fail or that waits for
review, and a hosted session waiting on you, worst first. Each open board is a
row with its lane bar, how much is done, what's going on and who leads it; it
opens the board's thread, where a post reaches the lead, and Board opens its
lanes. The tabs from before are in home's menu. On an iPad, home is the left
column and what it opens sits beside it.
