section: Changed
title: A team owner sees a teammate's machine as a teammate
line: On a teammate's own machine, a team's owner now watches the sessions in the team's projects and what's shared with them, as any teammate does, not every session there.
---
A team owner still owns the team's boxes and its cloud workspaces, and what they may do to a teammate's machine in Arugula cloud doesn't change. What they see on it is what a teammate sees: the sessions in the team's projects, as a viewer, and anything shared with them. The machine's own account is its owner, as before.
