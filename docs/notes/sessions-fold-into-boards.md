The Sessions page leaves Plan's bar and the phone. Its pieces live where the work is:

- **On the board.** Each card's sessions show on its row in Lanes, and in its drawer in Lanes or Steps: a computer's session put on the card, an agent that isn't attached to the row, a cloud workspace's session. Each shows its state, and what waits on you comes first. Finished sessions are left out. Click a computer's session to open its pane in Build.
- **In Build.** **Agents only**, beside the machine filter on Build's list of every machine's panes, shows only the panes with an agent in them. The two filters work together.
- **Under Build › Machines.** **Runs** and **Workspaces** are buttons at the top of the Machines list. A run's unread count is on Build now.

Putting a session on a card, or moving it to another card, is in the session's ⋯ menu. An old link to `/p/<project>/sessions` opens the project's Boards. A session's own page, `/p/<project>/sessions/ses_…`, stays where it was. The Label, Tracker, Asked by and Going only filters are gone with the page.
