section: Changed
title: A Doc's outline says where you are
line: On the Docs pages a row is just a Doc's title and time, the facts above it say how long it is, and its outline marks the section you're reading.
---
The bar on each Doc's row is gone: it drew the Doc's shape, which nobody could
read. A Doc's length is now in words above it, such as "8 sections · 12 min
read". The outline beside it is plain headings, without the length bars: the
section you're reading is marked as you scroll, ◧ marks a section that holds a
chart, picture or prototype, and a click on a heading still goes there.
