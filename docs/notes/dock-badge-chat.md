title: Chat on the Dock badge
line: The desktop app's Dock icon now counts your unread mentions and DMs, added to the panes that need you, and shows a dot when chat has unread messages and nothing more.
---
The count follows the Chat button's, across every project: mentions, DMs, and rooms you set to every message. A muted room adds only its mentions. Threads on issues and runs stay on their lists and the inbox, so they don't add to it. With everything read and nothing waiting on you, there's no badge.
