---
section: Changed
title: A team watches only its projects' sessions
line: On a team's machine, the team's members watch the sessions in the team's projects, and no longer see the ones in your own projects.
---
Who sees a session follows its project. A session in one of the team's
projects is watch-only for the team's members: they type in it only when you
share it with them as editors, or trust them with a pane. A session in one of
your own projects, another team's project or none is yours, and whoever you
shared it with. Moving a session to another project changes who sees it at
once. A share with a person stays wherever the session goes; a share with a
team counts only while the session is in that team's project. Until Arugula
cloud says which team a project is in, no team sees its sessions.
