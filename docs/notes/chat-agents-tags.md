In #agents and a board's channel, typing `@` lists `@channel`, `@here`, `@cos` (your chief of staff) and each board's lead. In #agents, the delivery rule is written under the list. A tag is sent with its board's id, so renaming a board doesn't break it. Tags show as pills.

The line under the box you write in says who a post reaches: "Every lead gets this, no tag needed" in #agents, and "Its lead gets this · @here adds active workers" in a board's channel. Once Arugula cloud reports it, each post shows who it reached, such as "✓ reached: Design lift, A2P" or "✓ reached: no lead · it tagged none".
