title: The Boards list follows the board you change
line: Closing, reopening or renaming a board on the Boards page moves its row in the list at once, and on a phone Escape on a row's menu closes only the menu.

Before, a board closed in the middle of the Boards page stayed with the open ones until a reload. On a phone, Escape on a row's ⋯ menu also took you back from the board to the list.
