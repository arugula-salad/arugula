"Where you were" on Machines is now one place for your account, not one
per device. Open a pane on your computer and the iPhone, iPad and Android
apps light that pane's row as where you were; open one in an app and the
web lights it. On an iPad or a tablet it's also what the right side shows
before you pick a session.

When the app can't reach Arugula, it shows the pane you last opened on
that device.
