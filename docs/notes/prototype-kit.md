Every Doc prototype carries a small kit: `--ar-` colors that follow the Doc's light or dark, four text sizes, and `ar-btn`, `ar-chip`, `ar-header`, `ar-row`, `ar-dialog`, `ar-menu`, `ar-empty` and `ar-toolbar` classes. A prototype that uses none of them looks as it did, and agents are told the kit is there.
