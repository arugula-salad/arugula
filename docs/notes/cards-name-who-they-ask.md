title: Cards say whom they ask
line: A card says "asks you" only when the question or the waiting session is yours; a teammate's says whom it asks, such as "asks Marcus", and doesn't count as needing you.

On the Overview, its boards and in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, a
card no longer says "asks you" because a teammate's agent on it is waiting.
It names the person, or says "asks someone" when Arugula can't tell. A
board's row opens by itself, and counts what needs you, only for yours.
