title: Back across projects, and flip back
line: "Escape goes back a place, across projects, and `g ;` (or ⌃Tab where the browser lets it through) flips to where you were in your last other project."
---
The app keeps one back stack for this tab, across projects, and a reload keeps it. Escape goes back a place when nothing else wants it: not from a field, a terminal, a dialog or an open menu, and not when the page uses Escape itself (zooming out, leaving the Swarm). Flip back goes to the last place you were in a different project; press it again to come back. Go to…, opened empty, now starts with the places you were lately, across projects.
