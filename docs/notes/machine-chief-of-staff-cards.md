Ask the chief of staff on one of your machines to get an agent on an issue, or to close some agents, and it puts a card with Approve and Deny in your staff room, under Needs you and on your phone, as a hosted one does. Approve starts the agent on that machine, in a new worktree of the repo the chief of staff works in, attached to the issue's row, or closes the agent blocks. Only you can answer it, and nothing said in chat does. Your answer wakes the chief of staff.
