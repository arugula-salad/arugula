---
section: Changed
title: Remote input off holds for shared sessions too
line: With remote input off, someone a session is shared with only watches it, as everyone who comes through Arugula cloud does.
headline: no
---
`arugulad sharing remote-input off` makes people who come through Arugula cloud viewers. That now holds whatever is shared with them: a session shared with them or their team as an editor, or their role in the machine's team, no longer lets them type while it's off. Other people's devices are viewers too; only the devices of the machine's own account are as they were. A channel that opened while remote input was on closes when it goes off, and the person's client opens a new one as a viewer.
