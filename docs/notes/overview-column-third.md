The column beside the list used to be a narrow strip with a wide empty middle. It now opens at a third of the page. Drag the divider between the list and the column (or press Left and Right on it) to make it wider or narrower, between 18rem and half the page; double-click it to go back to a third. Arugula remembers your width on this device. A phone and a narrow window keep the stacked layout, with no divider.
