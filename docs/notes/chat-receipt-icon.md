section: Changed
title: Who got a message, in one small icon
line: In Chat, a message to agents shows one small icon by its time; hover or tap it to see who got it, and a board channel's post now shows its lead as reached.
headline: no
---
A message that tags agents, or reaches leads in #agents or a board's
channel, used to carry one line per agent and a `reached:` line under it.
Now it has one small icon after its time: grey when everyone read or
answered it, in the accent colour while it's on its way, and amber when
something is waiting, offline, silenced or not granted. A number beside the
icon says how many agents got it.

Hover the icon, or tap it on a phone, for a card listing each agent and
where it got to: "read", "answered ↗" (which opens the reply), or "waiting:
no session took it". When a machine has no session by the name you used, the
card lists the names it does have, and picking one puts it in the box you
write in. In a board's channel, the card marks the board's lead, which now
shows as reached rather than "no lead".
