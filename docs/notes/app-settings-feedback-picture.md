section: Changed
title: The apps open Settings in the app and show your picture
line: In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, Settings opens in the app signed in as you, Send feedback sends from the app, and your picture shows at the top.
needs: control
---
Settings was the web's page in your browser, and Send feedback sent you there too. Now Settings opens inside the app, already signed in: the app hands its sign-in over once, and leaving Settings signs that page out again. As in the desktop app's window, adding a passkey, linking GitHub, changing your email and deleting your account stay in a browser. Send feedback sends from the sheet, and your picture replaces your initials.
