title: Arrows, closers and ticks are icons
line: Buttons draw their arrows, closers and ticks as icons, and every icon-only button says what it does.
headline: no
---
The ▾ ▸ ‹ › on menus and folds, the × on dialogs and chips, ⋯, ↗, ✓, ↻ and ✎ are drawn with the same icons as the rail, at four sizes, so they line up with the text beside them in every font. An icon-only button has both a hover tip and a name for screen readers. A Doc prototype's kit has `ar-icon` and `--ar-icon-xs` to `--ar-icon-lg` for the same.
