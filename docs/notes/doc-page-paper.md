A project Doc's page draws the Doc as a sheet of paper. Beside it, On this page lists its written sections, marking the one you're reading and the ones with a figure (◧); a click scrolls to it. Under that, Linked lists the boards it's on, which used to be a line under the Doc. Comments stay in the margin beside their paragraph, now highlighted in the Doc's own blue. In the app, the agent that wrote the Doc is a tab of the agent panel, folded to a strip: `\` opens it. Folding it there doesn't change your panel on other pages.
