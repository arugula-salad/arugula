title: Your projects on the rail
line: The rail shows a mark for each of your projects with what's for you there; one click lands where you left it, and the current one opens your projects.
---
The rail is one rail on every page now, a machine's own page included: Arugula's mark, your projects' marks (the current one ringed; hover one for where you left it), the five places, then the Inbox, Settings and you. Past five projects it shows the current one and the list. The Machines place no longer counts what's new in runs' threads (Runs, in Machines, still does), and your account menu is under your initials at the rail's foot on a machine's page too.
