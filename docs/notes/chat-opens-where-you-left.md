Chat remembers, on each device and for each account, the room (and thread) you were last in, per project. Open Chat from the rail or the app's Chat tab, or reload, and you land there, at the place #2498 keeps in it. A room that has gone, or that you no longer have, opens as before: the newest unread, then the newest.

Unread in another room shows in the sidebar and doesn't move you. A link or a notification to a room still opens that room, and it's the one chat comes back to. On a phone, Back from the room you were taken to is the room list, and if you leave from the list, Chat opens the list.
