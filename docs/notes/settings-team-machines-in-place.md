Each machine of yours has Settings, Take out of team (or Move into a team, under Just you) and Remove, each asked first; a team's owners take out its members' machines too. Bring in one of yours shares a machine you have with the team, and Add a machine is the `arugulad join` command with Copy (with `--team` for a team's). A machine whose Arugula is from before control's moves says so. Your machines left You › Sign-in, which is now Sign-in and teams. While your control doesn't serve these on the app's address yet, moving and removing still open the machines page.
