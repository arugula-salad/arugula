title: Move a Doc to your team
line: A Doc on your machine can move to your team's project from the Docs page, `arugula doc move` or an agent's `doc move`, and its open pane and old id follow it there.

The team Doc is made from the Doc as it is now, with its sections, pictures and layout, and the machine keeps only a hidden pointer to it: the Doc leaves the machine's Docs list, and its copy on the platform comes down. A pane showing it, an agent still filling it, `doc open` of its old id and a board's link all go on with the team's Doc. A public link made before the move keeps working, as it was. If the team Doc can't be made whole, nothing moves.

On the platform, a personal Doc's Share with <team> is now Move to <team>…, and asks first: the team will own it, its link keeps working, and nobody keeps a copy. A Doc written there moves in place, keeping its id, thread and pictures. A machine's copy is moved from its machine, which the action says.
