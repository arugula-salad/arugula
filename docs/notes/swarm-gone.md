---
section: Removed
title: Swarm is gone; Machines and Running take its place
line: The Swarm view is gone. Machines lists every machine's sessions, and Running shows every live process; old Swarm links open Machines.
---
The Swarm view, every pane on every machine as a field, is gone. Machines lists each machine's sessions one row each, and the top bar's **Running** shows every live process across your machines, with the alerts first. An old Swarm link (`#swarm`, or the Overview's Swarm address) opens Machines, at the pane it named where it can. A gate or a decision waiting on you still shows in its workspace block, on the phone's sheet and in Needs you.
