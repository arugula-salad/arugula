title: Who can reach a machine
line: Access, in a machine's settings and as `arugula access`, lists everyone who reaches the machine and as what, each row saying whose word it is.

One screen and one command answer "who can reach this machine?": its owner, the team's people by their role from the roster the machine checked itself, each session's shares and their floors, links and when they end, ssh guests, and trust live now. Each row says whose word it is: this machine's, or the team's roster. The machine's agents' powers are listed under them, labelled as Arugula cloud's word. Nothing changes in what's enforced, and the machine stores and sends nothing new for it.
