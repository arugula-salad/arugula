---
section: Changed
title: Move machines and share sessions without a device key
line: Signed in on any browser, you can put a machine in a team or take it out, and share a session with someone who signed up without a device key.
needs: control
---
Moving one of your machines into a team or back to just you, and a team owner taking a member's machine out, no longer needs a signature from one of your devices: Arugula cloud takes the move from you on its page (aru-141), and the machine takes it as Arugula cloud says. Machines from before this release only take a move a device signed, so Arugula cloud asks you to update them first. Sharing a session now finds people who have no device key too: they reach it with Arugula cloud's machine tokens, with no root device to pin or check.
