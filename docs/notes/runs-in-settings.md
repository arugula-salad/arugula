---
section: Changed
title: Runs move to Running and the project's Settings
line: The Runs tab is gone. What runs now is the top bar's Running, and New run and run specs are in the project's Settings, under Runs.
---
The Runs tab under Machines is gone. **Running**, in the top bar, shows what runs now across your machines, with the project's CI runs and alerts. **New run**, the project's **run specs** with their schedules and event rules, and the standing grant for runs are in the project's Settings, under **Runs**. A run keeps its own page, and an old Runs link opens Running.

The attention drawer is gone too: Needs you on the Overview, Running and each pane's own mark cover what it showed. A tab's head line keeps Tamed, Grid and + Pane; a pane's header still has Focus.
