Pick a machine at the top of This machine. Its own settings are read from the machine itself, so a machine the app can't reach, or one that's offline, says so in each section's summary. What its agents may do on Arugula cloud is one section per machine, with a switch per project and for your DMs. It replaces both *Agents on your machines* under You and the read-only *Agents' grants…* in the machine's menu, which now opens this section. An old link to `/settings#agents` lands here.
