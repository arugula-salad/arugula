Arugula's keys now follow one list, with a small core you learn first: `j`/`k`
to move, ↵ to open, Esc to go back, `l` for the panel, `a` to answer, `/` to
find, `s` for your chief of staff, `?` for the keys, ⌘K for Go to…, and the
`g` places (`g o` `g i` `g c` `g b` `g d` `g m`, and now `g a` for #agents).

- **Needs you:** `a` answers the first way (it was `y`). `e` and `n` have no
  key by default: ↵ and `j` do their jobs.
- **A board:** `a` writes to the lead (it was `l`), `n` adds a row (it was
  `a`), and `/` filters. `l` and `f` are now the panel's everywhere.
- **The panel:** `s` flips to the chief of staff's tab and back. `h` has no
  key by default: Esc leaves the panel.
- **Look:** `s`, `t` and `d` no longer open Look, flip light and dark, or step
  the depth. The top bar's Look does all three.
- **Chat:** ⌘K is the room switcher there, and `?` now lists it.
- **Panes:** Ctrl+Shift+Z focuses one pane and restores the layout (it was
  Ctrl+Shift+Enter, which a terminal program may want).
- **Gone:** `g p` (⌘K is Go to…), `[` on list pages (the fold's button does
  it), and Ctrl+Tab in the browser, which keeps it anyway (`g ;` flips).
