title: Run this board and Revoke on iPhone and Android
line: On the iPhone and Android apps, a board with no lead offers Run this board, and a board's grant shows under its lead with Revoke.
needs: control

In a team's project, a board no one leads and nothing is granted offers **Run this board** on the iPhone's Board level, in place of "Ask chief of staff to start one", and at the head of the board's thread on Android. Its card goes in your staff room, as it does from the web. A board with a grant says where its lead runs, which machines its issue agents may use, how many at once and until when; the person who gave it can press **Revoke** there.
