section: Changed
title: The Overview is the chief of staff's view
line: The Overview's Boards and Running are panes now. Your chief of staff can open Docs, runs and prompts beside them or in tabs, and Reset puts it back.
needs: control
---
The Overview is now tabs of panes, as a board's desk is. Tab 1 is the title line over its own panes, Boards and Running. Needs you and the chief of staff's panel stay on the right, on every tab.

Your chief of staff can open a Doc, a PR, a run or #agents beside Boards and Running while you talk. Boards becomes a list and the right column narrows to a quarter. It never takes your focus or switches your tab, and if you were busy in the last 4 seconds its pane arrives quietly with a dot. Something that waits on you, such as a review or an agent's permission prompt, gets a tab of its own, and Needs you gets a row with "tab N" that opens it. A failing run can stand in place of Boards for a while, and ↺ Boards puts Boards back.

While tab 1 differs from the default, the header says "changed by chief of staff" and when, with Reset beside it. A pane you close stays closed until you Reset, and the ⋯ menu reopens what was closed. `1`–`4` pick a tab.

Cuts:
- On a phone, the Overview stays one column with no panes or tabs.
- A PR pane is a link to the PR, not the PR itself.
