section: Added
title: Run this board
line: One card starts a board's lead on one of your machines and lets it start issue agents there, up to 3 at once, until the board closes.
needs: control
---
Press **Run this board** on a board with no lead, on the Overview or on the board's page, or say yes when your chief of staff offers to run a board it built. A card comes to Needs you (and your phone) saying where the lead runs, which machines its issue agents may use, how many at once and until when. Approve starts the lead there, and the board's thread reaches it. The board shows the grant under its lead, with Revoke: revoking leaves what's running alone, and nothing more starts under it. Going past the grant takes a new card.
