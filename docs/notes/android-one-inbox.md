The bell's number is the Inbox's count as the server says it, across your projects, and it moves as soon as the Inbox changes: the app no longer adds waiting deploys or thread news of its own. The sheet lists what needs you or names you in three groups (Decide, Names you, Chat), without the "Ready, merged or closed" group. An agent's question or permission on one of your machines is answered under its row, an agent's card with its options, and a to-do with Done or Can't and an optional note; Dismiss puts any item away. Asks that wait on a teammate fold under "2 asks for Jo ›" and aren't counted for you. The Overview's Needs you now lists the project's deploys waiting for your approval, as the iPhone's does.
