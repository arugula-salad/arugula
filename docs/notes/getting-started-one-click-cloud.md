---
section: Changed
title: Joining Arugula cloud is one click
line: In Getting started, Connect opens Arugula cloud with your machine's code; click Add there and it's in, with no device to approve it on and no fingerprint to compare.
---
Getting started's Cloud step no longer asks you to approve a code on a device you already use. Connect opens Arugula cloud with the machine's code, signed in as you (or signing you in first), and Add there puts the machine in your account or a team you own or edit. A browser that isn't one of your devices adds it as the signed-in person, and the machine joins on Arugula cloud's word (aru-141), so there's no account fingerprint to check on the machine afterwards. A browser that is one of your devices still signs the join, as before.
