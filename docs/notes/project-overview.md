The Overview in the app is Swarm for one project. Each board with cards in flight leads with its cards in Doing and Review, counting what's next and done, with its progress. Each card is a tile holding the sessions working on it: the agent and its title, where it runs and its last line. A card waiting on you pulses. Cards that need you come first, then failures, quiet ones and working ones, and boards are ordered by their most urgent card. **Not on a card** comes last.

**Needs you** on the right is Swarm's own rail for this project's panes, each card naming its card: allow, deny or answer it there. Click a card to open its drawer, the same one as on the board; the path in the bar and Esc go back up. Click a session to open it on its machine. On a phone, the Overview is one list with Needs you on top. Swarm for every machine stays under Machines, and what the project's page showed before is below the field.
