Machines lists each machine with whether it's online. Yours opens its settings; someone else's says whose it is. Members still lists who's in the team and their role. Inviting people, removing them and changing roles are signed by an owner's device, so a ↗ row there opens your machines page. Sharing a machine with a team works the same way. Labs is a ↗ row to your machines page too, until the app can reach those settings.

The sections are in two groups, the team's own and Every project. General is now called Default project.
