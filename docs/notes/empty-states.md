section: Changed
title: One empty, loading and error look
line: Empty lists, loading and errors look the same everywhere, and an empty one tells you what to do next.
headline: no
---
Every page, pane and block now says "nothing here" the same way: a dashed card with a title, a line on what the thing is, and at most one button. A list that can't be read says "Can't…" with Try again, and a loading line waits a third of a second so a quick load doesn't flash it. On the phone, Here with nothing waiting says "Nothing right now."
