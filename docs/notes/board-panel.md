A board's right column is now the agent panel, under the board's Needs you. ✦ is the project's chief of staff. The board's own tab shows **Lead 🔒**, your private conversation with the board's lead, with Transcript and Terminal (`v`). Its **⇄ channel** switch, or a second click on the tab, turns it into **# the board**, the board's channel, where everyone in the project reads and writes. A thread opens in place, and Esc goes back. When the lead also leads other boards, a line says which.
