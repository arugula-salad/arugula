*Move to…* and *Add a machine* no longer offer a team to its viewers, and a team's page tells a viewer that its owners and editors add machines. When a machine asks to join a team you view, the dialog says viewers can't add machines to it, where it used to say you weren't in the team. A machine a viewer moved in before stays, and its owner can still take it out. Arugula cloud refuses a viewer's move once its next release is out.
