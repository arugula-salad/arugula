Home is now called Overview. You is no longer a tab: tap your picture at the top of any tab for your name, Settings, Send feedback, the app's version and Sign out. The bell beside it opens your Inbox, and New is there too, on every tab. On a tablet, the five places are a rail down the left. Docs says the project's Docs are coming. Settings opens the web's page in your browser. Machines and its terminals are where they were, one tap away.
