A board's lanes read left to right Next, Doing, Review, Done, and the ones that wait on you come first in their lane. A tile is its number, a word for its state ("asks you", "asks Jake", "failing", "stalled 42m"), its title and one line: its PR and where it is, or who works on it, or "no agent yet". There are no more chips or colour fills on tiles, and no "no proof"; the drawer has the details. Done shows its first two and "N more".
