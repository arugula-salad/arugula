title: Team pickers offer only teams that take the machine
line: Adding a machine in one click no longer offers an editor a locked team, and Move to… no longer offers a team you're not in.
headline: no
---
When you add a machine and pick a team for it, the list now holds only the teams
Arugula will put it in. Adding a waiting machine in one click offers the teams you
own, and those you edit unless they're locked. Move to… on a machine offers the
teams you're in, and only those you own while they're locked.
