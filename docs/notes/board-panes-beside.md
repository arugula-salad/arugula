A pane opened on a board (a Doc, a PR, #agents, a run) used to land inside the board's own scroll, under its header and beside its lanes. Now the tab is split: the board is one pane with its header and lanes, and what you or the lead open sits beside it at full height, with its own title row and scroll, as panes do on Machines. The divider drags and the split is kept; Reset puts the board back alone.
