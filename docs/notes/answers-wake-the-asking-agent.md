title: Your answer wakes the agent that asked
line: When you answer an agent's approval card, to-do or board question, it hears your answer, and your note, right away, even if it forgot to watch for it.
needs: control
---
Your answer reaches only the conversation that asked: at its next turn, or as a follow-up that wakes it when it's idle. Your note goes to that agent and nowhere else, so it isn't in an event, a notification or chat. A watch the agent had set on its own card still fires, and the agent hears the answer once.
