title: Back goes up a level in chat
line: Back in chat climbs one level at a time, from a thread to its room to the room list, however you got there.
headline: no
---
Opening a room or a thread adds a history entry, and Back pops it. Moving
sideways (to another room, or another thread) replaces the entry instead, so
ten rooms later it's still one Back to the list. A thread or room opened by a
link gets the list (and the room) put under it.
