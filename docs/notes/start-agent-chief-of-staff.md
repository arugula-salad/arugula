title: Start a chief of staff on any machine
line: An agent can start your chief of staff on one of your machines with start_agent and recipe chief-of-staff; it leads your staff room in the project.
headline: no
---
`start_agent` with `recipe: chief-of-staff` starts a Claude Code agent block titled "Chief of staff" on the machine, in the project, with its standing instructions as its first prompt, and makes it the lead of your staff room there, so your posts in the room are its prompts. It runs the same on a Cloud workspace as on any of your machines, and needs no Labs flag.
