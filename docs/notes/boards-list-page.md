Each board is a row in the list on the left: its title, how far along it is (`13/18`) and a small bar in the lanes' colours. Boards from Plane and Done (folded) are groups of their own, and the search filters the rows.

The board you pick opens in the middle and the list stays. A board's link still opens it, now with its row selected. **New board** sits beside the title. **Not on a board** is the list's first row: its issues fill the middle, and you can drag one onto a board's row. On a phone the list is the page, and a board opens in its place.
