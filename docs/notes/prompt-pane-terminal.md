title: A prompt beside a board shows its terminal
line: When a lead puts an agent's question beside a board or in a tab for you, its terminal is under the question, so you see what it's asking about before you answer.

A prompt pane, an agent's pending permission or question that its lead put beside a board or in a tab for you, now shows the question at the top, answered in place, with the agent's terminal under it. Before, it showed the question alone.
