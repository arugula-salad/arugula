Pick the nights and one window, and the bars under them show when you're quiet as you edit, before you save. *Different times on some days…* adds a row of nights with hours of their own (sleeping in on weekends, say), and *Same times every night* folds them back into one. The list says nights in a row as a range, such as "Mon–Sat, times vary", and the time fields are wide enough for "10:00 PM" now.
