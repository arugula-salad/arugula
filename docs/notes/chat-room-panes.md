title: #agents and a board's channel as panes on a board

needs: control

On a board, **+ pane** lists #agents and the board's own channel. Either opens beside the lanes as a pane holding the whole room: its messages, its threads in place, its tags and the box to write in. `g a` opens #agents there too. In Chat, a board's channel has **Open as a pane** (`o`), which opens the board with the channel beside its lanes. The pane is kept in the board's layout, like the lead's panes.
