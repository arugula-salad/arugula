Machines on Android is now the web's page: each machine with something in
the project you're in, its session cards, and each session's panes as one
line with a Needs you chip. Machines with nothing in the project fold into
one line at the bottom, and an offline machine can't be opened.

On a phone, tapping a session's name opens it a tab at a time, its panes
stacked and live, and a question can be answered right there. Tapping a pane
opens it full screen with the keyboard and the key bar (Esc, Tab, Ctrl and
the arrows), to type in as before.

On a tablet the page sits beside the session's live tab. Before you pick
anything, that side shows the pane you were last in.
