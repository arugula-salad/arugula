---
section: Changed
title: Type into your own machine from your phone
line: Joining Arugula cloud no longer asks about remote typing: your own machine takes your typing from your phone, and a team's machine, joined or moved there, is watch-only until you turn it on.
---
`arugulad sharing remote-input off` (or `on`), run on the machine, still changes it, and `arugulad join --remote-input on|off` picks either way as it joins. A machine whose owner already said keeps what it has.
