line: Dialogs and side sheets look and behave the same everywhere: one backdrop, Escape closes, and focus comes back to where you were.

Every dialog and the board's row sheet sit on the same dimmed backdrop. Escape
closes the one on top and nothing under it, focus stays inside while it is up,
and it returns to what you pressed to open it. A click on the backdrop closes a
side sheet at every width, so on a desktop a click on another board card
closes the sheet first, and a second click opens that card.
