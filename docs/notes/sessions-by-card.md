---
section: Changed
title: Sessions lists every session, by card
line: A project's Sessions page lists every session in the project, on a computer or in a cloud workspace, grouped by the card it works on, and opens a pane live in two clicks.
needs: control
---
Sessions (`/p/<project>/sessions`) used to list only hosted agent sessions. It now lists the sessions on people's computers too, grouped by their card. Each group's heading shows the card, its board and its lane, with **Open card**, which opens the card's drawer on its board. Groups with something waiting on you come first, and **Not on a card** (lead sessions, scratch shells, release terminals) comes last. Finished sessions fold to one line per group. Click a computer's session to see its panes, each with its line: **Open** shows the pane live over the page, and **Open on its machine** goes to the machine's page on that pane. Label, Tracker, Asked by and Going only still narrow the list, and Runs and Workspaces stay at the top. Until Arugula cloud serves the new list, the page shows the hosted sessions as before, grouped by their issues.
