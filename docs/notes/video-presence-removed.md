---
section: Removed
title: Video presence is gone
line: People blocks and live presence (video avatars, voice and calls on arugula.stream) were taken out of Arugula; chat's presence dots stay.
---
The People block (a team's room on arugula.stream, with avatars or outlines from
your camera, waves, voice and calls) and live presence (the `live-presence` Labs
flag: Off, Watching or Live, and live faces on avatars across Arugula) were
removed. A People block left in a layout goes when its machine's daemon
restarts, and a `live-presence` flag left in `flags.json` does nothing. Chat's
presence (active, away, appear offline) is unchanged.
