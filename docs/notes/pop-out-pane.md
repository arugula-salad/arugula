section: Added
title: Pop a pane out
line: Pop out, in a block's pane menu or ⇧⌘O, opens the block alone in a window of its own, for a second monitor; closing the window puts it back in its tab.
---
Chat, a board, a Doc, a PR or any other block can pop out. Its place in the tab says it's in a window of its own, with Bring back, on every screen. In the desktop app the window is a separate window, not a tab of the one it came from. Terminals don't pop out yet: their size is still their tab's.
