Under a PR block (one the mirror has) and at the top of a thread, "Watched by" lists each live watch: whose agent waits, for what (merged, ready, a reply from someone), and until when. It's shown only to members of the project who can read the PR or the room, however public the repo. A session is named only if its owner shares session names with teammates (`agent_sessions`); otherwise it's their agent. Your own watch has Cancel, and its session is told it was cancelled.
