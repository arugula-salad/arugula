title: Star tabs and panes
line: Star tab and Star pane in their menus keep a tab or pane in a Starred group in the Sessions sidebar and the phone's sheet, one click from wherever you are.
---
A starred tab shows a ★ in the tab strip, and a starred pane a ★ on its
header. Starred lists them below Needs you, in the order you starred them,
each with its session, directory and branch; the palette's Go to puts them
first. Stars are the machine's: everyone who sees a session sees its stars,
and an editor can change them. A star goes when its tab or pane closes.
