On a machine's page, when Claude Code in a terminal asks a question or wants to use a tool, its card now sits under the tab's head line, above the panes, instead of over the terminal it's about. It needs no Hide any more. On a phone it stays in the pane. Pane headers no longer show a number; a pane's number and its Ctrl+Shift shortcut are in its kind icon's tooltip.
