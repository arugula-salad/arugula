title: Pin sessions
line: Pin a session from its ⋯ menu or its row's pin button, and it stays in a Pinned group below Needs you in the Sessions sidebar, first on the Panes page and on your phone.
needs: control
---
Pins are yours alone, kept with your account, so a teammate's view of a shared session never changes and your phone shows the same ones. A pinned session that needs you moves up to Needs you, and comes back to Pinned once it's answered. A machine that isn't joined to an account has no pins.
