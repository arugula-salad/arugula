title: Your chief of staff moves when you start it somewhere else
line: Starting your chief of staff on another machine moves it there: the old one stands down and hands over its place in the event log, and each project's has its own name on a machine.
headline: no
---
There's one chief of staff per person per project. Starting one with `start_agent` and `recipe: chief-of-staff` where another already leads your staff room moves it: the new one takes the lead, and the old one stops. One on another machine is told in your staff room to stand down; it posts its place in the event log there and closes its block, and the new one carries on from that place (or starts from now and says so until it arrives). A hosted one is stopped, and the new one starts from now and says so. One on the same machine is closed, and the new one carries on from its notes. A chief of staff's block is now titled "Chief of staff · <project>", so one machine can run one for each of two projects.
