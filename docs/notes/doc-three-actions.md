title: A Doc's three actions
line: Every Doc has Copy link, Share and Open in browser, at the right of its bar and in the Docs page's preview.

---
In a Doc open in a pane, the buttons now end the bar instead of sitting mid-bar, and stay there in a narrow pane. Share reads "Update the shared copy" when the shared copy is older. The Docs page's preview has all three too: Share opens the Doc beside first, then its share dialog.
