Each terminal is drawn at its own size, so nothing resizes the pane on your other screens. A tap brings up the keyboard, with esc, tab, ctrl and the arrows over it, and full screen sizes the pane to the device. An agent block shows what it says as it says it, with a box that types to it, and a session in Arugula cloud shows its last words with a reply box. On an iPad up to four panes stand side by side, with the rest in a strip under them. On an iPhone the panes come one at a time, with a strip to pick between them. A teammate's session can be read, not typed into.
