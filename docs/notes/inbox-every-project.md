---
section: Changed
title: The Inbox lists every project's items, answered in place
line: The Inbox shows what wants you in every project, grouped by project, and you answer each item where it is; the rail's Inbox badge is the one count.
---
The Inbox now lists what wants you in all your projects, under each project's name, with the project you're in first. Asks, permissions, approvals and to-dos come first and answer in place, as on the Overview's Needs you. Reviews, mentions, chat and failed checks on your PRs follow, and each can be dismissed. Press `↵` to go to an item, in whatever project, and `esc` to come back. A teammate's asks fold into one line ("2 asks for Jo ›").

The rail's Inbox badge is the server's count, and each project's mark shows that project's share. The Inbox no longer has an All projects toggle or deploy cards (a deploy's row opens its page).
