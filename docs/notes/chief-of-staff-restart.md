title: Restart your running chief of staff from its tab

headline: no
---
Press **Restart** in the Overview's Chief of staff tab to have your running chief of staff pick up a new recipe. It asks once; the old one's turn stops, and the new one starts on the same machine, takes over your staff room and carries on from its notes there. If it can't start, the tab says why and the old one keeps running. Restart shows only while it runs on one of your machines that can start one.
