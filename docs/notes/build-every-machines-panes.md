title: Build opens on every machine's panes
line: Build opens on one list of your panes from every machine, a section per machine, with the machine as a filter. A pane still opens on its machine's page.
needs: control
---
Build no longer opens on one machine's page with its picker in front. Its Panes is a list of every pane on every machine you reach, grouped by machine, with what needs you first and the pane you had open last marked. *Every machine* above the list narrows it to one. A row opens the pane on its machine's page, and Panes in that page's bar comes back to the list. Swarm's Back goes there too.
