Members has an invite row (a role, Ask me first, Make a link), each member's role and Remove, who waits on an Ask me first link (Let in, Turn down), and the one-click links nobody has used yet, each with Revoke. An invite link still opens on control's page, which signs its person in. Making someone an owner, or removing an owner, takes your passkey, so it still opens the machines page; so does all of Members while your control doesn't serve its teams on the app's address yet.
