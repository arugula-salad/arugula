title: Your chief of staff builds boards
line: Ask your chief of staff on a machine to turn a Doc or a conversation into work, and it makes the board: issues with a done-when, grouped by phase, with blockers and the Doc linked.
headline: no
---
It files each issue with a "Done when" line, puts one group per phase of the Doc in its order, links an issue that blocks another only where the Doc says so, and puts the Doc in the board's Docs strip. Then it posts the board in your staff room and leaves it with no lead: it starts nothing, and you edit the board as you like. It changes a board it made only while nobody leads it, and on a board another lead runs it only adds a row you ask for.
