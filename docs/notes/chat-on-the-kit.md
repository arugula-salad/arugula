Chat no longer draws a box of its own: the rooms and the messages are one section, and a thread or Details is a second section beside it. The bar names the room you're in (Arugula › Chat › #general), and the ⋯ for Mark all as read, sections and unread only sits beside search. Rooms are the same rows as the rest of the app, with unread in bold, and the box you write in glows while you're in it. Your presence (Automatic, Away, Appear offline) moved from the foot of Chat's rooms to your picture's menu.
