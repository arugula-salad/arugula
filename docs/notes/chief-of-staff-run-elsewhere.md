title: Run your chief of staff on another machine
line: When your chief of staff is asleep because its machine is offline, its tab on the Overview offers Run it on another machine.
---
While your chief of staff's machine is offline, nothing hears what you write in its room. Its tab on the Overview now says so and offers **Run it on another machine**, with the same pick of your online machines as Start. It moves there: the one on the offline machine stands down when it's back (a hosted one is stopped), so your room keeps one chief of staff, and the tab's head follows it to the new machine.
