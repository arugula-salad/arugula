A question from an agent (Claude's several questions with their options, checkboxes, Other and previews), a form, or a sign-in link is now a Needs-you row wherever it shows: in an agent block, beside a terminal running Claude Code, in a session's card in chat, on a machine's page and in the Needs-you drawer. The answers are the same as before.

Swarm's rail lists what needs you the same way: one row each, saying what it is and where, with its answers in place (Allow, the question's form, a diff, a gate's Approve, Rerun or Continue for a failure), then Open, Follow, Share and Dismiss. An answered one says who answered it, with its follow-up box.

Cut: the rail cards' colours by kind, their pop-in animation and their uppercase titles (the row's mark and word say what it is), and the cards' tinted frames around a question.
