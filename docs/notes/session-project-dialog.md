title: Project… says who will see the session
line: A session's ⋯ › Project… lists your projects with who sees each one's sessions, and says what moving the session there changes before you click.
---
Project… opens a dialog in place of the text prompt. Each project says who sees
its sessions: its team watches, or just you. The line under the list says who
sees the session now, or, as you point at a project, what moving it there does:
a team's project warns that the team will watch it, including what it printed
before; leaving one says only you will see it. A person you shared the session
with is named, because the share stays. A click moves it, set by hand from then
on, and typing a project's name and pressing Enter still works. No project is
gone from the list, since every session is in one.

In Share session…, a team's "Share with everyone in" shows only on a session in
one of that team's projects, the only place a team share counts. On any other
session it says so and offers "Move to a team project…", which opens Project…
with that team's projects.
