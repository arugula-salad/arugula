---
section: Changed
title: Issues and Docs without chat
line: A project's issues and Docs are open to everyone in the project, whether or not they have chat turned on.
---
Project membership and roles decide who reads and changes a project's
native issues and Docs: owners and editors write, viewers read, and anyone
outside the project gets nothing. Before, someone who had turned chat off
for their own account couldn't open their team's issues or Docs. Now they
can, on the page, in the CLI and through their agents. Their inbox gets
the issues they're assigned and the ones whose description names them.

An issue's comments are still chat, so with chat off they read the issue
without its comments, and the page says so. A machine's Doc copies still
follow the account's own word for them.
