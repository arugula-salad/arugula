The panel under Needs you always starts with your chief of staff: its room, where it runs and whether it hears you now. Moving through Needs you puts each asker in front, as an italic tab with its ×.

An agent's tab is its transcript, with each tool call folded to one line that opens; `v` shows its terminal live instead, and `f` makes the panel full screen (Esc leaves). The box at the foot writes to it, with Silence and Stop beside it. A session that has ended is read only, ending in when it ended; on a to-do, Done carries the conversation on with your note.

A message's replies open in the panel, over its room; back, or Esc, returns to the room where you were. `[` and `]` make the panel narrower or wider, `\` folds it to a strip, and `l` and `h` move between the panel and the work. The panel remembers its width.

Cut: the who-line under the tabs, an agent's rung line with who started it and its parent's last word, an issue agent's thread above its peek, and a peek's Open in Panes and Board thread buttons (the Terminal and the floor replace them).
