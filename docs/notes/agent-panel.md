The panel under Needs you always starts with your chief of staff: its room, where it runs and whether it hears you now. Moving through Needs you puts each asker in front, as an italic tab with its ×.

An agent's tab is its transcript, with each tool call folded to one line that opens, Claude Code's in a terminal too ("Read Crumb.tsx, window.spec.ts", "Edited 3 files"); `v` shows its terminal live instead, and `f` puts the transcript or terminal over the whole page (⤢ or Esc leaves). The box at the foot writes to it, with Silence and Stop beside it; the chief of staff's room and a thread write in the same box, with its glow and a line saying where a message goes. A session that has ended is read only, ending in when it ended; on a to-do, Done carries the conversation on with your note.

A message's replies open in the panel, over its room; Back, or Esc, returns to the room where you were, and Follow is in the ⋯ beside Back. `[` and `]` make the panel narrower or wider, `\` folds it to a strip, and `l` and `h` move between the panel and the work. The panel remembers its width.

Cut: a thread's own head in the panel (its "Thread" line and its ×), the panel's tabs and head on the floor, the who-line under the tabs, an agent's rung line with who started it and its parent's last word, an issue agent's thread above its peek, and a peek's Open in Panes and Board thread buttons (the Terminal and the floor replace them).
