section: Removed
title: The activity drawer, the machine's Agents page and the places bar are gone
line: A machine's page loses its activity drawer, its Agents page and the Panes · Runs · Recipes bar; the rail and Go to… have the places.
---
Three parts of a machine's own page are gone:

- **The activity drawer** under a tab. `arugula history` still reads a session's commands, answers and agent turns.
- **The Agents page** (`#agents`), which held a machine's recipes and the team's catalog. A machine's agents and the recipes it offers stay in its agents block. An old `#agents` link opens the panes.
- **The places bar** over the panes, with Panes, Runs and Recipes. The rail and Go to… have every place. On a phone, Settings keeps its Panes button to go back.

The Machines page's old **Agents only** address (`?agents`) opens Machines.
