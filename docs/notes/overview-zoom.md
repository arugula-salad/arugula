Each level has its own address, so a link, a reload and Back land on it. The talk panel keeps its tabs as you zoom, and brings the one in charge to the front: your chief of staff at the project, the board's lead at a board, the card's agent at its work. A Needs you card's title zooms to the work that's asking. The board and work views are early: their tickets and sessions, with fuller views to come. A board's chevron still opens its cards in place.
