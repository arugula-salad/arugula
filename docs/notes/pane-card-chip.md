title: Every pane names its card
line: A pane in a project's session shows its project and card above it (under its title on a phone), goes to the card on a click, and can be put on a card, moved to another or taken off.
---
The chip reads *Arugula › #983 L2: the platform knows…*, cut to the pane's width:
the title first, then only `#983`. Clicking it opens the card's place on its
board in Arugula cloud, with the card open; its ▾ menu has **Change card** and
**Take off the card**. A session in a project with no card has a dashed chip,
**Put on a card**, which picks one of the project's open issues by number or
title. A session row in the sidebar counts a card its panes share once.
