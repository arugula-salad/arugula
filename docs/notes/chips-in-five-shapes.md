A state keeps its dot, now at 11px on every page. A label is a raised box with square corners, a count is neutral unless it's unread or names you, and a linked thing (an issue, a board, a setting, a file) shows its id, its title and its state in the colour of that state. A merged pull request is purple everywhere. On a phone they're bigger, with a 44px target on anything you can press.
