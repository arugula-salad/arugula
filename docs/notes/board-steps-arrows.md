section: Fixed
title: Steps arrows point into the bar
line: In a board's Steps chart, a link's arrow now drops from its row's bar and turns into the next bar, with its head on the line, instead of a straight line down with the head beside it.
---
Bars fill their step's column, so a link to the next step had only a few pixels to travel sideways. The arrow is now an elbow: down from the bar's bottom, near the end that faces the later bar, then a short level run into that bar, which carries the head. An arrow's colour still follows the link's kind: `before` is accent, `blocks` heavy and yellow.
