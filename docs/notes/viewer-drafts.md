A viewer of a project reads its linked repos' pull requests, issues and
drafts, drafts for themselves, and sends their own drafts with their own
GitHub rights, as before. An agent on one of their machines makes no
drafts on the platform, whatever the machine was granted: the card on a
block says so once, and the block's own drafts stay on the block, where
the person sends them with their own `gh`. A viewer, and a viewer's
machine, discards only the viewer's own drafts. Owners and editors are as
before. A viewer's own comment on an exported issue still goes to GitHub
as them; their agent's stays here.

A cloud workspace names who made it only while that person is in the
project. One a former member made shows no maker, on the page, in the
list and on a chat link's card.
