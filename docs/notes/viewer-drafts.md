A viewer of a project reads its linked repos' pull requests, issues and
drafts, and makes no drafts there: not as themselves, and not through an
agent on one of their machines, whatever the machine was granted. They
discard only their own drafts. Owners and editors are as before.

A cloud workspace names who made it only while that person is in the
project. One a former member made shows no maker.
