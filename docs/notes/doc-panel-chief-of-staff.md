On a Doc your chief of staff wrote, the agent panel used to open the session
that wrote it. If the chief of staff had been restarted since, that tab
stayed empty. Clicking the Chief of staff tab did nothing. Now the panel
opens the Chief of staff tab, which follows whichever session is your
chief of staff now. On a Doc another agent wrote, the panel finds that
agent's session by its conversation's title too, and you can switch
between its tab and the Chief of staff's.

The chief of staff's name no longer shows the project's id. It reads
"Chief of staff" in the panel's tabs and header, in "Chief of staff sees
it" beside a Doc, and on its messages in chat.
