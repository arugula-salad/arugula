title: A delegation's revoke stays
headline: no
line: Revoking a project's delegation ends it for good, even after the owner who revoked it leaves, and a project has one live delegation at a time.
---
Every owner's revoke is kept, and control says the delegation ended to the
browser, the CLI, daemons and hosted boxes, which remember it. Delegating
again while another delegation stands is refused: revoke that one first.
