The × on the note under the bar closes it for you, in this browser; it stays closed. Undo is still there for the week in the project's Settings, under General, beside *Move to project…*, as a list of the moves into and out of it ("1 Doc moved to Rhythm · Nov 15").

The project switcher's *New project from…* asks which of your own projects to take work from, then opens its *Move to project…* page with a new project as where it goes. It shows only if you own a project.
