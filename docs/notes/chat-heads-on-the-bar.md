Activity, Threads, Saved, Search, a thread's panel and the phone's chat list draw their header as the same 34px bar as a room's (44px on the phone). A long name ends in an ellipsis instead of wrapping. A room's header puts its buttons under ⋯ as it narrows, in this order: Go to pane, Members, pins, star, bell. A thread's Follow goes there only when the panel is under 300px.
