title: Swipe between levels in chat on a phone
line: In chat on a phone, swipe right to go up a level (thread, room, room list, projects) and swipe left to go back down into the room or thread you had open.
needs: control
---
The level you swipe away slides off to the right as a card, and the one above
it shows underneath as you drag. Let go past a third of the width, or flick, and
it commits; short of that it springs back. A swipe goes where Back goes, so
history stays right. Swiping left from the list or a room returns to the last
room or thread you had open there. Vertical scrolling, long-press, the
composer's text selection and sideways-scrolling code blocks are unchanged.
