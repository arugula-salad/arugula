title: A machine's sessions are on the Machines page
line: The Arugula mark, the session's name in the header and the palette's Go to <machine> on Machines open the Machines page at that machine, or that session's card.
headline: no
---
The "Sessions on <machine>" view that lay over a machine's panes is gone:
the Machines page has every machine's sessions, with their tabs, panes and
what needs you. Old links to it open the Machines page instead. A machine
on its own, with no Machines page, keeps its sidebar: the mark moves to
its search, and the session's name opens the session's menu.
