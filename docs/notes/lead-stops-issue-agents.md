title: A board's lead stops issue agents on your other machines
line: A board's lead stops an issue agent it started on another of your machines with close and to, and the board's grant has the slot back at once.
headline: no
---
Under a board's grant, a lead already started issue agents on your machines. Now it can stop one wherever it runs, and the next start fits right away, without waiting for the machine's next report. Only an agent a board's start opened can be stopped this way, and only by the machine that started it or the board's lead. Revoking the grant still refuses the next start.
