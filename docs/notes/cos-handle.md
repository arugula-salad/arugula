title: "@cos names your chief of staff"
line: Type @cos to reach your chief of staff in a project's chat, or @machine/cos for that machine owner's. It follows a Restart.
headline: no
---
Your chief of staff's block is named after its project, so its handle was long and changed on Restart. `@cos` in a project's rooms now reaches your own chief of staff, and `@machine/cos` reaches the chief of staff of that machine's owner: whichever session leads their staff room at the time. In the `@` list it's shown as Chief of staff under its machine.
