On the Boards page, the board keeps the middle to itself. Its controls are in a column at the right under small headings: **View** (Lanes or Steps), **Find** (the search, with the Label and Person menus, and a **Group** menu on a board with groups), **Docs**, **Add** (Add row, Add a group, New issue) and **Board** (Workers and ⋯).

A row's details open in that column, in place of the controls, so the board stays in sight beside them. **‹ Board controls** or Esc goes back. On a narrower screen the column moves under the board and a row's details open over the page, as before. A board in a workspace pane keeps its one column.
