A project's Chat has an **Agents** section under Direct messages, which folds like the others. It lists:

- your **chief of staff** (no longer among Direct messages);
- **#agents**, for work across boards such as deploys, merge order and holds, once the project has it;
- a **#** channel for each open board, with a dot when the board has a lead. Closed boards fold under **archived**.

Issue comments aren't listed. Beside #agents or a board's channel, a column says who's in it and who a post reaches. A board channel's column also has Open the board. A thread there opens in the channel's own column, and `esc` or "‹ #channel" goes back. In Chat, `j` and `k` move through the Agents rows and `g a` opens #agents.
