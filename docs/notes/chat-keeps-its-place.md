---
section: Fixed
title: Chat keeps its place
line: A room or thread opens where you left it, and the view no longer jumps when the keyboard opens, you send, or pictures and older messages load.
needs: control
---
Leave a room scrolled back in the history and come back, on the same device: it opens at the same message, at the same place. A room left at the bottom opens at the bottom, and a room with unread messages still opens at the bottom with its New line. Threads do the same.

The view also holds still while the phone's keyboard opens and closes, when you send a message, and when link previews, pictures or older history load in above or below: at the bottom it stays at the bottom, and further up it keeps the message you were reading where it was.
