title: Machines show the project's sessions
line: Under Build, Machines, Panes, Swarm and a machine's sessions show only the current project's, and switching project keeps you on the machine, on your last session there.

In the app, a machine's page is in a project, and everything under Build follows it:
Machines lists your machines, the project's cloud workspaces and any other machine
with a session of it you can see, and counts only its sessions; Panes and Swarm show
only its panes; a machine's sidebar, tabs and panes show only its sessions. A session
of another project isn't there at all. A machine with none of the project's says so,
with New session on your own. Switching project from a machine's page keeps you on
that machine, on the session you last had there in that project, and a link to a
pane in another of your projects switches to it first. The machine's own page still
shows every session.
