In a project's Settings, under General, *Move to project…* opens a page: pick where it goes (a new project, with its name and owner, or one you can write), then what moves. A repo brings its boards, issues and sessions; a board brings its repo; a Doc on a moving board comes along. Before you move says only what you have to deal with (who will now see it, a Doc on a machine, an environment's secrets), and Comes along counts the rest, with each issue's new number. Links to the old numbers keep working. If something changes in the project while you're deciding, nothing moves and the page shows the new plan.

A machine's own Docs move from that machine: in the app, on your machine that's online, you can pick them and it moves them into a team's project once the rest has; anywhere else the page says where they move from, with a link there.
