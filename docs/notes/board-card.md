An issue that's on a board is that board's card: beside it, its board, the worker on it and where it runs, the board's lead, the branch and the PR; above Activity, its open ask, answered right there; and in Activity, a line for each answer ("aaron answered Squash"). The path climbs Boards, the board, then the card. Labels and assignees are clicked to edit; their Edit buttons are gone.
