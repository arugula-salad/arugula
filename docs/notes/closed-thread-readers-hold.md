title: Closed threads keep the right readers
line: Someone taken off a closed pane's thread stays off it, and a team's lock or a machine catching up on its team no longer changes who reads it for good.
headline: no
---
A closed pane's or session's thread keeps the people who could read it when
it closed, less anyone who loses access. Losing access now counts the moment
it happens, even while chat is off, so a share taken away and given again
doesn't bring the thread back. A team's lock keeps its members out only while
it lasts. A pane that closes before the machine knows its team, or while its
enrollment can't be read, gets the team's members and owners once it does,
and nobody else.
