The Inbox is grouped as on the web: deploys waiting for your approval first, then reviews requested, changes requested, checks failed, runs failed, mentions, assigned to you, new in threads, and ready, merged or closed. Opening something marks it seen, and Dismiss clears it. A native issue or a thread opens in the app; a PR opens on GitHub. On iPhone and iPad, tapping a notification about something in your Inbox opens it and marks it seen. On Android, `arugula://inbox/<item>` does the same, and `arugula://overview`, `boards`, `docs`, `machines` and `chat` open those places. On an iPad, when the sidebar is folded away (as in portrait), the bell and your picture go back to the top of each place. Phones keep them at the top, as before. Machines and its terminals are where they were.
