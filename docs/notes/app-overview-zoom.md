title: The iPhone and iPad app zooms from Home into a board
line: In the iPhone and iPad app, a board's row on Home zooms to that board, and a ticket on it zooms to its work. The path bar or Back zooms out.
headline: no
---
Each level has an address that the app opens: `arugula://p/PROJECT`, `/board/BOARD`, `/board/BOARD/card/ROW` and `/work/SESSION`. These are the web's own paths, so a link from a Doc opens the same level in the app. A notification about a pane opens its card's work when the pane is on a card, and one about an issue on a board opens that card. The board's thread and its lanes are buttons on the board's level. The board and work levels are early: their tickets and sessions now, with fuller views to come.
