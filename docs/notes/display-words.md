Some words on screen meant two different things. They now mean one each:

- The agents strip in a thread said "Idle" for a hosted session that had
  failed or been stopped. It now says Failed or Stopped. Idle still means
  between turns, ready for the next prompt.
- A session card said "done" when a turn ended, though the session takes
  another prompt. It now says "turn ended".
- With no rooms yet, a personal chat said "No threads yet" beside the
  Threads list of replies. It now says "No rooms yet".
- A draft's button said Drop in a pane and Discard on the platform. It says
  Discard in both, and so do the draft's card and a tracker write's.
- The MCP tools speak of a board's lanes, not its zones, and of a Cloud
  workspace's id, not a hosted box's.

A board write takes `group` where it took `zone`, and `zone` still works.
