---
section: Changed
title: Approve or expire a gate from Needs you
line: A pane waiting at a gate can be approved or expired from its Needs you row on a computer, as on the phone.
---
A pane waiting at a workspace gate can now be answered from its row in Needs you, on the Overview or in the Inbox, without opening its workspace block: **Approve**, or **Expire** to turn a chant gate down so the next run stops there again. A decision point offers its choices there too. This used to be possible only on the phone's sheet and in the workspace block.
