Where the work left, a note under the bar says what moved and where to ("25 issues, 1 board and 2 Docs moved to Rhythm."); where it arrived, where it came from. Undo puts it all back, and the note then says where it went. Only the latest move shows. An issue, board or Doc opened from its old project's address lands on its page in the new one.
