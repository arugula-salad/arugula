section: Fixed
title: Boards and issues in a pane show in the app
line: A Board, native issue or team chat block in a machine's pane on app.arugula.io reads as you, and its links, like the pane's card chip, open the card or page right there in the app.
headline: no
---
Inside the app, the shell's blocks didn't know who was signed in, so a
Board block said "A board is on Arugula cloud… Open this machine in Arugula
cloud, signed in, to see it here" although you were. They now read as the
person signed in to the app, through the app's own address. A pane's
dashed *Put on a card* chip shows only where someone is signed in to put
it on one.

Links from a machine's pane to Arugula cloud's own pages (a board row's
issue, the card chip's card, a native issue's page, What's new's *Try it*)
now go to that page in the app, in place, so Back returns to the pane,
instead of opening another tab. On control's page they open as before.
