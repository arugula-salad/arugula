A machine joined to your account alone copies its Docs to your Docs on the platform, where they read with the machine asleep, only once you turn **Your Docs on the platform** on, under Teams on your machines page. It's your account's own setting: a team can't turn it on, and it doesn't turn chat on. If your account had chat on for itself, it's on already. Off stops new and changed Docs from being copied; copies already there stay until you delete them.

A team's machine no longer copies its Docs: before, it copied every Doc on it into the personal Docs of the member who added it. The platform now refuses its copies whatever version it runs, and the Docs stay on the machine. Copies it made before stay where they are, and nothing removes them; you can delete one on the platform, and deleting the Doc on the machine takes its copy down.

A machine joined to your account alone also follows your teams' chat for the threads on its panes now, so their readers reach the platform.
