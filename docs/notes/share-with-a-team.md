A Doc a machine made already has a read-only copy on the platform, in your
own project. Share with…, on that copy's page and in the machine's share
dialog, picks one of your teams' projects and makes a new Doc there from the
copy's title, sections and pictures as they are now. The team owns it and
edits it like any Doc; the Doc on your machine stays as it is, and is still the
truth for itself. The team's Doc says which Doc it was made from.

Nothing is live. When the Doc on your machine changes, Update copy (beside the
project's row) replaces the team's Doc with the copy again, and asks first if the
team edited it since. Only you can share your copies, and only into a project
where you can make Docs.
