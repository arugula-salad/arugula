title: A Doc's copy follows the Doc
line: Deleting a Doc on your machine takes its copy on the platform down too, and a copy on the platform can be deleted there.
try: {"route": "/docs"}
needs: control
---
When a machine has copied a Doc up to your Docs on the platform, deleting the Doc on the machine now takes the copy down too, even with copying turned off, instead of leaving it there and making a second copy at the next change. Links to that copy, a board's included, then read as a deleted Doc. A copy can also be deleted on the platform by anyone who writes in its project, from the Doc's page; the machine's next change to the Doc brings a new copy, with a new link.
