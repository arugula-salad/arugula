On a machine's page, each tab now starts with its state: working, asks you, done or quiet. The line over the panes names the tab, the machine it runs on and what it is (a shell, an agent, a server), before the pane count. The layouts are **Tamed** (the active pane large, the rest beside it; it was "Main + stack"), **Grid** and **Columns**. Renaming a tab (double-click or F2), dragging tabs and panes, and the tab's menu work as before.
