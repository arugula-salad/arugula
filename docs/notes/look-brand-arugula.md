line: The app is a near-black with a green accent in dark, and a grey paper with an olive green in light, in Helvetica Neue and SF Mono. Settings › Appearance has five presets.

Pick a preset next to the theme: Arugula (the default), Moss, Sea glass, Stone & ink or Graphite & acid. Each sets the accent and the tint, and where the warmth and depth sliders start; the sliders tune it from there. Amber still means something asks you and red still means failing, whatever the preset.

Ids, counts, times and labels are now set in mono. The app no longer downloads its own fonts: it uses Helvetica Neue and SF Mono, or the closest your system has. If you'd moved the sliders, they start again at Arugula's; your theme stays.

Cut, and can come back if people miss them: Slate's and Latte's colors for the app itself (Docs, terminals and editors keep them for now), and the bundled Instrument Sans, Big Shoulders and JetBrains Mono fonts.
