The top bar now says how many processes are running across your machines. Clicking it opens **Running**, a drawer at the right: the project's red branches first ("main has been red for 3 hours"), then each machine's live agents, servers and shells, then the project's CI runs. Up and Down move through it, and a process's last lines show under the list. On a board, **Pin to desk** puts the process beside the board. Esc closes it.
