---
section: Changed
title: Click a Doc to read it beside the list
line: On the Docs pages, clicking a row shows the Doc beside the list; moving the mouse over the rows only highlights them.
---
A Doc's preview reads and draws the whole Doc, so resting the pointer on a row
no longer swaps it in. Click a row (or tap it) to show it; ↑↓ and j/k still
move the selection, and Enter or a double click still opens the Doc. Other
lists keep previewing on hover.
