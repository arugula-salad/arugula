title: An issue's comments open their reply threads
line: A comment answered in its own thread shows its replies link on the issue's page, and opens the Thread panel, with its agent strip, as a channel message does.
headline: no
---
On an issue's or a run's page, a comment with replies in its thread now shows "N replies" and opens the same Thread panel a channel does: beside the page on a computer, the whole screen on a phone, with its address (`?thread=`) so a link lands on it. A mention's `answered ›` chip opens the thread at the reply it names, wherever it shows.
