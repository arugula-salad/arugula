section: Changed
title: An idle agent wakes once for what comes together
line: Messages and watch alerts that reach an idle agent block at about the same time are now one prompt, so a chief of staff back online wakes once, not once per message.
headline: no
---
An agent block that isn't in a turn waits about a second after a chat message or a watch's alert reaches it, for others coming too, then takes everything that came as one prompt. When your machine comes back from being offline or asleep, control sends everything it kept for it at once, so your chief of staff wakes once for all of it and catches up from where it left off. A block in the middle of a turn queues what comes for it as before.
