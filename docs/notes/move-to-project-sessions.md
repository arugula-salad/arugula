A session a picked repo placed still comes with it, ticked and locked, even from a machine that's offline (it moves when the machine is back). Any other session on your own online machines can be ticked by itself: it moves to the new project as set by hand, so its repo doesn't move it back. Sessions on someone else's machine, or on an offline one, are listed without a box, saying why. Before you move and Comes along say who will see the sessions once they've moved.
