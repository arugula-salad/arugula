Bar buttons are 24px, page buttons 32 and touch buttons 40. Open and Closed,
the phone's tabs and the swarm's pickers are one segmented control, and a
toggle that is on is the accent tint with accent text wherever it is: the pane
toolbar, chat, the issues filters and the phone's keys. Boards' dashed "+ Add"
buttons are plain small buttons with a plus.
