title: The Overview tidies itself and suggests asks
line: Each morning the Overview closes what your chief of staff opened yesterday; while it's empty, example asks fill your chief of staff's message box.
needs: control
---
The first time you open the Overview on a new day, it sends what your chief of staff opened before today, and you haven't touched, to the closed list. That covers its panes beside Boards and its tabs that no longer wait on you. A tab still waiting on you stays, such as an agent's permission prompt or a review you haven't answered. "↺ N closed overnight" at the end of the tab strip brings any of them back.

While nothing is open beside Boards and Running, the space below them shows what your chief of staff can put there, with a few asks to start from: "Show me what merged today", "Put the failing runs beside Boards", "Open the Docs that wait on me" and "Keep a standup Doc here each morning". Each one fills your chief of staff's message box for you to edit and send.
