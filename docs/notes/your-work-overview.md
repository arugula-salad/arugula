Signing in no longer empties the screen: where a new personal project said "Nothing in flight.", its Overview now shows the same Your work list as your machine's page, read from each of your machines directly. Continue here (or Move here) opens the conversation in a terminal pane on its machine. A conversation already open in a pane shows in *Not on a card* instead. A teammate's machines never show, and a team's projects don't list conversations.
