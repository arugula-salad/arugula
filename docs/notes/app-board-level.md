title: The board's own view in the iPhone and iPad app
line: In the iPhone and iPad app, a board zoomed to from Home shows its lead's card, with Talk to the lead and Board thread, and its open tickets with the agents on each.
headline: no
---
The tickets are grouped the way the board groups them: Doing, then Review, then Next. Each one shows its PR or checks and a word for how it stands: working, in review, next or asks you. The agents on it show as names, and a name opens that agent. A ticket opens its work, and the done tickets fold into one line. A board with no lead says so. In a team's project, it offers to ask your chief of staff to start one. All lanes opens the board's full lanes.
