section: Added
title: Rename a Doc
line: Owners and editors rename a Doc from its menu or by clicking its title, and an agent renames one with doc rename.
---
A Doc's menu, on its page and on its row in the Docs list, has Rename. On the
Doc's page you can also click the title to edit it in place: Enter saves,
Escape cancels. A title is one line of at most 200 characters. The list, the
page, the tab's title and any open Doc block show the new one.

An agent renames a Doc with `doc` kind `rename`, given the Doc's block or its
id, for a team's Doc and for one kept on this machine. Its sections stay as
they are.
