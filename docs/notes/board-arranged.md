section: Changed
title: The lead arranges the board
line: A board's lead can open Docs, PRs, runs and prompts beside the board or in tabs. It never takes your focus, and Reset puts the board back.
needs: control
---
A board's groups are now its panes. You and the board's lead can open Docs, PRs, runs, previews and an agent's pending prompt beside them. The groups compact while something is open, and the lead's conversation narrows to a quarter of the width. At 1440 pixels, a third pane stacks under the second instead of squeezing the board.

The lead never takes your focus or switches your tab. If you were typing, scrolling or clicking in the last 4 seconds, its pane arrives quietly with a dot. Something that waits on you, such as a review or a permission prompt, opens as a tab of its own, and Needs you gets a row that opens it.

The lead keeps at most three of its panes beside the board; a fourth closes the oldest one you haven't touched. A pane you open, move, resize or maximise is yours. A group you close stays closed until you Reset. While the layout differs from the default, the header says who changed it and when, with Reset beside it.

Keys: `1`–`6` switch tabs, `[` and `]` move between panes, `w` closes a pane, `W` closes a tab, `f` maximises a pane and Esc comes back. Filter is now `/` only.

Cuts:
- Linked Docs and PRs are no longer fixed tabs. Open them from **+ pane**.
- A preview opens as a link, not inline.
- You can't drag a pane onto the tab strip. Use the pane's move button.
