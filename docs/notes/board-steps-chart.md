---
section: Changed
title: Steps is a chart
line: A board's Steps view now draws a chart, one line per row with a bar in its step, and you can drag the title column wider or narrower.
---
The Steps view shows each row as one line: its title on the left and a bar in the column of the step it waits for, with done rows in ✓. Hover a line, or tap it on a phone, to see the arrows of its links. Drag the title column's right edge to change its width, or focus it and use the arrow keys; double-click puts it back. Each device remembers its width.
