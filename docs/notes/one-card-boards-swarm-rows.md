A board on the Boards page, the cards of the swarm's lists (projects, issues,
runs, sessions, the inbox, settings) and the rows of a board are one card.
Hovering lifts its edge instead of glowing, a state's tint is the same in every
place, and on Latte a board's rows take the swarm's lighter tint, so a warn row
no longer reads as a brownish grey.
