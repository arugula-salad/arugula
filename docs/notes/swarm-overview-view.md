Swarm opens from the Overview's views in the top bar (Overview · Swarm) and now sits under the bar, beside the rail, like any other page. The Overview stays lit on the rail and Swarm is lit in the bar. Escape, or the bar's Overview, goes back to the Overview.

When you open a card's pane, it opens on its machine's page, and the browser's Back brings you back to the Swarm. `/machines/swarm` links, with or without a pane or a board, still open the Swarm.

Swarm is for computers only. On a phone the Overview has no views, and a Swarm link opens the Overview.
