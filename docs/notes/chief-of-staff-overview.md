Your chief of staff now arranges your Overview. While you talk with it, it opens the Doc or run you're discussing beside Boards and Running. Something that waits on you gets a tab of its own with a Needs you row pointing at it: a Doc ready for review, a PR to approve, or an agent that's blocked on a permission prompt while you're away. You answer the prompt in that tab, and the agent carries on. When main goes red it can show the failing run in place of Boards until it's green.

It never takes your focus or switches your tab. It leaves your own panes alone and doesn't reopen what you closed.
