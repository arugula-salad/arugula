title: Start your chief of staff from the Overview
line: With no chief of staff yet, the Overview's Chief of staff tab says what it does and starts one on a machine of yours you pick.
---
The Chief of staff tab is there before you have one. It says what the chief of staff does, and **Start** runs it on one of your machines that's online and on this release or newer: the machine you're on by default, or one you pick. A Cloud workspace of the project is one more choice, never the default. If the machine can't read your direct messages yet, the tab asks you to allow it first. Once it starts, the tab is its room, and its head says where it runs and whether it's awake.
