During the beta, cloud workspaces are for accounts let in from the waitlist:
making one without an admission puts you on the waitlist and says so. Each
account may have 2 cloud workspaces at a time, whose boxes may run 60 hours a
month (UTC); past that a new one, a rebuild or a wake says what to do and when
the hours start again. Relayed traffic slows past 10 GB a day.

Operators of a control can pause sign-ups, turn new hosted boxes off, change
the caps for everyone or one account, and suspend an account, all without a
restart: `arugula-control switches` (docs/control-ops.md, "Launch switches").
