section: Changed
title: Remote typing off still lets you watch
line: "With remote typing off on a machine, its owner can still watch every session on it from their other devices, read-only, instead of seeing nothing."
headline: no
---
`arugulad sharing remote-input off` (or answering no when a machine joins) stops everyone typing into the machine through Arugula cloud, its owner included. Its owner now keeps a read-only view of every session on it from other devices: panes and agents show, but typing, answering an agent and sending a follow-up are refused. Other people still see only what's shared with them, or their team's.
