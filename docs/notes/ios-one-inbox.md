The bell's number is the server's count, the same as on the web. It no longer adds deploys waiting or threads with news, and it updates as things change instead of every minute. The app icon's badge shows the same count. The Inbox groups what needs you as Decide, Names you and Chat. A teammate's asks fold under one row, such as "2 asks for Jake ›". "Ready, merged or closed" is gone. Anything the app doesn't recognise still shows as a plain row. You can answer in the row itself:
- An agent's card with its own buttons, such as Start or Not now.
- A to-do with Done or Can't, and an optional note.
- A board's ask with its options or your own words.
- An agent's question or permission prompt, as Needs you answers it.

Dismiss still clears an item. A deploy waiting for you opens its approval. When an item clears, its notification goes away. `arugula://inbox/<item>` opens the Inbox at that item and marks it seen, as on Android.
