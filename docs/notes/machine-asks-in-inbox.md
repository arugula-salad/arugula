section: Added
title: Your machines' agents ask in your Inbox
line: When an agent on one of your machines asks a question, wants a permission, fails or finishes its turn, it's in your Inbox and its count, unless the agent that started it answers first.
needs: control
---
Your machine tells control which of its panes wait on someone: what kind of ask it is, since when, whether only a person may answer, and the agent block that started it. It never sends the question itself. The app still reads that from the machine. An ask from an agent that another agent started goes to that agent first, and reaches you after ten minutes without an answer, or at once when only a person may give it (a gate, an invite, a Doc's share). A finished turn shows in your Inbox but isn't counted.
