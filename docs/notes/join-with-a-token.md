title: Join a machine with a join token
line: "`arugulad join --token ajt_…` (or ARUGULA_JOIN_TOKEN) adds a machine at once, with no browser, and Getting started finishes as soon as you click Add in Arugula cloud."
headline: no
---
A machine joined with a join token, or added with one click by a signed-in person, is let in on control's word (aru-141): it pins no account root, so devices don't reach it by their certificates. Channels with control's machine tokens and read-only links do. A machine joined the way it always has keeps working as before. A daemon older than this one won't load a join made this way, and runs as not joined until it's updated.
