**Not on a board** is now the first row of a project's Boards list, with how many open issues are on no board, and it stays there whatever you search for. Pick it and its issues fill the middle. Order and the labels, each with its count, are in the column on the right, and a label filters the issues.

Drag an issue, or a selection of them, onto a board's row in the list, or press ＋ to pick a board. The Not on a board link from every project's Boards selects this row. On a phone it's the first row too, and its issues open in the list's place.
