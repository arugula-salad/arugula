Up to four panes sit side by side, and the rest wait as tabs. The pane whose agent you're talking to is outlined, and each pane has Talk and Open in Panes. Terminals are read from their machines through your own connection, not through Arugula cloud. A teammate's session is shown read-only. A cloud workspace's session shows its box's terminal while the box runs, or its last answer and a reply box while the box is paused. A card no agent works on says so and offers to ask its board's lead.
