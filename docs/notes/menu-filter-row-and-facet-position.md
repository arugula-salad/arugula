title: A long menu has a filter row
line: A long menu has a filter row, and a board's filter menus open under their buttons.
headline: no
---
A menu of 13 rows or more starts with a filter: type to narrow the rows, use the arrow keys and Enter over what is left, and Escape clears the text before it closes the menu. On a phone the filter doesn't take the focus, so the keyboard stays down. A board's Label and Person menus now hang under their buttons, and right-aligned to them near the window's edge.
