Each ask on Overview's Needs you is two lines: a mark for its kind and the question, then whose it is and where it runs. It opens on a click, or on hover where your device has one, to the full question, its details and its buttons; Enter and Space open it from the keyboard and Escape closes it. Approve, Allow and Answer are only there while the row is open, so a stray tap can't approve a command.

The asks are grouped by owner: yours first, then a group for each other person under their name and how many asks they have (the one with more first), and a team's box or a hosted session under the person who started it, else the team's name. When every ask is yours there's no heading. A teammate's ask you only watch still says an editor answers it, and has no button to answer it.

The Swarm rail's cards are as they were.
