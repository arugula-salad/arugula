title: A fired watch wakes its session once
line: A machine back after days away shows each fired watch once, and a watch dropped before its session took it is sent again.
headline: no
---
A watch's message is remembered by its watch for up to a week, across restarts, so one sent again reaches its session once. One dropped from a pane's queue (the session stopped or exited before taking it) is sent again.
