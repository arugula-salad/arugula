The rail's **Boards** now uses the same list as a project's Boards page. Each project with boards gets a heading, with how many of its issues are on no board, and under it its boards as rows: the title, how far along it is (`13/18`) and the lanes' bar. Every project's Done boards are folded together at the foot, each naming its project. The search filters across all of them.

The board you pick opens in the middle and the list stays. Above it are links to the board on its project's Boards and to that project's issues on no board. A board's address here selects its row, and older links to `/boards` still work. On a phone the list is the page, and a board opens in its place.
