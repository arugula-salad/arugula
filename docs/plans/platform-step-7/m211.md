M211: the standing project grant

Part of #TRACKER. Needs M207. Builds on S37's standing grant (q3) and M88's format.

## What

- **An owner's device signs one grant per project:** "start and instruct on this project's workspaces".
  - Offered when the project gets its first hosted workspace.
  - Can be signed again from the phone.
- **The platform stores it, but can't forge or widen it.** Hosted `arugulad` verifies it against the project's delegation (step 5's M178).
- **Revoking it** stops new runs. Running ones finish, or are cancelled if the owner says so.
- **CLI and page:** see it, sign it, revoke it.

**Done when:** a run starts with every owner device offline, and is refused after a revoke, on testnet.
