M223: stuck leases, crashes mid-dispatch, and the runbook

Part of #TRACKER. Needs M212 and M219.

## What

Tests:
- **A worker dies holding a lease.** Another one takes the job when the lease runs out, and nothing runs twice.
- **The platform crashes between "approved" and "dispatched".** The deploy dispatches once.
- **The daemon crashes mid-run.** The ledger stops it rerunning, and the run is marked failed.
- **A driver fails mid-apply.** The deploy is marked failed, and Jake is paged.

Runbook:
- **Add a runs and deploys section to `docs/control-ops.md`:** what Jake does when each of these happens.

**Done when:** these run in CI, and the runbook is merged.
