M213: durable run logs and artifacts

Part of #TRACKER. Needs M212.

## What

- **The daemon uploads the run's log** every few MB and at the end, through **signed uploads** to an object bucket. The platform signs each URL for one run's prefix.
- **Artifacts** go the same way, with their digest recorded in `run_artifacts`.
- **Hosted workspaces only.** The daemon refuses to upload from a personal machine.
- **30-day retention,** set on the bucket. Usage is shown to Jake as operator.
- **The bucket:** propose Tigris on Fly, next to the platform. Ask Jake before creating it.
- **Update `tables.toml`, control-e2e.md and the privacy page** to say the platform reads these logs.

**Done when:** a run's full log and an artifact can be read on the platform page with every device asleep, and are gone after the retention period (tested with a short TTL).
