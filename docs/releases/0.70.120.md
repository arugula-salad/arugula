## Added

- **Run this board and Revoke on iPhone and Android** (#2883). In a team's project, a board no one leads and nothing is granted offers **Run this board** on the iPhone's Board level, in place of "Ask chief of staff to start one", and at the head of the board's thread on Android. Its card goes in your staff room, as it does from the web. A board with a grant says where its lead runs, which machines its issue agents may use, how many at once and until when; the person who gave it can press **Revoke** there.
- **Run your chief of staff on another machine** (#2887). While your chief of staff's machine is offline, nothing hears what you write in its room. Its tab on the Overview now says so and offers **Run it on another machine**, with the same pick of your online machines as Start. It moves there: the one on the offline machine stands down when it's back (a hosted one is stopped), so your room keeps one chief of staff, and the tab's head follows it to the new machine.

## Fixed

- **A board's lead hears its wider grant** (#2886). The chief of staff used to answer in your staff room, which only it reads, so
a lead whose wider grant you had approved went on waiting until someone posted
on its board.
- **A lead closes its issue agents on its own machine** (#2888). A lead's `close {pane}` closes a block that a start for its board opened on the same machine, and tells Arugula cloud at once so the board's grant has the slot back. Before, only `close {pane, to}` on another machine worked, and a person had to close the agents here by hand. Any other pane in another tab is still refused, and so is an issue agent closing its sibling.
