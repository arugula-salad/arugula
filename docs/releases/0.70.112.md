## Added

- **Start your chief of staff from the Overview** (#2873). The Chief of staff tab is there before you have one. It says what the chief of staff does, and **Start** runs it on one of your machines that's online and on this release or newer: the machine you're on by default, or one you pick. A Cloud workspace of the project is one more choice, never the default. If the machine can't read your direct messages yet, the tab asks you to allow it first. Once it starts, the tab is its room, and its head says where it runs and whether it's awake.
- **Approve your chief of staff's asks on any machine** (#2872). Ask the chief of staff on one of your machines to get an agent on an issue, or to close some agents, and it puts a card with Approve and Deny in your staff room, under Needs you and on your phone, as a hosted one does. Approve starts the agent on that machine, in a new worktree of the repo the chief of staff works in, attached to the issue's row, or closes the agent blocks. Only you can answer it, and nothing said in chat does. Your answer wakes the chief of staff.
