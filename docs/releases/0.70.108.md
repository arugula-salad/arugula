## Added

- **An issue's thread has a lead** (#2861). `start_agent` with `issue` makes the new agent the lead of that issue's thread; another agent started on it takes the lead over, and `post_thread` with `lead: true` (or `remove: true`) takes or lets go of it, as in a staff room. A post there is told to the lead, never the lead's own. `read_thread` names the lead, and the issue's page shows it above the comments.
- **Start a chief of staff on any machine** (#2863). `start_agent` with `recipe: chief-of-staff` starts a Claude Code agent block titled "Chief of staff" on the machine, in the project, with its standing instructions as its first prompt, and makes it the lead of your staff room there, so your posts in the room are its prompts. It runs the same on a Cloud workspace as on any of your machines, and needs no Labs flag.

## Changed

- **A finished turn's notification is quieter** (#2864). A finished command's notification is quiet too, and keeps the title "Done". Questions, approvals and failures still notify as before, and a pane you have on screen isn't pushed at all.
