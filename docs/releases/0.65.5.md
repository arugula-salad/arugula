## Fixed

- **Your call or voice video keeps its camera** (#1822). arugula.stream has one camera for your character and your video, and stopping it ends the video. Live presence's store stopped it in every room but Live's, and in all of them at Off, Watching or with the window hidden. So in a People block call or voice, your video went off when you switched tabs, changed the switch or the team you're in, or another room joined, and the block's own camera button was undone. Now the room you're on video in keeps its camera, Live's goes off elsewhere while you are (one camera) and comes back after, and the store only stops a camera it started.
- **What's new opens on a daemon's own page again** (#1824). The panel took the newest three releases, then left out what you can't use. 0.65.2–0.65.4 are all team things, so on a page not signed in to control nothing was left and it never opened. It now shows the newest three releases with something for you.
