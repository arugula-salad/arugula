# Arugula 0.66.3

One rail everywhere: the same places down the left on your machines' pages and on the platform page. Boards look like the rest of the platform, and Deploys rows open the right deploy in Safari and the app.

## Changed

- **One rail everywhere** (#1871: #376, #377, #378). The rail down the left is the same on your machines' pages and on the platform page, with the place you're on lit. Projects is at its top. On the platform page, Panes, Swarm and Docs take you back to your machine's page, and the bar keeps the project's own places. A caret beside the machine menu shows and hides the sessions sidebar, replacing the rail's Sessions button. On a phone nothing moves.
- **Boards pages as cards** (#1858). A board is a card with its goal or done note, and each list has stats, like Issues and Docs.

## Fixed

- **Deploys rows in WebKit** (#1857). In the desktop app and Safari, any click on a project's Deploys page opened the last row's deploy. Each row now opens its own, and a deploy's page goes back to Deploys.
- **One click to your browser** (#381). In the desktop app, Add passkey ↗, Link GitHub ↗, Upgrade and the GitHub App install open your browser on the first click, with *Open again* if you need it. Older apps keep the extra click.
- **Escape on a chat picture** (#380). Escape closes a full-size picture in chat, and the desktop app stays in full screen.
- **Running checks are yellow** (#379). A running or queued check in a PR block is yellow, not the accent colour, so it no longer looks like a pass.

## Note

- Control serves `/.well-known/webauthn` with the platform page's origin (#1868), the first step toward passkeys working on the platform page itself (spike S46, #1865).
