## Fixed

- **Words that still said devices and approvals** (#2652). Since signing in became enough (aru-141), some text still described approving devices. `arugulad join` now says to open its link in a browser that's signed in and click Add, and waits for that. The desktop app's sign-in no longer says it makes the app one of your devices. Teams says that owners, and members who drive, add their machines to a team.
