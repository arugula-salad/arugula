# Arugula 0.60.14

Made as a deploy.

## New

- **Settings on app.arugula.io are short and scannable** (#1455). What
  the agents on your machines may do is now one table per project: a row
  per machine, and a switch each for **Threads & issues**, **Runs**,
  **Commands** and **Deploys**, with your DMs in a small table of their
  own. A switch that holds only part of what it gives says so ("only
  runs.read"). Each section has a heading and one line, with the rest
  under **More**, and wide screens get an index of the sections. Every
  switch does what it did before.
