# Arugula 0.70.140

Escape goes back a place, across projects, and `g ;` (or ⌃Tab where the browser lets it through) flips to where you were in your last other project. The top bar has Look, and s opens it from anywhere you're not typing; s again closes it. Every page's top bar is the same now: where you are as a crumb that starts with the project (it opens your projects), then Go to… and Share. A board opens on its own page, with its Docs as tabs (1–4), main's red alert, its open PRs one row each, and Needs you and the lead's thread on the right. A board shows its lanes only; the Steps view and its Done and Links toggles are gone.

## Added

- **Back across projects, and flip back** (#2920). The app keeps one back stack for this tab, across projects, and a reload keeps it. Escape goes back a place when nothing else wants it: not from a field, a terminal, a dialog or an open menu, and not when the page uses Escape itself (zooming out, leaving the Swarm). Flip back goes to the last place you were in a different project; press it again to come back. Go to…, opened empty, now starts with the places you were lately, across projects.
- **An agent can withdraw or replace its approval card** (#2938). When an agent on your machine asks you to approve something, it now gets the card's link back and can give it to you in chat, since chat can't approve a card. For a personal project the link goes to your Inbox. If the agent asks again for the same thing it passes the old card's id as `supersedes`, so the old card is withdrawn instead of two cards starting two agents. It can also withdraw a card you no longer want (`withdraw`). An older Arugula cloud still takes the card, without the replacing.

## Changed

- **Look from the top bar, or s** (#2920). Look (this device's theme and its sliders) is one click or one key away on every page: the top bar's Look, or `s` when the keys aren't in a field or a terminal. The design reference keeps its own `s` for its page-only Look.
- **One top bar, a crumb you can climb** (#2920). The app's pages, a machine's page and a machine's own page share one top bar. Its crumb starts with the project, which opens the list of your projects, then the place and what's in it, each a step up; on a machine's page it's the machine, the session and the tab. The team's name is no longer the crumb's first step, and the command search field in a machine's header is now the Go to… button.
- **A board's page: its tabs, Needs you and the lead beside it** (#2930). A board is its own page now. On the left: its tabs (`1`–`4`: the board, each linked Doc, a PR you open from the row above the lanes), a red strip while a branch is red, the open PRs one row each, and the title with its lead, machine and progress. On the right: what on the board needs you (asks for a teammate folded under one row), answered in place, and the board's thread, which its lead reads. `f` filters the rows, `a` adds one, `j`/`k` move between them and `l` writes to the lead. Every project's Boards (`/boards`) is gone: Go to… finds a board, and old addresses still land.
- **Board tiles say their state and who they wait on** (#2930). A board's lanes read left to right Next, Doing, Review, Done, and the ones that wait on you come first in their lane. A tile is its number, a word for its state ("asks you", "asks Jake", "failing", "stalled 42m"), its title and one line: its PR and where it is, or who works on it, or "no agent yet". There are no more chips or colour fills on tiles, and no "no proof"; the drawer has the details. Done shows its first two and "N more".

## Removed

- **A board's Steps view** (#2930). A board is its four lanes, on a phone too. The order agents give a group's rows still shows in `board_read`'s Steps block.
