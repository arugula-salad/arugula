## Added

- **The iPhone and iPad app opens on home** (#2733). Home is the current project's conversations, sorted by what needs you, as the
desktop's Overview reads them: a pane that asks or failed on any of your
machines, a board's question for you, a PR whose checks fail or that waits for
review, and a hosted session waiting on you, worst first. Each open board is a
row with its lane bar, how much is done, what's going on and who leads it; it
opens the board's thread, where a post reaches the lead, and Board opens its
lanes. The tabs from before are in home's menu. On an iPad, home is the left
column and what it opens sits beside it.
- **Comments on Docs** (#2732). A comment is a message in the Doc's thread that links to its section and quotes the passage, so the thread and the agents reading it see what it's about. It mentions the agent session that made the Doc; Docs made from now on record it. On a narrow screen the comments are listed under the Doc. Control also serves a page that's only the Doc, for the iPhone and iPad app to show.
