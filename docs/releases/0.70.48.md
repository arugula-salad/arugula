## Changed

- **Remote typing off still lets you watch** (#2692). `arugulad sharing remote-input off` (or answering no when a machine joins) stops everyone typing into the machine through Arugula cloud, its owner included. Its owner now keeps a read-only view of every session on it from other devices: panes and agents show, but typing, answering an agent and sending a follow-up are refused. Other people still see only what's shared with them, or their team's.
