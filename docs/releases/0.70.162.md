## Added

- **A chat post can carry a signal to watch for** (#3027). `post_thread` takes an optional `signal`: a short slug (lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`, starting with a letter, at most 40 characters) such as `pr_ready` or `blocked`. A lead can sleep until a worker posts it with `watch` event `chat.reply`, match `{room, signal}`, instead of waking at every `agent.idle`. Board leads and issue agents are told to use it.
