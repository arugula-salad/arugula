# Arugula 0.70.71

Settings' This machine is Machines now, and its seven sections are four: Access and sharing, Agents, Name and Advanced. Old links land on the section that holds what they named. My Docs ends with Shared by you, the Docs you shared with a link, with Open, Copy link and Unshare, and Settings no longer sends you to your machines page for them. Settings › Team and billing › Machines removes a machine, takes one out of a team, moves or brings one of yours in, and shows the join command to add a new one, all in place. A team's owners invite people, change roles, remove members and answer who's waiting to join in Settings › Team and billing › Members, without opening the machines page. A project's settings drop the Overrides section; each setting its team has a default for says under its label whether it follows the team or is set here, with Reset.

## Changed

- **Your picture's menu leaves Settings to the rail** (#2766). Your picture's menu no longer has Your account…, since the rail's Settings goes there; on a phone, which has no rail, it stays.
- **Appearance's controls in Settings** (#2766). The daemon's own Settings page does the same. Preferences is part of Appearance now, so its old links open Appearance. On a phone's settings menu and in the command palette, Appearance… still opens the dialog.
- **Settings' sections in one flat list** (#2766). Every Settings scope lists its sections in one list, in the same order, with no group headings over them.
- **Settings › Machines, in four sections** (#2768). Settings' This machine is Machines now, and its seven sections are four: Access and sharing, Agents, Name and Advanced. Old links land on the section that holds what they named.
- **Remove arugula.nvim and Remote typing in Settings** (#2768). Settings › Machines has Remove arugula.nvim, in Advanced, and says whether Remote typing is on, in Access and sharing, with the command on the machine that changes it.
- **An unreachable machine explains itself in Settings** (#2768). When Settings › Machines can't reach a machine, one note says why (offline, not answering, can't reach it from here) and what to do, with its settings dimmed under it.
- **Labs flags in Settings › Team and billing** (#2767). It's the list your machines page's Teams panel shows. Just you sets yours; a
team's owners set the team's, and everyone else in it reads them. Labs shows
only once one of your machines has Developer settings.
- **Docs you shared by link are in Docs** (#2772). Open and Copy link ask the machine that shared the Doc, so they wait while it's offline. The account menu's Shared Docs… on your machines page opens the same place. Until Arugula cloud serves these links on the app, they stay on your machines page, as before.
- **In the iPhone and iPad app, a lead's name opens that agent** (#2769). This applies on Home's board rows, on the Boards tab's cards and over a board's thread. If the lead runs on a machine this device can't reach, its page says where it runs. A line under a thread or a peek says who you're talking to and where a post goes: straight to the agent, or to the board's thread, where its lead and anyone mentioned read it.
- **Team machines change in Settings** (#2770). Each machine of yours has Settings, Take out of team (or Move into a team, under Just you) and Remove, each asked first; a team's owners take out its members' machines too. Bring in one of yours shares a machine you have with the team, and Add a machine is the `arugulad join` command with Copy (with `--team` for a team's). A machine whose Arugula is from before control's moves says so. Your machines left You › Sign-in, which is now Sign-in and teams. While your control doesn't serve these on the app's address yet, moving and removing still open the machines page.
- **Team members change in Settings** (#2770). Members has an invite row (a role, Ask me first, Make a link), each member's role and Remove, who waits on an Ask me first link (Let in, Turn down), and the one-click links nobody has used yet, each with Revoke. An invite link still opens on control's page, which signs its person in. Making someone an owner, or removing an owner, takes your passkey, so it still opens the machines page; so does all of Members while your control doesn't serve its teams on the app's address yet.
- **Each project setting says Team default or Set here** (#2773). Where new issues go, Stalled after and Agent model say "Team default · follows Acme" (the team's name links to its Project defaults) or "Set here · team default is Opus · Reset"; Reset puts the project back on the team's default. Agent workspace, which no team sets, is now in Agents. The table of every project's choices stays in Team and billing › Project defaults, and the old Overrides address opens Issues and boards.
