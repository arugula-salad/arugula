## Added

- **@cos names your chief of staff** (#2926). Your chief of staff's block is named after its project, so its handle was long and changed on Restart. `@cos` in a project's rooms now reaches your own chief of staff, and `@machine/cos` reaches the chief of staff of that machine's owner: whichever session leads their staff room at the time. In the `@` list it's shown as Chief of staff under its machine.

## Changed

- **Your projects on the rail** (#2917). The rail is one rail on every page now, a machine's own page included: Arugula's mark, your projects' marks (the current one ringed; hover one for where you left it), the five places, then the Inbox, Settings and you. Past five projects it shows the current one and the list. The Machines place no longer counts what's new in runs' threads (Runs, in Machines, still does), and your account menu is under your initials at the rail's foot on a machine's page too.

## Fixed

- **A terminal Claude waiting on you reaches your Inbox** (#2927). Claude Code in a terminal pane with Arugula's hooks installed raises a notification when it needs you. The pane showed it, but your Inbox and a chief of staff watching for it never heard of it. A prompt to read a file outside the working directory, or to use a tool, now goes to your Inbox as a permission, and any other notification as a question. It leaves when the pane stops needing you. A pane that needs input some other way, with no hook, still isn't listed.
