## Fixed

- **The talk panel draws an agent's Markdown** (#2805). On the Overview, an agent's peek in the talk panel draws its transcript's Markdown as chat does, and on a phone Needs you sits right under the page's head.

## Removed

- **A board's lanes follow the work only** (#2804). A row's lane comes from its work: its issue, branch, pull request and agent. Dragging a card onto a lane, the row menu's **Move to**, the lane select in a row's details and the "set by" mark are gone, and so is closing an issue by moving its row to Done. Close an issue from the issue itself.

A row still moves between groups, by dragging it onto one or with **Move to group** in its menu, and Steps still reorders rows.
