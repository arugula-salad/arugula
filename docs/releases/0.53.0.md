# Arugula 0.53.0

Step 1 of the platform roadmap (#1122): control's planes written down,
the project row, and machines' power over your Cloud workspaces as grants
you can revoke. Nothing on screen changes.

## Grants

- **Each machine drives your Cloud workspaces through a `cloud.drive`
  grant** (aru-073). Every machine already joined to your account has one,
  and each new one gets one when it joins, so nothing changes until you
  revoke one.
- **`arugula grants`** lists them, and `arugula grants revoke <machine>` /
  `arugula grants restore <machine>` take the power from one machine and
  give it back. A machine without a live grant is refused on the Cloud
  workspace routes, with a message saying how to restore it. Your other
  machines aren't affected.

## Control

- **Projects** (aru-072, aru-074): every team has a default project, and
  Cloud workspaces, team channels and cloud secrets belong to one. Nothing
  reads them yet.
- **What control stores** is now a list, `crates/control/tables.toml`, with
  each table's plane, what it holds, why it has to exist while your machines
  are asleep, what deleting your account does to it, and who else sees it.
  A test fails when a table isn't listed, and "What control stores" in
  `docs/control-e2e.md` is written from it (aru-071).
- **Control deploys from release tags**, starting with this one (M133).
- `arugula-control census` counts the daemons control has seen by version.
