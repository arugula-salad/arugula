## Changed

- **Notifications you can check** (#1976, #468). Under ☰ › More › Notifications, the page says whether this device gets notifications and why not when it doesn't (on an iPhone: add Arugula to your Home Screen first). **Send a test notification** sends one to each of your devices and says how many it reached. When control has lost a device's subscription, the page hands it over again by itself. Control and the platform log what became of each push, without its contents.
- **A waiting deploy, one tap away** (#1976, #468). A deploy waiting for your approval shows as a row under the bar, on the phone too, and opens the deploy with **Approve with passkey**. A tap on a deploy's notification always lands on the deploy.
- **A second passkey takes only a passkey** (#1974, #470). Adding a passkey when you already have one needs that passkey's confirmation and nothing else: no device of yours has to sign it. A first passkey and removing one are unchanged (aru-126).
- **The confirmation names the new passkey** (#1975, #471). "Confirm the new passkey" says what's being added, for example "1Password passkey, made just now in Chrome on Linux".
- **Live signals outside a pane** (#1971, #453). Claude Code in VS Code, the desktop app or the editor block reports its live context to its conversation, not only from a terminal pane. Older plugins keep working.
- **Device keys, on one page** (#1973, #390). docs/control-e2e.md opens with what device keys are for, the kinds and who approves whom: device keys decide who reaches a machine, and passkeys decide what a person meant.

## Fixed

- **A merge closes the issues its body names** (#1969, #467) when the project doesn't mirror PR content: the merge reads the body from GitHub's delivery for that pass, without keeping it.

## Note

- Control no longer answers the four internal hub calls the platform stopped using in 0.66.6 (#1972). Update the platform and control together, as a release does.
- Deploy boxes still accept approvals given before 0.66.6 until they expire; that ends with a later release.
