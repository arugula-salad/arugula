# Arugula 0.66.16

Boards are on for everyone, and a project's Boards page is now the one place for its tickets. Counts on issue threads show only what wants you, and a Doc's public link now needs a confirm.

## Changed

- **Boards leave Labs** (#2137, #2140, #191). Every account has boards, epics and issues on boards.
  - Who can read and write them depends on project membership and role, and for an agent on its machine's `boards.read` and `boards.write` grants, as before.
  - `board_read`, `board_write` and `tracker_write` are listed for every agent. `arugula hooks install` allows `board_read` by default, and the other two with `--allow-acting`.
  - `arugula agent --issue` and `--take` now show in `arugula agent --help`.
  - An old `flags.json` that names `boards` still loads and turns nothing on, and `arugulad flags boards on` says boards left Labs.
- **Triage moves under Boards** (#2144, #2143, #656, #661). A project's Boards page shows its open boards, then **Not on a board**, then the Done fold, and the bar no longer has Triage. `/p/:project/triage` still works and lands on that section.
  - Rows can be selected, shift-selected, dragged onto a board, or added with ＋.
  - Select all covers every match of the filter, loaded or not, up to 500 issues at a time.
  - The list holds up past 1,000 open issues: it loads in pages, and filters, sorting and label counts run on the server.
  - On a phone, Boards and Not on a board are two tabs, and adding is select and ＋, with no drag.
- **Drag a row into another group** (#2169, #664). On a board with groups, dropping a row on another group, or on No group, moves it there and keeps its lane. The whole group shows the drop outline. Dragging within its own group still changes its lane.
- **The Boards page says when your agents can't see it** (#2167, #313). When one of your machines has no Boards grant over the project, a line under the header names it, with a link to Settings → Agents. Only owners and editors see it, and only about their own machines.
- **Counts say what wants you** (#2146). A count on an object's thread (an issue, a run, a mirrored PR) now includes only posts that mention you and, on an open native issue assigned to you, posts other than your own agents' and session cards. Other unread posts show as a grey dot with no number and stay out of the inbox's New in threads. Setting a thread to every message still counts all of it. Channels, DMs and pane threads don't change.
- **A Doc's link and share buttons** (#2142, #650). A Doc's bar has two icon buttons in place of its menu. On a Doc that isn't shared yet, the link button opens the share dialog, and the link is made only on **Create link**. Before this, one click made a public link.
- **A prototype's chips sit above its frame** (#2142, #620), so a prototype's own top-right controls can be clicked again.
- **A machine's copy of a Doc is read-only on the web** (#2150, #678). It says which machine it's from and where to change it, and agent sessions get the same refusal. Edits made to a copy before this release were already replaced by the machine's next copy, and can't be recovered.
- **Naming an agent replaces a reply's default** (#2139). A reply naming an agent who isn't in the thread yet no longer wakes the thread's lead too, and the To row shows the named agent.
- **"Offline" means offline** (#2139). Only a machine that isn't on the relay reads as offline. One that's too old to answer, has just reconnected or timed out reads as unknown.
- **The app says where things are in plain words** (#2134, #598). "Control" and "the platform page" become your machines page or Arugula cloud. "Use Control look" is now "Use the Slate look". Text only: no route or behaviour changes.
- **Hosted machines connect only through the relay** (#2155). Control lists them with no direct URLs, and the web client and CLI try none. Joined machines keep theirs.

## Fixed

- **Several agent blocks on screen no longer freeze the page** (#2166). Plain tool output draws as text rather than as a terminal, and older transcript entries aren't laid out until they scroll into view. Seven busy blocks went from freezes of 5–8 seconds on load to under half a second. Output with escape codes still gets a terminal.
- **The Copy link button is readable in light themes** (#2171, #462). Its text now passes 4.5:1 contrast on boards and Docs, and the button keeps a visible edge in every palette.
- **Host invites and waiting invites no longer wipe each other** (#2149, #677). Waiting person invites move to their own file, `person-invites.json`, and existing ones carry over on upgrade. After a downgrade, an older daemon won't see invites saved there, and picks them up again on the next upgrade if they're under a day old.

## Removed

- **`arugulad install --tailnet` and `arugulad sandbox`** (#2157). The install command's `--tailnet`, `--home`, `--join`, `--owner`, `--hostname`, `--port` and `--no-serve` go with them. On a machine without systemd, use `arugulad install --no-service`. A tailnet host already in a machine's `hosts.json` keeps working.

## Labs

- **Sandbox providers follow their flag** (#2153, #489). The Sprites and loam adapters used to open whenever a token or key file existed. With `sandbox-providers` off, neither opens now, no token is read and each call gets the flag's refusal.

## Under the hood

- **A PR or issue block reads GitHub at once when control refuses a read** (#2159, #674), instead of showing control's last answer for ten minutes. The inbox's shadow reporting, whose trial ended on 2026-11-02, is gone from the daemon and control.
- **Chat's follow-ups leave the mux** (#2160, #675). The terminal multiplexer keeps an opaque follow-up queue, and the daemon owns the chat rules. No change in behaviour or on the wire.
- **The first iPhone app is in the repository** (#2136, #603), and can't pair until `ARUGULA_NATIVE_CLIENTS` is set on the hub, so nothing changes for anyone yet.
- **More of CI runs on GitHub's runners** (#2131, #2152, #2154). That includes this release's arm64 tarball.
