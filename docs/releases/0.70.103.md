# Arugula 0.70.103

Installing with the install script on a machine with a browser opens your machine's page on Your work, already signed in, instead of a page asking for its sign-in link. In the Android app, Docs lists the current project's Docs, newest first, and one opens with a tap, prototypes and comments working. In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, the bell counts what wants you and opens the web's Inbox; on tablets it sits with your picture at the foot of the sidebar. Moving the mouse down Needs you no longer closes the open row in the gap between rows, so the list stays still. Claude Code's reminder a minute after a turn no longer turns its pane into "needs you" or sends a notification.

## Added

- **Docs on Android lists the project's Docs** (#2848). The list is the web's: Today, Yesterday, This week and Earlier, each Doc with when it was last edited, and a mark for a tabs or a prototype Doc. In a team's project, My Docs and Team Docs sit at the top, and the app remembers the one you used last. Switch project from the menu at the top and the list follows. On a wide tablet the open Doc sits beside the list; on a phone it opens over it, with Back to return. A board linked from the Doc opens on the Overview, an issue opens over the list, and another Doc opens in its place.
- **The Inbox behind a bell, and a tablet sidebar, in the apps** (#2847). The Inbox is grouped as on the web: deploys waiting for your approval first, then reviews requested, changes requested, checks failed, runs failed, mentions, assigned to you, new in threads, and ready, merged or closed. Opening something marks it seen, and Dismiss clears it. A native issue or a thread opens in the app; a PR opens on GitHub. On iPhone and iPad, tapping a notification about something in your Inbox opens it and marks it seen. On Android, `arugula://inbox/<item>` does the same, and `arugula://overview`, `boards`, `docs`, `machines` and `chat` open those places. On an iPad, when the sidebar is folded away (as in portrait), the bell and your picture go back to the top of each place. Phones keep them at the top, as before. Machines and its terminals are where they were.

## Fixed

- **The installer opens your page signed in** (#2845). The script opens the page with `arugula web --if-browser`, which opens the sign-in link in this machine's browser and never prints it. Over ssh, with no browser, or with the Mac app (which shows the page itself) it opens nothing and says to run `arugula web`, as before. `ARUGULA_NO_OPEN=1` keeps it from opening.
- **Needs you keeps its row open between rows** (#2850). Moving the mouse down Needs you no longer closes the open row in the gap between rows, so the list stays still.
- **No second "needs you" for an answer you read** (#2851). Claude Code's reminder a minute after a turn no longer turns its pane into "needs you" or sends a notification.
- **The app opens on a machine that's online** (#2852). Opening the app goes to the machine you were last on, or the first one online, instead of waiting on an offline machine that sorts first.
