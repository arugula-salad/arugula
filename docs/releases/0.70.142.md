## Changed

- **The agent panel** (#2940). The panel under Needs you always starts with your chief of staff: its room, where it runs and whether it hears you now. Moving through Needs you puts each asker in front, as an italic tab with its ×.

An agent's tab is its transcript, with each tool call folded to one line that opens, Claude Code's in a terminal too ("Read Crumb.tsx, window.spec.ts", "Edited 3 files"); `v` shows its terminal live instead, and `f` puts the transcript or terminal over the whole page (⤢ or Esc leaves). The box at the foot writes to it, with Silence and Stop beside it; the chief of staff's room and a thread write in the same box, with its glow and a line saying where a message goes. A session that has ended is read only, ending in when it ended; on a to-do, Done carries the conversation on with your note.

A message's replies open in the panel, over its room; Back, or Esc, returns to the room where you were, and Follow is in the ⋯ beside Back. `[` and `]` make the panel narrower or wider, `\` folds it to a strip, and `l` and `h` move between the panel and the work. The panel remembers its width.

Cut: a thread's own head in the panel (its "Thread" line and its ×), the panel's tabs and head on the floor, the who-line under the tabs, an agent's rung line with who started it and its parent's last word, an issue agent's thread above its peek, and a peek's Open in Panes and Board thread buttons (the Terminal and the floor replace them).
- **Questions and Swarm's rail as Needs-you rows** (#2940). A question from an agent (Claude's several questions with their options, checkboxes, Other and previews), a form, or a sign-in link is now a Needs-you row wherever it shows: in an agent block, beside a terminal running Claude Code, in a session's card in chat, on a machine's page and in the Needs-you drawer. The answers are the same as before.

Swarm's rail lists what needs you the same way: one row each, saying what it is and where, with its answers in place (Allow, the question's form, a diff, a gate's Approve, Rerun or Continue for a failure), then Open, Follow, Share and Dismiss. An answered one says who answered it, with its follow-up box.

Cut: the rail cards' colours by kind, their pop-in animation and their uppercase titles (the row's mark and word say what it is), and the cards' tinted frames around a question.
- **Docs, terminals and editors in the Arugula look** (#2943). A terminal's background is now the well it sits in, and its sixteen colors are the look's, each readable on it whatever preset or sliders you pick. VS Code and nvim blocks follow the same look. A Doc reads the same for everyone, in Arugula's own dark or light colors: its paper, ink, links and the named colors an agent draws with (leaf, pepper, sky, plum, sun, stone) changed with it, on the page, a shared link and the daemon's drawings. Terminals and agent output use SF Mono, falling back to the system's mono.
