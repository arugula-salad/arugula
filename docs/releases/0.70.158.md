# Arugula 0.70.158

`a` answers on Needs you and writes to a board's lead, `s` flips the panel to your chief of staff, `g a` opens #agents, and `?` shows the core first. A Codex agent block started in auto mode runs in Codex's own auto-review mode instead of stopping on every action, and a finished turn reads as done, not as waiting on you.

## Changed

- **Who got a message, in one small icon** (#3000). A message that tags agents, or reaches leads in #agents or a board's
channel, used to carry one line per agent and a `reached:` line under it.
Now it has one small icon after its time: a quiet check when everyone read
or answered it, a dim pulsing mark while it's on its way, and amber when
something is waiting, offline, silenced or not granted. A number beside the
icon says how many agents got it.

Hover the icon, or tap it on a phone, for a card listing each agent and
where it got to: "read", "answered ↗" (which opens the reply), or "waiting:
no session took it". When a machine has no session by the name you used, the
card lists the names it does have, and picking one puts it in the box you
write in. In a board's channel, every post shows the board's lead as
reached, including posts nobody replied to, in the channel, its threads and
on the board.
- **Pull requests wear GitHub's marks** (#3005). The board's work strip drops the "PR" word and the amber ◇ and green ●: each PR leads with GitHub's own shape for its state, then a running dot, a passed tick or a failed cross for its checks. The colours are GitHub's, tuned to read in both themes.
- **One letter, one verb: the default keys** (#3014). Arugula's keys now follow one list, with a small core you learn first: `j`/`k`
to move, ↵ to open, Esc to go back, `l` for the panel, `a` to answer, `/` to
find, `s` for your chief of staff, `?` for the keys, ⌘K for Go to…, and the
`g` places (`g o` `g i` `g c` `g b` `g d` `g m`, and now `g a` for #agents).

- **Needs you:** `a` answers the first way (it was `y`). `e` and `n` have no
  key by default: ↵ and `j` do their jobs.
- **A board:** `a` writes to the lead (it was `l`), `n` adds a row (it was
  `a`), and `/` filters. `l` and `f` are now the panel's everywhere.
- **The panel:** `s` flips to the chief of staff's tab and back. `h` has no
  key by default: Esc leaves the panel.
- **Look:** `s`, `t` and `d` no longer open Look, flip light and dark, or step
  the depth. The top bar's Look does all three.
- **Chat:** ⌘K is the room switcher there, and `?` now lists it.
- **Panes:** Ctrl+Shift+Z focuses one pane and restores the layout (it was
  Ctrl+Shift+Enter, which a terminal program may want).
- **Gone:** `g p` (⌘K is Go to…), `[` on list pages (the fold's button does
  it), and Ctrl+Tab in the browser, which keeps it anyway (`g ;` flips).
- **The panel's terminal drops its machine tag** (#3013). The agent panel's terminal no longer repeats its machine's name in its corner; the panel's head already says it.
- **Board PRs show drafts and passed checks** (#3020). Control now sends whether a board's PR is a draft and where its checks are (running, passed, failed or none). A board on a control from before this shows what it did.

## Fixed

- **Answer a board's ask from Needs you** (#3016). Open on a board's ask used to go to an old address and do nothing. Now it opens the ask's board, with the ask open in the board's Needs you, on a computer and on a phone.
- **Threads in #agents and a board's channel** (#3007). #agents and a board's channel showed a thread's replies but had no "Reply
in thread" on their messages, so only agents could start one. Now they
offer it as every channel does: it opens the thread in the room's own
column, and the "1 reply" line shows under the message.
- **Codex agent blocks run in auto mode** (#3019). Arugula's `auto` permission mode is Codex's `agent` mode, which asks only about what Codex's reviewer finds unsafe and keeps Codex's sandbox. It never lifts a restriction of Codex's own. If an agent doesn't offer the mode asked for, its block says so instead of starting in a different one. Agents still can't start another agent with checks turned off, under Codex's name for that mode too.
- **Machines' pane lines stay in their card** (#3002). On Machines, a pane's line under its session stays inside the card, starts with the session's own mark, and says one word and its kind.
- **A board's panes open beside it** (#3006). A pane opened on a board (a Doc, a PR, #agents, a run) used to land inside the board's own scroll, under its header and beside its lanes. Now the tab is split: the board is one pane with its header and lanes, and what you or the lead open sits beside it at full height, with its own title row and scroll, as panes do on Machines. The divider drags and the split is kept; Reset puts the board back alone.
- **A renamed lead still hears its board** (#3003). A Claude Code session that took a board's lead and was renamed afterwards (with `/rename`, say)
was told of the board's posts by its old name, which matched no session, so they waited unread
in its machine's inbox. A lead now goes by its Claude Code conversation: its posts reach the pane
that holds it, whatever it's called, and a lead whose conversation no pane holds any more is let go.
- **Your window in front sizes the terminal** (#3013). A terminal takes the size of your window in front, as tmux's latest client does, so a narrow view you left open no longer keeps a wide panel's terminal narrow.
