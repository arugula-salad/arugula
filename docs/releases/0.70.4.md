## Added

- **Watch-only remote access, and a log of who came** (#2507). Control is moving to deciding who reaches a machine (aru-141), so each machine gets two guards of its own. `arugulad sharing remote-input off`, run on the machine, makes everyone who comes through control a viewer: a channel that could type closes within two seconds and comes back as a viewer's. Only the machine sets it; nothing through control can turn it back on. And every channel opened to the machine is written to `remote-sessions.jsonl` in its state directory, with who it was, what they could do and how long they stayed.

## Fixed

- **Chat opens in the room you left** (#2503). Chat remembers, on each device and for each account, the room (and thread) you were last in, per project. Open Chat from the rail or the app's Chat tab, or reload, and you land there, at the place #2498 keeps in it. A room that has gone, or that you no longer have, opens as before: the newest unread, then the newest.

Unread in another room shows in the sidebar and doesn't move you. A link or a notification to a room still opens that room, and it's the one chat comes back to. On a phone, Back from the room you were taken to is the room list, and if you leave from the list, Chat opens the list.
