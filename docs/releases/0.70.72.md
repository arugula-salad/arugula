## Added

- **The board's own view in the iPhone and iPad app** (#2774). The tickets are grouped the way the board groups them: Doing, then Review, then Next. Each one shows its PR or checks and a word for how it stands: working, in review, next or asks you. The agents on it show as names, and a name opens that agent. A ticket opens its work, and the done tickets fold into one line. A board with no lead says so. In a team's project, it offers to ask your chief of staff to start one. All lanes opens the board's full lanes.
- **A ticket's work, live, in the iPhone and iPad app** (#2776). Each terminal is drawn at its own size, so nothing resizes the pane on your other screens. A tap brings up the keyboard, with esc, tab, ctrl and the arrows over it, and full screen sizes the pane to the device. An agent block shows what it says as it says it, with a box that types to it, and a session in Arugula cloud shows its last words with a reply box. On an iPad up to four panes stand side by side, with the rest in a strip under them. On an iPhone the panes come one at a time, with a strip to pick between them. A teammate's session can be read, not typed into.

## Changed

- **What's pushed, set in Settings › You** (#2771). Turning this device's notifications on or off, and sending a test, still
open your machines page: each device turns itself on with its own keys
there. Until your Arugula cloud serves these on the app, both stay links
to your machines page.
- **Sign-in and security in Settings › You** (#2771). Sign out of one place or everywhere, unlink GitHub, change your email and
delete your account right there. Adding or removing a passkey and linking
GitHub still open your machines page, where your browser asks for them.
Until your Arugula cloud serves these on the app, the section is the link
to your machines page it was.
- **Team and billing's default project is in Project defaults** (#2777). The sections are Members, Machines, Chat, Plan and billing, Project defaults and Labs, in that order, with no headings between them. Project defaults opens with the default project, still a select and a Save that only the team's owners see, and its row in the list says it first. An old link to General, `/settings/team/<team>/general` or `#general`, opens Project defaults.
- **Lend this device to agents from the app** (#2775). The switch is this device's alone, as before. Where the app is, control's Sign-in and account no longer shows it, except to turn off lending that's already on there.
