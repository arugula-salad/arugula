## Changed

- **Appearance is a theme and three sliders** (#2909). Look replaces the nine palettes, the four finishes, the three corner styles and chat's compact rows. In the middle of each slider the app looks as it did with Slate (dark) or Latte (light).

If you'd picked a light palette (Latte, Platinum, Solarized light), you're on Light; Match system is System; any other palette is Dark. Panes keep their soft corners, and chat keeps its pictures and names over the text.

Cut, and can come back if people miss them: the Mocha, Platinum, Solarized light, Nord, Gruvbox, Tokyo Night and Dracula palettes; the Aqua, Brushed metal and Glass finishes; square and round corners; compact chat rows; the "Use the Slate look" button.
- **The design reference shows the new kit** (#2912). The page at `/design` draws the fourteen parts of the new kit (surfaces, rows,
the Needs-you row, wells, state marks, the crumb, keys, avatars, the agent
panel, the composer, tabs, sheets, buttons and fields) with the real
components. Look (`s`) repaints the page in Slate or Latte and along the
light, warmth and depth sliders. Nothing else in the app changes yet.
