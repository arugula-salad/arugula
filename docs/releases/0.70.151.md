## Changed

- **A plain claude asks over its tab, not its terminal** (#2986). On a machine's page, when Claude Code in a terminal asks a question or wants to use a tool, its card now sits under the tab's head line, above the panes, instead of over the terminal it's about. It needs no Hide any more. On a phone it stays in the pane. Pane headers no longer show a number; a pane's number and its Ctrl+Shift shortcut are in its kind icon's tooltip.
- **A board's lead hears when work it waits on moves** (#2988). A session that leads a board (`lead_board`, or by making it with `create_board`) gets a standing watch on the new `board.wait.changed` event for that board: a blocker held by another board had its PR merged, go red or leave the merge queue. `lead_board` says so, and `release` cancels the watch. A session with no hooks gets none, and is told.

## Removed

- **Swarm is gone; Machines and Running take its place** (#2987). The Swarm view, every pane on every machine as a field, is gone. Machines lists each machine's sessions one row each, and the top bar's **Running** shows every live process across your machines, with the alerts first. An old Swarm link (`#swarm`, or the Overview's Swarm address) opens Machines, at the pane it named where it can. A gate or a decision waiting on you still shows in its workspace block, on the phone's sheet and in Needs you.
