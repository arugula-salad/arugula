## Fixed

- **Agents' build folders are cleaned up** (#2837). Agents build in a folder of their own under `~/.cache`. Closing a worktree now
removes its folder, a hosted session's end removes its build folders (sccache stays),
and the daemon sweeps the rest every 6 hours: folders no open worktree owns, that
nothing has touched for 3 days and that no build is using.
- **A hung daemon no longer holds up Claude Code's tools** (#2846). When the Arugula daemon stops answering, Claude Code's tool calls in a pane wait at most 2 seconds for its hook, not half a minute.
