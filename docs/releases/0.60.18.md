# Arugula 0.60.18

Made as a deploy.

## New

- **A project's settings are short and scannable** (#1465), like your own
  settings in 0.60.14. Linked repos are a table, with **Keep bodies** as
  a switch on each row and Unlink as a quiet action. An unlinked repo says
  when it goes and offers **Link again**. Each environment is a card:
  its bar shows the latest deploy's state, its secrets show as "held" or
  "minted per run", its deploys are a short table, and **Deploy…** sits on
  the card. Runs, agent tokens, project secrets, Claude spend and apps
  from products follow in the same sections, each with one line and the
  rest under **More**, and wide screens get an index of them. On a phone
  the page no longer runs off the side.
