## Changed

- **The rail lights a pill around the icon** (#2585). The place you are in lights a small pill around its icon, with its label in the accent colour below, instead of the whole rail button.
- **A session's card is in its ⋯ menu** (#2592). A pane's header had a chip naming its session's project and card, cut to fit beside the title. The card belongs to the session, so it's now in the session's ⋯ menu (the sidebar row's ⋯ and the header's ▾), and the pane's bar keeps its room for the title. On a phone the chip under the bar stays, as before.
- **Overview rows say one thing** (#2589). Each row in flight on the Overview is one line with one state word, such as asks you, checks failing or in review. Hover or focus a row to see its agent, machine and last line.
- **Your presence is in Chat** (#2593). Presence left your picture's menu for a row at the foot of Chat's rooms list, and the menu's Settings row is now *Your account…*, still opening Settings.
- **The rail is three spaces: Plan, Build, Talk** (#2594). Seven places on the rail become three. Pick a space on the rail, then a place in the bar beside the path: Plan's bar is Overview, Boards, Docs and, for now, Sessions; Build's is Panes, Swarm and Machines. Talk is Chat. Inbox, Settings and you stay at the rail's foot.

A machine's own page (control's and a daemon's) draws the same three spaces. Plan and Talk open the app's, Build is the page itself, and Swarm and Machines move from the rail into the machine's bar. A machine's own Docs are under Plan's Docs wherever there is a platform page; a machine on its own keeps its Docs place in the bar.

On a phone the Inbox is a tab, so the bell leaves the top bar, and Settings is under your picture.
- **The Overview uses the whole window** (#2596). A board that asks you opens by itself. Click any other board's row to see its cards in flight. While chat is on, the header shows your team's faces and who is around. When nothing needs you, Needs you is one quiet line. On a phone it comes first.

## Fixed

- **No double gap at the top of Machines on the Mac** (#2588). In the Mac app, the Machines page no longer leaves an empty band above its bar.
- **Back right after a page opens** (#2584). A page that tidies its own address (an issue's readable name, a room found by its id) could do so a moment after Back and overwrite the page Back went to.

## Removed

- **No settings link under the project title** (#2587). The project's Overview no longer has a Project settings link under its title; Settings on the rail opens it.
