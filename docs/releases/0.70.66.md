## Added

- **The Overview says what happened since you last looked** (#2754). Clicking it opens the chief of staff's tab. The page counts the line from the event log itself, so it works while your chief of staff is paused. It counts three things. Merged is pull requests merged. Went red is a branch whose check failed, when that branch is main or a card's session's. Wait on your review is pull requests that became ready where you're a requested reviewer now. Control keeps the number your Overview last showed you, per project. It moves on when you leave the Overview or hide its window. A person reads the log through `GET /api/projects/{project}/events`, the agents' read checked against that person's own access. Needs the next server release.
- **Your chief of staff and board leads in the iPhone app** (#2755). In a team's project, Home starts with "Ask your chief of staff": your staff
room, which only you and your own agents read, with who leads it and how
many messages wait there. It opens over Home on an iPhone and beside it on
an iPad. On Boards, each board with a lead shows it under the board: a dot
for how its session stands, its name (a teammate's with theirs) and its
machine. Tapping it opens the board's thread, where a post reaches the lead.
Over the staff room and a board's thread, Peek opens the lead's session.
