## Added

- **Answer a to-do from an ended session's panel** (#2969). The agent panel opens on the to-do's agent when you open its row under To do. Once the to-do is answered, its card in your chief of staff's room shows the same receipt as the row: Done or Can't, when, and your note, which only you see.
- **Watches hear a PR leave the merge queue** (#2971). A pull request that leaves a linked repo's merge queue without merging now wakes a watch on it, with GitHub's reason (`failed_checks`, `merge_conflict`, `manual` and so on). Watch a PR `until: "dequeued"`, or watch the events `github.merge_queue.added` and `github.merge_queue.removed`, matching on `pr`, `repo` and `reason`. A `checks_failed` watch on a queued PR also fires when its merge-group run fails.

## Changed

- **Layout, the sentences the lead follows** (#2962). Layout (a board's ⋯ → Layout…) holds the sentences the board's lead follows when it arranges the board, each off until you turn it on: "Docs open as tabs" and "Previews as tabs". They're kept with your account, so every machine's agents read the same.
- **The board's lead and channel in one tab** (#2968). A board's right column is now the agent panel, under the board's Needs you. ✦ is the project's chief of staff. The board's own tab shows **Lead 🔒**, your private conversation with the board's lead, with Transcript and Terminal (`v`). Its **⇄ channel** switch, or a second click on the tab, turns it into **# the board**, the board's channel, where everyone in the project reads and writes. A thread opens in place, and Esc goes back. When the lead also leads other boards, a line says which.
- **Chat is drawn on the kit** (#2965). Chat no longer draws a box of its own: the rooms and the messages are one section, and a thread or Details is a second section beside it. The bar names the room you're in (Arugula › Chat › #general), and the ⋯ for Mark all as read, sections and unread only sits beside search. Rooms are the same rows as the rest of the app, with unread in bold, and the box you write in glows while you're in it. Your presence (Automatic, Away, Appear offline) moved from the foot of Chat's rooms to your picture's menu.
- **A card opens its page, with its worker beside it** (#2970). On a board's page, a tile's body opens the card's page. Its ⋯ → Details still opens the drawer over the board. Moving through the tiles with `j` and `k` shows each card's worker in the agent panel as a visitor tab, and a card with no worker brings the board's tab back. A card's page (an issue on a board) now has the agent panel beside it, with its worker in front when it has one.

## Fixed

- **A to-do's card answers as a to-do** (#2972). It answers as the to-do's row under To do does: the note is checked for secrets as you type, and once answered, the card shows the same receipt as the row.
