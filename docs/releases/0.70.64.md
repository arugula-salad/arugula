# Arugula 0.70.64

The Overview has a panel on its right with Needs you on top and conversation tabs below; a board row's thread button opens the board's thread there, so you can read and post without leaving the page. An agent anywhere, a cloud one included, can be woken when an agent block on one of your machines asks you something or finishes its turn. Clicking a card's row or a session on no card opens a peek in the Overview's talk panel: its last lines, a box to reply, Silence and Stop.

## Added

- **Talk from the Overview** (#2746). A tab shows the thread's messages and a box to write in, with a dot when something new arrives while another tab is open; × closes it, and up to five stay open, kept for the browser tab. On a narrow window the panel docks under the boards. Threads come with chat: while chat is off, the rows have no thread button.
- **An Android app, for testers** (#2745). The Android app runs on the same core as the iPhone app. You sign in through
the browser, and the tab bar is always there: Home, Boards, Chat, Machines and
You. Testers install a debug build for now; Play's internal testing comes
next.
- **Agents watch a machine's agents ask and go idle** (#2748). `watch` with `event` set to `session.needs_input` (with `kind`: ask or permission) or `session.idle`, matched by `session` (`<machine>/<pane>`), `project`, `machine` or `pane`. They come from the state your machines already report for boards, so they need the project's boards.read grant, and they say only which block, its kind and when: never what it asks, a command or its output.
- **Agents read a project's event log** (#2747). `watch` with kind `events` (`project`, `after`, `types`, `limit`) answers the log's events after `after` in order, as stored (ids and states only), with `next_after` and the log's head. Each one is checked as a watch on it is: its type's grant over the project, and whether its owner may read what it's about now. A hosted session reads its own project's. An `after` older than the 14 days the log keeps says those events are gone. Needs the next server release.
- **A staff room for you and your own agents** (#2749). Agents open it as room `staff` in read_thread and post_thread, and `post_thread` with `lead: true` makes the calling session its lead. It shows in Talk and on the phone as a direct message named "Chief of staff". It's where the chief of staff will talk to you.
- **Peek at a session from the Overview** (#2752). The peek says the session's state, machine and branch, and reads its last lines from its machine only while the tab is open. A reply goes into an agent block at its next tool call, and is typed with Enter into Claude Code in a terminal or a shell. Open in Panes ↗ opens the whole terminal. A teammate's session is read-only, with Ask its lead opening the board's thread. A session in a cloud workspace is read from its box while it runs, and shows what it last said once its box is paused. A card with a session now opens its peek; the card's link in the peek opens the card.
