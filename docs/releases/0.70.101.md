## Changed

- **Android's Machines is one page of your sessions** (#2840). Machines on Android is now the web's page: each machine with something in
the project you're in, its session cards, and each session's panes as one
line with a Needs you chip. Machines with nothing in the project fold into
one line at the bottom, and an offline machine can't be opened.

On a phone, tapping a session's name opens it a tab at a time, its panes
stacked and live, and a question can be answered right there. Tapping a pane
opens it full screen with the keyboard and the key bar (Esc, Tab, Ctrl and
the arrows), to type in as before.

On a tablet the page sits beside the session's live tab. Before you pick
anything, that side shows the pane you were last in.
- **The iPhone and iPad app's tabs are the web's five places** (#2839). Home is now called Overview. You is no longer a tab: tap your picture at the top of any tab for your name, Settings, Send feedback, the app's version and Sign out. The bell beside it opens your Inbox, and New is there too, on every tab. On an iPad, the five places fold out into a sidebar. Docs says the project's Docs are coming. Settings opens the web's page in your browser. Machines and its terminals are where they were, one tap away.
