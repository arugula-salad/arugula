## Added

- **Docs on iPhone and iPad lists the project's Docs** (#2843). The list is the web's: Today, Yesterday, This week and Earlier, each Doc with when it was last edited, and a mark for a tabs or a prototype Doc. In a team's project, My Docs and Team Docs sit at the top, and the app remembers the one you used last. Switch project from the menu at the top and the list follows. On an iPad the open Doc sits beside the list; on an iPhone it opens over it, with Back to return. A board linked from the Doc opens on the Overview, and an issue opens over the list.
- **Pinned sessions come first in the apps' Machines** (#2841). Machines in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps now opens with Pinned: the
sessions you pinned, as cards in the order you pinned them, each naming its
machine. They're the same pins as the web's, so a session pinned on your
computer is pinned on your phone too.

Long-press a session's card to pin or unpin it. A pinned session leaves its
machine's list while it's pinned, as it does on the web.

## Changed

- **The Android app's tabs are the web's five places** (#2842). Home is now called Overview. You is no longer a tab: tap your picture at the top of any tab for your name, Settings, Send feedback, the app's version and Sign out. The bell beside it opens your Inbox, and New is there too, on every tab. On a tablet, the five places are a rail down the left. Docs says the project's Docs are coming. Settings opens the web's page in your browser. Machines and its terminals are where they were, one tap away.
