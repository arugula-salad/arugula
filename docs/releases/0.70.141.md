## Changed

- **Add row and New issue are a sheet** (#2936). On a board, Add row (or `a`) opens a sheet: a title, a description, and Start an agent on it. It opens the issue, puts it on the board and, when you ask, starts an agent on it. New issue under All issues is the same sheet, and the New issue page's address opens it. To put an issue or PR that's already there on the board, use the board's ⋯, Add an existing item.
- **An issue on a board is its card** (#2936). An issue that's on a board is that board's card: beside it, its board, the worker on it and where it runs, the board's lead, the branch and the PR; above Activity, its open ask, answered right there; and in Activity, a line for each answer ("aaron answered Squash"). The path climbs Boards, the board, then the card. Labels and assignees are clicked to edit; their Edit buttons are gone.
- **All issues is a row of Boards** (#2936). A project's issues are a row of Boards now, All issues, after the boards: select it and they fill the middle, with Open and Closed, search, Label, Assignee and Export as before. Old Issues links land on it. Not on a board, first in the list, shows only while some open issue is on no board.
- **The Overview, in the new look** (#2929). The left shows the project's name with how many sessions are working, how many cards fail and how many were done today; then Boards, each moving board a card with its progress and what's going on, the quiet ones folded into one card; then Running, on to Machines. A board's card opens the board.

On the right, Needs you lists this project's asks. A row opens in place: a permission's command with Allow and Deny, an approval's terms with its options, a question's choices. Answered, it stays as a receipt until you leave. To do follows, with the how to copy and a note. A teammate's asks fold into "2 asks for Jo". `j` and `k` move through them, `e` opens one, `y` and `x` answer, `D` is Done, `n` goes to the next thing that needs you and `↵` opens it where it lives.

The zoom (Project, Board, Work), the briefing line, Not on a card and Your work are gone from the Overview; the board, the chief of staff's room and Machines have what they showed.
