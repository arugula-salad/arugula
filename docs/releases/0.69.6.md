# Arugula 0.69.6

People blocks and live presence (video avatars, voice and calls on arugula.stream) were taken out of Arugula; chat's presence dots stay.

## Fixed

- **Chat's send arrow is full size again** (#2317). Chat's send button shows its arrow again: since 0.69.3 it had shrunk to a dot in the platform's chat.

## Removed

- **Video presence is gone** (#2324). The People block (a team's room on arugula.stream, with avatars or outlines from
your camera, waves, voice and calls) and live presence (the `live-presence` Labs
flag: Off, Watching or Live, and live faces on avatars across Arugula) were
removed. A People block left in a layout goes when its machine's daemon
restarts, and a `live-presence` flag left in `flags.json` does nothing. Chat's
presence (active, away, appear offline) is unchanged.
