## Changed

- **Arugula has its own look** (#2925). Pick a preset next to the theme: Arugula (the default), Moss, Sea glass, Stone & ink or Graphite & acid. Each sets the accent and the tint, and where the warmth and depth sliders start; the sliders tune it from there. Amber means something needs or asks you and red means failing, whatever the preset.

What's working (a session, a Doing card, a board's bar, checks running) now takes the accent, not yellow; what needs you (an ask, a question, waiting for you) takes amber, not the accent. In review is blue.

Ids, counts, times and labels are now set in mono. The app no longer downloads its own fonts: it uses Helvetica Neue and SF Mono, or the closest your system has. If you'd moved the sliders, they start again at Arugula's; your theme stays.

Cut, and can come back if people miss them: Slate's and Latte's colors for the app itself (Docs, terminals and editors keep them for now), and the bundled Instrument Sans, Big Shoulders and JetBrains Mono fonts.
