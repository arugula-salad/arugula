## Changed

- **Runs move to Running and the project's Settings** (#2990). The Runs tab under Machines is gone. **Running**, in the top bar, shows what runs now across your machines, with the project's CI runs and alerts. **New run**, the project's **run specs** with their schedules and event rules, and the standing grant for runs are in the project's Settings, under **Runs**. A run keeps its own page, and an old Runs link opens Running.

The attention drawer is gone too: Needs you on the Overview, Running and each pane's own mark cover what it showed. A tab's head line keeps Tamed, Grid and + Pane; a pane's header still has Focus.

## Fixed

- **One key does one thing** (#2993). When a page and the agent panel (or the whole app) both had a key, pressing
it ran all of them. Now the most specific one runs: a dialog's, then the
page's, then the agent panel's, then the app's. On a board, `l` puts you in
the lead's box and `f` opens Filter, and the panel stays as it was. On Chat,
⌘K (Ctrl+K) opens Jump to a room, without Go to… opening behind it;
Ctrl+Shift+K still opens Go to… there. Escape closes the top dialog, not
every one under it.

After a `g`, Arugula waits a moment for the place's letter. If you click
into a field instead, it stops waiting, so the next key you type lands in
the field.

## Removed

- **A machine's activity drawer, Agents page and places bar go** (#2990). Three parts of a machine's own page are gone:

- **The activity drawer** under a tab. `arugula history` still reads a session's commands, answers and agent turns.
- **The Agents page** (`#agents`), which held a machine's recipes and the team's catalog. A machine's agents and the recipes it offers stay in its agents block. An old `#agents` link opens the panes.
- **The places bar** over the panes, with Panes, Runs and Recipes. The rail and Go to… have every place. On a phone, Settings keeps its Panes button to go back.

The Machines page's old **Agents only** address (`?agents`) opens Machines.
