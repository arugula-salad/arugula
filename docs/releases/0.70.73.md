## Added

- **Your devices in Notifications** (#2779). Under This device, Your devices shows each browser, desktop app and iPhone with its kind and when it was last used, most recent first. Off means nothing is sent to that device, and it stays off even if it turns notifications on again; Remove, which asks first, deletes it until that device turns notifications on again.

## Changed

- **Settings › You is four sections** (#2779). Notifications holds this device, what every device is pushed about, how loud chat is, the words that notify you and quiet hours, in that order. Connected apps and agents holds your linked products, your agent tokens and the apps that may use Claude for you. Teams are in Team and billing; the Shared Docs row, shown while Docs can't list them, is at the bottom of Sign-in and security. An old link to one of You's sections, such as `/settings/you/quiet-hours`, `/settings/you/tokens` or `#push`, opens the section that holds it now.
