# Arugula 0.70.7

A follow-up or mention waiting for Claude Code in a terminal is kept on disk, so a daemon restart or update no longer loses it, and one meant for an earlier session in the pane says so. On app.arugula.io, Panes and Swarm on the rail open your machines inside the app, in the project you're in, instead of on a page of their own. The rail is your project's places, then your team's (Chat, Machines); its top tile switches project, and on a phone the places are a bar along the bottom. In the app, Machines shows each machine with whether it's online, whose it is and what you may do there, its sessions and the ones that need you; a click opens its page. "`arugula login` prints a link: click Allow in a browser that's signed in, and the CLI reaches your machines with tokens from Arugula cloud, with no device to approve."

## Added

- **Machines lists your team's machines** (#2534). The Machines page at `/p/<project>/machines` lists every machine you reach, with Cloud workspaces marked as cloud. Each row says whether the machine is online, whose it is and whether you own it, can steer it or can watch it, how many sessions it has and how many need you, and the projects those sessions serve. Machines with sessions that need you come first, then the ones that are online, then the rest by name. On a phone it's a simple list.

## Changed

- **Panes and Swarm open inside the app** (#2532). A machine's page (its sessions, tabs and panes) and Swarm are at `/p/<project>/machines…`, beside the app's rail, reached through Arugula cloud with machine tokens. Until Arugula cloud reaches machines from app.arugula.io, they open on your machines page as before.
- **The app opens on your project** (#2533). The bar above a page is now the path to where you are (the team, the project, the place), each a step up. Swarm is under Machines, beside Panes. Runs and Workspaces are at the top of Sessions. On a phone, More holds Docs, Deploys, Machines and Settings, and the bell is the Inbox.
- **The CLI signs in from your browser** (#2539). `arugula login` no longer makes the CLI a device that another of your devices approves. It prints a link to Arugula cloud; click Allow there in a browser that's signed in, and the CLI is signed in with a session, as the desktop app is. If the browser is on another computer, paste the address it lands on into the CLI. `--host NAME` then opens each machine with a short-lived machine token (aru-141) and a key made for that connection. A CLI logged in before keeps working: it uses tokens too, and its key only where Arugula cloud gives none or a machine's daemon is older than 0.70.4. `arugula login --device` still makes a device the old way.
- **The app reopens where you left each project** (#2535). Kept in your account's prefs, so your phone and your computer agree. A project you haven't opened a page of opens on its Overview. A machine's page keeps the pane it shows in its address, so a reload comes back to it.

## Fixed

- **Follow-ups survive a daemon restart** (#2530). Claude Code's hooks now confirm each follow-up they print. One the hook never confirmed is offered again, so a hook that died mid-answer doesn't lose it either.
- **Mention states catch up after a lost connection** (#2531). A mention sent to a machine twice reaches its agent once.
