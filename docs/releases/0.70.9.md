# Arugula 0.70.9

"`arugula login` makes the CLI one of your devices again, approved from one you have; `arugula login --browser` signs it in from a browser with no key." In the app, a board card's drawer shows its agent's terminal live, with its question at the top to answer in place, Hand off to me and Give it back, and Open on its machine. A project's Overview shows each board's cards in Doing and Review with the sessions on them, the sessions on no card, and what needs you, answered in place. A project's Sessions page lists every session in the project, on a computer or in a cloud workspace, grouped by the card it works on, and opens a pane live in two clicks.

## Added

- **Steer a card's agent from its drawer** (#2548). While you drive, the drawer says so and outlines the terminal; on a teammate's computer you watch, and it says why. A card with no session says so. Agent blocks moved from Attached into Live session, so each shows once. On a phone the drawer fills the screen, with the key bar while you drive. Control's own page keeps the session's state card.
- **A project's Overview is its work in flight** (#2550). The Overview in the app is Swarm for one project. Each board with cards in flight leads with its cards in Doing and Review, counting what's next and done, with its progress. Each card is a tile holding the sessions working on it: the agent and its title, where it runs and its last line. A card waiting on you pulses. Cards that need you come first, then failures, quiet ones and working ones, and boards are ordered by their most urgent card. **Not on a card** comes last.

**Needs you** on the right is Swarm's own rail for this project's panes, each card naming its card: allow, deny or answer it there. Click a card to open its drawer, the same one as on the board; the path in the bar and Esc go back up. Click a session to open it on its machine. On a phone, the Overview is one list with Needs you on top. Swarm for every machine stays under Machines, and what the project's page showed before is below the field.

## Changed

- **Browser sign-in for the CLI is opt-in for now** (#2541). `arugula login` is back to making the CLI one of your devices, approved with a code from a device you already have. Signing in from a browser, new in 0.70.7, is now `arugula login --browser`. It stays opt-in until Arugula cloud can tell a CLI's session from a browser page's. Either way, `--host NAME` reaches machines with short-lived machine tokens first. A CLI already signed in from a browser keeps working.
- **Sessions lists every session, by card** (#2552). Sessions (`/p/<project>/sessions`) used to list only hosted agent sessions. It now lists the sessions on people's computers too, grouped by their card. Each group's heading shows the card, its board and its lane, with **Open card**, which opens the card's drawer on its board. Groups with something waiting on you come first, and **Not on a card** (lead sessions, scratch shells, release terminals) comes last. Finished sessions fold to one line per group. Click a computer's session to see its panes, each with its line: **Open** shows the pane live over the page, and **Open on its machine** goes to the machine's page on that pane. Label, Tracker, Asked by and Going only still narrow the list, and Runs and Workspaces stay at the top. Until Arugula cloud serves the new list, the page shows the hosted sessions as before, grouped by their issues.

## Fixed

- **A cloud session whose turn ended stays in sight** (#2553). A hosted session's `done` only says its turn ended; `stopped` is the one that ended. `unresponsive` now counts as a failure rather than quiet.
