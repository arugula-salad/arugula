# Arugula 0.34.0

A second pass over the web client from a review of control.arugula.io
(#753): what each view covers, one list of what needs you, clearer
buttons, and tidier dialogs and menus.

## What each view covers

- **Views that show every machine say so** (#754). The swarm, chat and
  Agents → Team show *All machines* where the machine picker is; Panes and
  Recipes keep the picker. Chat lists the machines it doesn't show, and
  why (*Not here: jake-mini (chat is off there)*).
- **Machine settings are in the machine menu** (#757): *This machine:
  geek* has Permission rules and Developer settings; the account menu
  keeps them only where there's no machine menu.
- **The command palette starts with the view it's opened over** (#757):
  Places first (Go to Panes, Swarm or Chat, Chat's Activity, Agents'
  Recipes and Team), then the pane's commands, each saying which pane.

## What needs you

- **The swarm and Chat's Activity agree** (#754): both read one list, and
  a finished pane leaves 15 s after it's first shown on either.
- **Cards hold still under the pointer**: one cleared elsewhere fades in
  place instead of letting the next slide under your click. One you act
  on goes at once (#766).
- **One set of buttons**: the card's own action (Rerun, Allow, Answer…)
  or **Open**, then **Dismiss**, as in Activity. *Show* and *Edit* are
  gone; the card's title finds it in the swarm.
- Cards and Activity rows lead with what the pane is doing, a grouped
  card names every project it covers, and machine names drop `.local`.
- A project is named after the repository its origin points at, a
  worktree's included, not its folder.

## Names and buttons

- **Run on <machine>** everywhere an agent runs (#755), for *Run here*,
  *Run* and *Run there*. *Refresh*, *offers no agents* and *last use
  unknown* replace terms that only made sense inside.
- The swarm's header is *Swarm*. The session's button says *Session*; the
  bar's **Huddle** and **Share** sit beside it, and its menu doesn't
  repeat them.
- **New recipe here** on each project with none (#756); a **▾** beside
  **+** for the other kinds of tab; Agents' Recipes and Team are small
  tabs of their own; the swarm's how-to is said once; blocks label their
  panes sooner; an @mention that reached nobody is marked as such.

## Dialogs

- **Dialogs never run off the screen** (#757): they scroll, with a ×
  always in reach.
- Recovery codes and your name moved to *Sign-in and account*, with the
  passkeys. Unused browsers fold into one line (*Show them · Remove
  them…*). A machine's team picker is labelled *Team*. Every Remove looks
  like the destructive action it is.
- The swarm's city keeps its towers off the controls and the colour key.

## Also

- **A resumed agent under zsh runs again** (#752). A pane restored with
  its agent hung on a Mac (zsh's `-c` never gave the agent the terminal);
  it now runs through `/bin/sh` inside the login shell.
- `just dev-control` stops everything it started and won't start over an
  earlier run's port (#754).
- `docs/testing.md` shows the journey map (#728).
