## Changed

- **Settings is one list for You, Project and Team** (#2949). - **One list per scope.** You, Project and Team each show their sections as rows, with what's set on each line. A row opens in place, and a link opens it.
- **Appearance is Look's.** Theme, preset and the sliders are in Look (`s`, or Look in the top bar). Tips, the editor and lending this device are under Preferences.
- **Add a machine is on Machines.** The join command moved from a project's settings to Machines' Add a machine.
- **The Agents row** holds the model, workspaces, the standing grant, CI alerts and Claude spend.
- **A team's page is Settings › Team.** Old links to it, to Appearance and to the sections that merged still land in the right place.
- **Agents' asks and to-dos on a board and its cards** (#2941). A board's Needs you, in its right third, now holds what the agents working on its issues wait on: their approvals and to-dos, answered in place, and their questions, opened where they're asked. Each row says who it waits on: "asks you", "waits on you" or "asks Jake". A teammate's rows are neutral, not amber, and fold under one line each ("· 1 for Jake ›"). A tile in the lanes names who is asked too. An issue's card shows the same items above Activity, and once one is answered, Activity says so: "aaron answered Approve", or "aaron did Rotate the CI deploy key · rotated in the vault", the note as its record keeps it, shown to its person alone.
- **Docs are one list** (#2947). Docs no longer have My Docs and Team Docs tabs, a sort, a search box or a preview: each row is a Doc's title, who made it and when, the latest changed first, and a team's list has All and Mine (`m`). `j` and `k` move, Enter opens the Doc's page, where its ⋯ renames, moves, downloads, shares and deletes it. A team's deleted Docs are under a folded Deleted row, each opening in place with Restore. A machine's own Docs page is gone: its Docs are in Go to… by title, and an old link to it lands on your project's Docs.
- **A teammate's to-dos fold; an ask with its lead says so** (#2955). A teammate's approvals and to-dos on a project's issues now show on its boards and cards, folded under their name ("· 1 for Jake ›") and neutral: only the person they're for sees what's asked, and answers them. An agent session's question that the agent who started it answers first now says so: its card's state is "waits on its lead", with who has it and when it comes to you, and a thread's agents say "Waits on its lead" instead of "Needs you".

## Removed

- **Deploys are approved from Needs you** (#2950). A deploy is planned by an agent (`plan_deploy`) or with `arugula deploys plan`, and approved on its own page with your passkey, as before. Each environment's history is in Settings › Project › Secrets and environments.
