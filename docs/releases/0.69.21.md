## Added

- **Chat on the Dock badge** (#2433). The count follows the Chat button's, across every project: mentions, DMs, and rooms you set to every message. A muted room adds only its mentions. Threads on issues and runs stay on their lists and the inbox, so they don't add to it. With everything read and nothing waiting on you, there's no badge.

## Changed

- **A row waiting on a person says who** (#2446). The words show on the agent's chip that holds the row, or as a mark of their own, and in the Steps view and `board_read`. A row the board flagged as stalled on its own still says stalled.

## Fixed

- **Sharing a team Doc from its page stores the link** (#2453). The page sealed the Doc and then sent it to an address only control answers, so a link made from the page never got its content. The page now puts the sealed parts through the platform, which checks that you may edit the Doc and passes them on. They are sealed in your browser, so neither the platform's relay nor control sees the Doc in the clear, and control still never has the key.
