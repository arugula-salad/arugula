# Arugula 0.55.1

## Control

- **Fewer database round trips on the platform:** listing chat rooms and
  listing grants each take a fixed few queries, however many rooms or
  machines you have (chat's list of 40 rooms took 83).
- **`Server-Timing` on every platform answer:** where a request's time
  went (database, hub calls, the platform, the gateway), as durations and
  counts only. `ARUGULA_DEBUG_HTTP=1 arugula grants` prints it.
- A change the hub just made is waited for at most 250 ms before the
  platform answers from what it knows.
