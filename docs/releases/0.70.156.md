## Fixed

- **The panel's terminal fits its pane** (#2998). In the agent panel the terminal fits its pane, its program redrawn to that size; the wheel scrolls it anywhere over its text, and neither the page nor the tab row scrolls.
- **Files dropped on a thread reply attach to it** (#2996). Dropping or pasting an image on a thread's reply box (in a board's channel, the agent panel or Chat) attaches it to that reply, and the agent panel's composer takes files too.
- **Short refs in the issues list** (#3001). The issues list and a board's item picker show a GitHub ref short, as cloud#310, on one line with the full ref in a tooltip, so it no longer runs under the title.
- **A Doc's agent panel opens its chief of staff** (#2999). On a Doc your chief of staff wrote, the agent panel used to open the session
that wrote it. If the chief of staff had been restarted since, that tab
stayed empty. Clicking the Chief of staff tab did nothing. Now the panel
opens the Chief of staff tab, which follows whichever session is your
chief of staff now. On a Doc another agent wrote, the panel finds that
agent's session by its conversation's title too, and you can switch
between its tab and the Chief of staff's. As on a board, the chief of
staff's tab is ✦, ahead of the page's own tab.

The chief of staff's name no longer shows the project's id anywhere. On a
project's own pages it reads "Chief of staff": the panel's tabs and header,
"Chief of staff sees it" beside a Doc, its messages in chat, and a board's
or an issue's lead. Where projects mix, as in chat's rooms and an @mention's
line, it reads "Chief of staff · <the project's name>", or "Chief of staff"
while the project's name isn't known yet.
