## Added

- **A board on the Overview shows who's on each ticket** (#2761). An agent's chip opens that agent's tab in the talk panel, and a click anywhere else on a ticket zooms to its work. Done tickets fold into one line. A board with no lead offers to ask your chief of staff to start one.
- **The iPhone and iPad app zooms from Home into a board** (#2765). Each level has an address that the app opens: `arugula://p/PROJECT`, `/board/BOARD`, `/board/BOARD/card/ROW` and `/work/SESSION`. These are the web's own paths, so a link from a Doc opens the same level in the app. A notification about a pane opens its card's work when the pane is on a card, and one about an issue on a board opens that card. The board's thread and its lanes are buttons on the board's level. The board and work levels are early: their tickets and sessions now, with fuller views to come.
