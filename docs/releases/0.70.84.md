## Changed

- **A board's page says who leads it** (#2798). Before, a board's lead showed only on its row in the Overview. Talk opens
the board's thread, which its lead reads; Open is there when the lead's pane
is on one of your machines that the page reaches, and goes to it as the
Overview's Open in Panes does. A teammate's lead has the line and Talk. A
board no one leads says nothing of it.

## Fixed

- **Closing an agent removes a worktree with submodules** (#2795). Closing an agent block whose worktree has a submodule removes the worktree once it's merged, when nothing in it or its submodules is uncommitted or unpushed, instead of always keeping it.
