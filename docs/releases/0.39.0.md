# Arugula 0.39.0

Conversations in chat (Labs) can now span sessions and drive them (#851). A
conversation links panes on any of your machines, or on a teammate's, and
its lead agent drives them. You talk to the lead in chat. What the linked
agents do, and what they ask, comes back into the conversation, so you can
follow it and answer from the phone.

## Conversations (Labs: chat)

- **A conversation of its own** (#855): *Channels*, above the machines in
  Chat, lists your conversations, and *New conversation* starts one on the
  machine you're on. It's kept on that machine, its home, like any thread,
  and never passes through control unencrypted. Add people from its
  members list. Membership lets them reach the home machine for that
  conversation only. Pane and session threads are unchanged.
- **Links to panes, here or on another machine** (#856): *Add to
  conversation…* in a pane's menu links it as L1, L2, and so on. The link
  is a grant kept on the pane's machine, and only someone who may drive the
  pane can make it. It reaches that pane only (prompt it, wait on it, read
  its screen, answer its cards), with that person's rights. It's listed on
  the pane under *Linked conversations…*, and revoking it there cuts the
  link. Links to other machines go through control's relay, end to end
  encrypted.
- **What linked panes do shows up in the conversation** (#857): each
  turn's end, with a summary of what the agent said last and how many
  approvals it took. Permission prompts and Claude's questions become
  cards that anyone in the conversation can answer from chat or the
  phone (#859), and each card is pushed to the members. A prompt that a
  standing rule answers within a moment is counted instead of posted.
- **The lead** (#858): every conversation has a lead agent, Claude Code in
  an agent block in the home machine's *conversations* session. `@lead
  …` is its next prompt. It drives the links by name, waits on them, and
  reports back in the conversation. A link finishing its turn wakes it, a
  helper it starts becomes a link, and it picks up where it was after a
  restart. It acts with the conversation owner's rights and can reach only
  the conversation's links. Its own permission prompts are cards in the
  conversation too.
- **Members see everything linked:** the invite and *Add to
  conversation…* say so, because anyone in the conversation can ask the
  lead about any link.
- **Turn summaries need this CLI.** Claude Code's hooks run the `arugula`
  on the pane's PATH, so update it on every machine with linked panes.
- **The lead runs on your Claude Code default model.** Check its usage line
  in the *conversations* session.
