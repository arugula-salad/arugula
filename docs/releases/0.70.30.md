On the Docs pages a row is just a Doc's title and time, the facts above it say how long it is, and its outline marks the section you're reading. Signing in is all it takes: no screen asks another device to approve this browser, and recovery codes, the device reset and passkeys as devices are gone. Signed in on any browser, you can put a machine in a team or take it out, and share a session with someone who signed up without a device key. ## Changed

- **A Doc's outline says where you are** (#2646). The bar on each Doc's row is gone: it drew the Doc's shape, which nobody could
read. A Doc's length is now in words above it, such as "8 sections · 12 min
read". The outline beside it is plain headings, without the length bars: the
section you're reading is marked as you scroll, ◧ marks a section that holds a
chart, picture or prototype, and a click on a heading still goes there.
- **Move machines and share sessions without a device key** (#2639). Moving one of your machines into a team or back to just you, and a team owner taking a member's machine out, no longer needs a signature from one of your devices: Arugula cloud takes the move from you on its page (aru-141), and the machine takes it as Arugula cloud says. Machines from before this release only take a move a device signed, so Arugula cloud asks you to update them first. Sharing a session now finds people who have no device key too: they reach it with Arugula cloud's machine tokens, with no root device to pin or check.
- **Boards is a list, with the board beside it** (#2649). Each board is a row in the list on the left: its title, how far along it is (`13/18`) and a small bar in the lanes' colours. Boards from Plane and Done (folded) are groups of their own, and the search filters the rows.

The board you pick opens in the middle and the list stays. A board's link still opens it, now with its row selected. **New board** sits beside the title. **Not on a board** is a link over the list: its issues fill the middle, and you can drag one onto a board's row. On a phone the list is the page, and a board opens in its place.

## Removed

- **No device approvals, recovery codes or device reset** (#2642). Since aru-141 a browser reaches your machines with short-lived tokens from Arugula cloud, so the screens that made a browser one of your devices are gone:

- **Approve this browser**, its code and the waiting screen, *New device?* on your other devices, and the screens for a browser that was turned down, lost its key or stopped being trusted. A browser your account stopped trusting works on with tokens.
- **Recovery codes**: none are made, shown or asked for.
- **Reset your devices** and its warning. The passkey reset stays, under Passkeys in *Sign-in and account*.
- **Use on new computers** for a passkey: signing in with a passkey signs you in, and no browser takes device keys from it.
- **Add a phone or browser** and the browsers in *Devices and machines*, which is now *Machines*: your machines, with Remove (on Arugula cloud's word when this browser has no device key), hosted machines and runs on cloud workspaces.

A browser that is already one of your devices keeps its key, and still approves `arugula login` and the phone app. A browser without one says how to sign the command line in instead: `arugula login --browser`, and `arugula login` says so too.
