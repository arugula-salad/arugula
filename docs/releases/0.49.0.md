# Arugula 0.49.0

Chat moves into control (#978, S41, M121–M124), and agents leave it.
Control now holds a team's chat in plain text, so it works with every
machine asleep. It's behind control's own `chat` flag, which is off by
default: nothing changes for anyone until an account or a team turns it
on. Also new: chat blocks, a plain conversation with Claude (#1102).

**Breaking:** agents are out of chat. `@agent`, `@claude` and `@lead` no
longer reach anyone, conversations have no lead and link no panes, and the
`arugula` MCP server no longer has `read_thread` or `post_thread`. See
below.

## Chat in control (behind control's `chat` flag)

- **Team channels and DMs.** The Chat page on control's page lists each
  team's *Channels* (owners and editors make them with +) and *Direct
  messages* (+ picks a teammate). Someone with no machine at all can use
  it. Messages arrive live, can be edited and deleted, and carry unread
  counts, a New line, `@` mentions with autocomplete, and pushes to your
  phone ("jake mentioned you in #general", "jake wrote to you"); a tap
  opens the message. Mute a room to stop its pushes.
- **Threads on panes and sessions live in control too.** *Thread*, *Quote
  selection in thread*, *Session thread* and the pane's badge open the
  pane's or session's room in control, listed on the Chat page by machine
  and readable while the machine sleeps. Who reads one comes from the
  machine: the people it shares the pane or session with, from when they
  were shared, and its owner always.
- **Quote a pane.** The composer picks a pane on a machine that's up and
  lets you trim its lines; only those lines go to control. A quote's chip
  opens the pane only for someone who may reach its machine, and says when
  the machine is asleep. Terminal content itself stays end-to-end
  encrypted.
- **Viewers are read-only.** Someone watching a session reads its threads
  but can't post in them until its owner lets them.
- **Search** across rooms, with `in:#channel`, `from:@login` and
  `has:quote`. *Activity* lists messages that mention you.
- **Old threads come in on upgrade.** A machine on 0.49.0 in control with
  the flag on brings its pane and session threads in once, with their
  numbers, authors and quotes, and moves its `threads/` aside to
  `threads.imported/`. Its old conversations become read-only **archives**
  for the people who were in them. A machine on an older version is marked
  *Update to bring its threads into chat* under Devices and machines.
- With the flag off, or on a page a machine serves itself, chat is the
  machine's own, as before, until M125 removes it.

## Agents are out of chat (breaking)

- Threads and conversations are people's. No agent reads or posts in
  them, and `@agent`, `@claude` and `@lead` are plain text.
- Gone: conversation leads, links between a conversation and panes (with
  their grants, events and question cards), and the `@agent` follow-ups
  to a pane's agent.
- **MCP:** `read_thread` and `post_thread` are removed, and so is `list`'s
  `links` kind; an agent or script that used them has to stop. Messages an agent posted before read as the text they were kept
  with.
- Agents may come back into chat through the grants of #959 and #1002.

## Chat blocks

- A new block, **chat**: a conversation with Claude, with no tools, repo
  or shell. It runs Claude Code with tools, settings and MCP servers off,
  on the machine's existing login, in a folder the daemon owns. Open one
  from *+ Pane → Chat*, the pane menu's *Start a chat*, the phone sheet's
  *New chat*, `arugula agent --chat`, or MCP's `start_agent {chat: true}`.
  It has a model picker and survives restarts. Codex isn't offered.

## For whoever runs control

- Deploy control before turning the flag on; a 0.49.0 daemon against an
  older control logs the refused import once and tries again later.
- Control's database gains the chat tables (rooms, members, messages, an
  FTS5 index, read marks, reader lists) on start, and `migrate()` adds two
  columns to them. Deleting an account takes its DMs, its machines'
  threads and its archives, and blanks its messages elsewhere.
- The `chat` flag is set per account or per team (`POST /api/flags`).
  Leave it off until the privacy notice for chat is published.
- New limits: 8 KB per message, 60 posts a minute per account, 1 GB of
  chat per team or machine, 120 imports an hour per machine.

## For contributors

- `DEV_CONTROL_CHAT=1 just dev-control` makes team *Acme*, turns its
  `chat` flag on and seeds *#general* and a DM; `DEV_CONTROL_LOGINS` adds
  members, each with a device that approves their browsers.
- `web/e2e/chat-control.spec.ts` runs the chat flow on a local control.
