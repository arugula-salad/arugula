# Arugula 0.60.15

Made as a deploy.

## New

- **The phone's menu is three tabs** (#1458). The ☰ sheet was one long
  scroll; it now opens on **Here**: what needs you, New tab, New agent,
  Chat and Conversations, then this machine's sessions and tabs. A tab
  bar at the bottom of the open sheet, where your thumb is, switches to
  **Machines** (one line each, with yours first, then each team's, Cloud
  workspaces folded, and a filter once you have more than eight) and
  **More** (this pane, what you can open, and you: notifications,
  account, Getting started). Written on Arugula's own Cloud workspaces.
- **Chat's count is only what chat lists** (#1457). The Chat button no
  longer counts the threads on issues and runs, which chat never shows.
  Those show where they live instead: a dot and count on an issue's or a
  run's row, the project's total on its Issues and Runs places, and a
  **New in threads** group in the inbox. Opening the issue or run, or
  **Done** there, reads them.
