## Changed

- **One search field, with the / key** (#2620). Boards' filter, a board's find bar, Issues, Not on a board, the sessions sidebar, the phone's machine filter and chat's search are now one field. Only a page's main search answers /: on Boards that's the boards' filter, not the one over Not on a board.
- **Checkboxes and radio buttons drawn by Arugula** (#2619). They're 16px on a page and 20px on a phone, and show the same focus halo as a text box when you tab to them. Switches in Settings are unchanged.
