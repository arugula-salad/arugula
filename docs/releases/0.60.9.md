# Arugula 0.60.9

Made as a deploy, like every release since 0.60.6.

## New

- **A project's deploy history** (#1426). Each environment on a project's
  Environments settings page lists its latest deploys, with their state,
  commit and when they last moved, each linking to its page; **Older**
  loads more. `arugula deploys ls [--env NAME]` lists them from a
  terminal, and `GET /api/projects/{project}/deploys` serves both. Before
  this, a deploy that was done was reachable only from its push or a
  link.

## Platform step 8, off until configured

These ship dormant. Each stays off until the platform or control is
given its configuration.

- **Account links by GitHub id** (#1410, M229).
- **The Claude credential broker moves to the platform** (#1406, M231):
  off without `ARUGULA_INFERENCE_*`.
- **hud and studio become clients** of the hub's issuer (#1413 M237,
  #1415 M236): off without the issuer key (0.60.7) and their client rows.
- **Planter's accounts and repos are absorbed, and the planter driver
  arrives** (#1414 M232, #1417 M233): off without `PLANTER_PROJECTS`.
- **redone is absorbed** (#1418, M235).

They add platform migrations 0032–0037, all additive.
