# Arugula app 0.32.1

- **"Finish in your browser" opens your browser** (#1841). Adding or
  removing a passkey, linking or unlinking GitHub, signing out everywhere
  and deleting your account opened control's account page as a tab in the
  app, where they can't finish. They now open in your browser, and the
  app picks up the change when you come back.
