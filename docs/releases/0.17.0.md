# illogical 0.17.0

## New

- **It says when there's a newer release** (#176). At most twice a day the
  daemon asks GitHub which release is the latest (nothing else is sent),
  and the top bar shows the command that updates this install: install.sh,
  Homebrew, or the desktop app. `--no-update-check` turns it off.
- **The desktop app updates an older daemon** (#176). When the daemon
  running as the service is older than the one the app carries, the app
  puts its own in its place, keeping the daemon's flags and your panes.
- **Delete your account** on control's page (#173): *Sign-in and
  account…* lists where you're signed in (sign one out, or everywhere) and
  your passkeys (remove one, never the last way in), and *Delete account…*
  removes the account with its devices, machines, sessions, passkeys,
  GitHub link, push subscriptions and team requests, after you type your
  login. A team you founded goes with it. Expired sessions are pruned.
- **Hosted control, ready for more people** (#174): 1 GB, an idle relay
  connection costs a fifth of what it did, per-account relay limits
  (connections, and a daily traffic budget while billing is off), and a
  continuous off-site backup with Litestream (to Cloudflare R2), restored
  automatically onto an empty volume.
