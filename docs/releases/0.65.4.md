## New

### Live presence

Behind the `live-presence` Labs flag, off for everyone until turned on (for an account or a team, in control's Labs). Arugula #341–#347.

- **One switch** (#341, #1815). A camera button beside the account button, on control's page and the platform page, and *Your presence* in the account menu, choose **Off** (the default), **Watching** or **Live**. Off and Watching are kept on your account (platform migration **0070**), so every page and window follows. Live is Watching plus this window's camera: it's the window's own, ends when the page reloads, and the phone can be Live too.
- **Live faces where people are** (#344, #1817). While someone's camera is on, their avatar becomes their live character in chat's messages, the members chip, rooms and DMs, threads and issues, and in a session's people. At these sizes it's always their character, even for someone who chose an outline. Faces run only while they're on screen. An agent's avatar stays a picture.
- **One connection per page** (#343, #1816). With Watching or Live, a page is in every one of your teams' rooms on arugula.stream. Live's camera goes to one room, the team of the project on screen, and stops while the window is hidden.
- **Chat's page lets it in** (#342, #1814). With an arugula.stream app, the platform page's CSP lets arugula.stream's scripts, `blob:` workers, WebAssembly and its sockets in. Without one it's unchanged.

## Changed

- **People blocks share the page's connection** (#346, #1819). The block holds its team's room on the same connection live presence uses, so you're one member in it, not two. It joins even with the switch Off. While joining it says so. A refused room (control's reason) or one out of reach says so, with **Try again**, instead of the block going blank (as it could on 0.65.3 until the app restarted).
- **Who else sees it** (#347, #1818): with Watching or Live, arugula.stream learns you're around (your account, roster name and teams) whenever a page of yours is open, and with Live your character's movements, never a picture. `docs/labs.md` describes the switch.
- **arugula.stream v0.5.0** (arugula-salad/presence#13, Arugula #345): one person on several pages is one member, elements can draw from a room already connected, and small faces can be drawn as the character. It loads at runtime, so it's already live.
- **CI** (#1810, #1820): chant-ci-green's tick writes its records, and e2e's flag lists include `live-presence`. Nothing changes for people.

## Known

- In a People block, a voice participant's video doesn't reach the tiles (#355): the track is never attached, though their tile says their video is on. Voice, characters and outlines work. Under investigation.

## Note

- The platform gains **0070_live_presence** (`chat_presence.live`, `off` or `watching`; additive, sqlite and postgres). An older platform ignores it.
- A self-hosted control with an arugula.stream app opens the platform page's CSP to the relay too (`docs/control-ops.md`).
