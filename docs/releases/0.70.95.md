# Arugula 0.70.95

Joining Arugula cloud no longer asks about remote typing: your own machine takes your typing from your phone, and a team's machine, joined or moved there, is watch-only until you turn it on. A Claude Code conversation still running in another terminal asks "Running in iTerm. Move here?": Move here stops it there and continues it in a pane. On a machine with no account, a pane that waited 5 minutes or more while you were away offers, when you're back, to sign in and open Arugula on your phone, where you can answer and type. Ask your chief of staff to get an agent on an issue, or to close some sessions, and you get a card with Approve and Deny in your staff room, under Needs you and on your phone.

## Added

- **Move here** (#2827). In Claude Code conversations, one that's running in a terminal outside Arugula on this machine now offers Move here instead of a greyed-out Continue here. It asks Claude Code there to stop (as Ctrl-C would, then a polite terminate; never a kill), waits for it to let go, then continues the conversation here with its history. If it won't stop, or isn't yours, it stays running and you're asked to quit it there. Fork here (it was Fork) still starts a new session from it without stopping anything.
- **Get this on your phone, when a prompt waited for you** (#2829). It's said once a day at most, never on a machine that's joined, and instead of the *Notify this device* tip there. Sign in is Getting started's one-click join; once it's done, a QR code opens the app on your phone. Long tips at the bottom of the window now wrap at a phone's width instead of squeezing.
- **Approve your chief of staff's asks on a card** (#2823). The chief of staff never starts or stops a session itself, and nothing said in chat approves one. It asks with `ask_approval`, a tool every agent session now has. **Approve** does it as you: **Agent on this** on the issue, or each session's **Stop**. Either answer wakes it through a new event, `ask.answered`. For now a card starts sessions only on native issues, on the project's Cloud workspace. See [the chief of staff](../chief-of-staff.md#approval-cards). Needs the next server release.

## Changed

- **Type into your own machine from your phone** (#2826). `arugulad sharing remote-input off` (or `on`), run on the machine, still changes it, and `arugulad join --remote-input on|off` picks either way as it joins. A machine whose owner already said keeps what it has.
