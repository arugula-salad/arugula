## Fixed

- **Start an agent… is on the pane menu again** (#2809). A pane's right-click menu offers Start an agent… on its own row again, beside Split right and Split down, instead of only under Open beside.
- **Machines: a busy session's card shows its first tabs** (#2810). A tab with a pane that needs you, or the pane you had open last, stays in
view too. "+N more tabs" opens the rest in the card, and Show fewer folds
them again.
