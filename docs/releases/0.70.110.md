# Arugula 0.70.110

start_agent with a board starts its lead or an issue agent on another of your machines, under the board's grant, checked by control and by that machine. Messages and watch alerts that reach an idle agent block at about the same time are now one prompt, so a chief of staff back online wakes once, not once per message.

## Added

- **A lead starts agents on your other machines** (#2862). `start_agent {board, issue?, to?}` asks control for a start ticket within the board's grant, sends it over the machine-to-machine channel, and the target machine checks it, opens the agent in a worktree of its own clone, and attaches it to the issue's row or makes it the board's lead (aru-143). The grant itself comes with "Run this board"; until then control refuses every ticket.

## Changed

- **An idle agent wakes once for what comes together** (#2874). An agent block that isn't in a turn waits about a second after a chat message or a watch's alert reaches it, for others coming too, then takes everything that came as one prompt. When your machine comes back from being offline or asleep, control sends everything it kept for it at once, so your chief of staff wakes once for all of it and catches up from where it left off. A block in the middle of a turn queues what comes for it as before.
