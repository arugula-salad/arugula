## Changed

- **Machines: one page of machines, sessions and panes** (#2790). The Panes list and the grid of machines are one page now. Each machine is a section: whether it's online, whose it is and how many of the project's sessions it has. Each session is a card with its panes, grouped by tab when it has more than one. Your machines come before teammates' and online before offline; machines with nothing of the project fold into one line at the bottom. Agents only stays; the machine filter is gone. The old Panes address opens Machines.
- **One rail button per place** (#2789). The Plan, Build and Talk spaces are gone: each place has its own button on the rail, and the bar shows the place's views beside the path. The Overview's views are Overview and Swarm. Machines' views are Machines (every pane on every machine), Runs and, on hosted Arugula, Workspaces. Boards, Docs and Chat have no views. Machines stays lit on a machine's page, its panes, Runs and Workspaces.

Counts follow their place: what's new in the issues' threads is on Boards, the runs' threads on Machines, and chat's unread on Chat.

A machine's own page (control's and a daemon's) has the same rail. Overview, Boards, Docs, Chat and the Inbox open the app's, and Machines is the page itself.

On a phone the bottom bar has the same five places. The Inbox is a bell with its count in the top bar, Settings stays under your picture, and the Overview has no Swarm view.
