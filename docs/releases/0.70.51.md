# Arugula 0.70.51

## Fixed

- **Tab switches over the relay stay quick** (#2700). On a direct link nothing changes: every pane keeps streaming, so a switch sends nothing. Through control's relay, or once a link is measured slow (the page's lag probe waits behind its output for over a second), a hidden tab's terminals are detached and come back when it's shown: what they printed meanwhile if that's no bigger than the screen, else the screen, with a rule marking the skipped output. Attention (a bell, a question, an agent that's done) is read by the daemon, so it still reaches every screen and your phone while a tab is hidden.
