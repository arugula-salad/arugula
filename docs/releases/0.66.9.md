# Arugula 0.66.9

The panes page comes up faster on a machine with many terminals: the tab on screen no longer waits behind the others, and each terminal costs far less to bring up. A Plane epic can now be a board that follows it. Update every machine.

## Changed

- **The tab on screen loads first** (#1999). Opening the panes page asked for every terminal on the machine at once, and the pane you were looking at waited its turn. Now the tab on screen comes at once, the others follow one at a time when the page is idle, and a tab you open before its turn loads right away. Their history is written in pieces, so the page keeps drawing. Terminals in other tabs finish loading a little later than they did.
- **Plane epics become boards** (#1996, #483; #1998, #484). Each open epic gets a board with its children as rows. The board follows the epic: new, moved and deleted children, its title, and closing or reopening when the epic does. Its header says "From Plane" and links to the epic, the Boards page lists these boards in a group of their own, and Settings, Plane has an "Epics become boards" switch on each connection, on by default. A row that follows Plane can't be removed by hand: change its parent in Plane.

## Fixed

- **Terminals no longer reset their theme once per color** (#2000). Every terminal's first screen sets all 256 colors, and each one reset the theme even when nothing changed. That was most of the time a terminal took to come up, whatever its size.

## Note

- Control runs migration 0086 (additive).
- A person who reopens a completed epic's board sees it closed again at that epic's next sync.
