## Changed

- **Your machines page's settings dialogs open Settings** (#2787). On your machines page, the account menu no longer opens Teams…, Machines… or Sign-in and account…: its Settings row opens the app's Settings, and Shared Docs… opens My Docs' Shared by you. A machine's menu has one Settings… row, opening Settings › Machines at that machine, in place of Access…, Permission rules…, Agents' grants… and the rest. Old links to those dialogs, and to the old Settings sections, land on the right place in the app.

A team's chat switch is now in Settings › Team and billing › Chat, beside its owners-only switches, for every member to read and its owners to change.

A few things still open your machines page, until it and the app share one address: making or removing a team's owner, turning notifications on for this device (and its test), and adding or removing a passkey or linking GitHub. A machine's own page, with no app, keeps its Settings as before.

## Fixed

- **Overview's page no longer scrolls under the talk panel** (#2786). The talk panel reached to within 12px of the window's foot, but the page keeps
more than that under the Overview, so with Needs you quiet (nothing short
enough to fill the page) the page grew and scrolled to make room. The panel now
stops where the page's own padding begins.
