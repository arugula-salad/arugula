# Arugula 0.70.28

Settings is one page with four tabs (You, This machine, Project, Team and billing), each a list of sections saying what's set, with the one you pick beside it. A browser signed in to Arugula cloud shows a machine's port and editor blocks without being one of your devices: the hub signs each block's frame a grant for that block alone. Your first browser, the desktop app's window and any other new browser reach your machines with tokens from Arugula cloud; none is made one of your devices any more. Settings' quiet hours draw the week you've set, a bar a day, with a night running into the next morning; different times on some days wait behind a link. A project's settings are 11 rows in four groups (Basics, Work, Agents and runs, Spend) instead of 17, with related settings together in one row.

## Added

- **Fields in the design reference** (#2635). The design reference (`/design`) shows the form kit by its real pieces:
empty, filled, focused, invalid and disabled, and on, off, partly on and
some-of-a-list where they apply. The design language says when to use a
switch, a box, a dot, segments or weekday chips. A radio in a group with
nothing picked yet now draws off, not on.

## Changed

- **Instant settings are switches, instant filters segments** (#2633). Now switches: team chat, Lend this device, Preview web links, Developer settings' flags, Edit in nvim, Usage reports, and the people CI alerts mention. Claude Code conversations' "Open now" and "All" are switches. Chat search picks "This team" or "All teams", and the workspace graph picks "Source" or "Live" with segments.
- **Settings: one page for you, a machine, a project and a team** (#2606). Each section in the list says what's set in a few words, such as "Weeknights 10 PM – 8 AM" or "acme/shop", so you can see what's on without opening it. Pick one and its controls open beside the list, with a line under the heading saying what it's for; the More toggles are gone. A picker under the tabs chooses which machine, project or team. On a phone you see the list first, and a section opens full width with a way back.

Every section has its own address, such as `/settings/you/quiet-hours` or `/settings/project/<project>/repos`, so a link opens it. Old addresses still work: a project's settings (`/p/<project>/settings`), Personal's (`/settings/personal`) and a team's (`/t/<team>/settings`) open the matching tab. The rail's Settings opens the project you're in, and "Your account…" under your picture opens You. This machine links to the machine's own page for now.
- **No new browser becomes a device** (#2628). Since aru-141, a browser that would have waited for another device's approval reached your machines with short-lived machine tokens instead. Now no browser becomes a device at all: not your account's first (which used to make itself the account's root and show recovery codes), not the desktop app's window after a browser allows its sign-in, and not one opened with a machine's join link (it adds the machine in one click). A browser that is already one of your devices keeps its key. Approving a command line (`arugula login`) or the phone app still needs a device: `arugula login --browser` signs the CLI in from any browser. A control that signs no tokens (a dev or test hub), or `localStorage["arugula.machine-tokens"] = "off"`, enrolls a new browser as before.
- **Settings: a team's machines, and its roster in place** (#2622). Machines lists each machine with whether it's online. Yours opens its settings; someone else's says whose it is. Members still lists who's in the team and their role. Inviting people, removing them and changing roles are signed by an owner's device, so a ↗ row there opens your machines page. Sharing a machine with a team works the same way. Labs is a ↗ row to your machines page too, until the app can reach those settings.

The sections are in two groups, the team's own and Every project. General is now called Default project.
- **Quiet hours draw your week** (#2634). Pick the nights and one window, and the bars under them show when you're quiet as you edit, before you save. *Different times on some days…* adds a row of nights with hours of their own (sleeping in on weekends, say), and *Same times every night* folds them back into one. The list says nights in a row as a range, such as "Mon–Sat, times vary", and the time fields are wide enough for "10:00 PM" now.
- **A project's settings in four groups** (#2625). A project's settings in Settings now come in four groups: Basics (General, Access, and the team's or your defaults), Work (Repos and trackers, Issues and boards, CI alerts), Agents and runs (Runs and workspaces, Agents, Secrets and environments) and Spend (Claude spend, Apps from products).

Settings that belong together share a row. Linked repos and Plane are under Repos and trackers. Where new issues go and Boards are under Issues and boards. The standing grant for runs, workspace size and finish step are under Runs and workspaces. The agent model and agent tokens are under Agents. Environments and project secrets are under Secrets and environments. Each row's line puts together what's set in each of its parts, and each part keeps its own heading when you open the row. Overrides is now called Team defaults (Your defaults for a personal project). Old links to a section that moved, such as `/settings/project/<project>/environments`, open the row it's in now.
- **Teams work without a device key** (#2630). A team's members, roles, invite links and lock are Arugula cloud's now (aru-141): a change no longer needs a signature from one of the owner's devices, so someone who signed in on a new browser, or signed up without a device key, runs their team like anyone else. Making someone an owner, or removing one, still takes the owner's passkey. Team machines take these changes as Arugula cloud says, and a machine token's role follows them; machines from before this release stop at the first change made this way, so update them.

## Fixed

- **Notifications reach browsers signed in without a device key** (#2631). A browser that reaches your machines by signing in, with no approval from another device, now gets its panes' notifications, once Arugula cloud has the matching update.
- **Port and editor blocks show without a device key** (#2629). A browser signed in to Arugula cloud shows a machine's port and editor blocks without being one of your devices: the hub signs each block's frame a grant for that block alone.
