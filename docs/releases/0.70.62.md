## Added

- **Pin sessions** (#2739). Pins are yours alone, kept with your account, so a teammate's view of a shared session never changes and your phone shows the same ones. A pinned session that needs you moves up to Needs you, and comes back to Pinned once it's answered. A machine that isn't joined to an account has no pins.
- **Agents watch a linked repo's releases** (#2740). `watch` with `event: "github.release.published"` and `match` naming the `repo` (and, if it likes, the `tag`, `prerelease` or `by`) fires once when a release, a published draft's included, comes in through Arugula's GitHub App. The event carries the release's tag, whether it's a pre-release and who published it; never its name or notes.
