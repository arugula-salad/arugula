# Arugula 0.66.8

The size of a tab you share between screens moves only when you ask: a tap on your phone or a mouse over your desktop no longer takes it. Clicking a pane you weren't using keeps your place and does what you clicked. Boards put what's in motion first, and a group folds away with a click. Update every machine.

## Changed

- **Only "Use this window's size" takes a tab's size from another screen** (#1980, #473). While another of your screens is connected and holds a tab's size, nothing but the button moves it. That includes typing, a tap on the phone, mouse movement over a full-screen program like Claude Code, switching tabs and zooming. When no connected screen holds it, the first screen to show the tab takes it, as before, and the holder's own resizes and rotations still count. A screen that doesn't hold the size can still open any pane: picking one ends the holder's focus, and the size stays where it is (#439). This applies between your own windows too, not only between people.
- **Boards: Doing, Review, Next, Done** (#1982, #479). Lanes come in that order on the board page, the board block, the phone and the swarm's board view, with what's in motion first. Rows no longer carry the issue's open/closed pill, since the lane says it; the row's drawer still does.
- **Fold a board's groups** (#1984, #480). Click a group's heading, such as "UX", to fold everything under it. It then reads "UX · 12". On a board without groups, the lane headings fold the same way. Folds are kept per board, in your browser, and nobody else sees them.
- **Plane items read with their module, labels and type** (#1990, #475). `forge_read` `items` and `issue` answer a Plane item's `type`, `labels` and `modules` by name. Modules are refreshed on each sync (every 5 minutes), and a write that puts an item in a module shows it at once. Items mirrored before this get their labels when they next change.

## Fixed

- **Clicking a pane you weren't using keeps its place** (#1979, #472). A board, or any other block, no longer jumps to the top when a click makes its pane the active one, and the click opens what you clicked instead of only activating the pane. A terminal you click still gets the keyboard.
- **Forge latency counts only submitted reviews** (#1978). A dismissed or edited review no longer adds a days-long "late delivery" sample to the webhook timing.

## Note

- Control runs migration 0085 (`forge_items.modules`, additive).
- Older clients still send `typed` with a view. The daemon ignores it now that typing doesn't claim a tab's size.
