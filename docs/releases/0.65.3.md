# Arugula 0.65.3

People blocks get arugula.stream's noise cancellation: in voice and calls, Krisp now runs on control's page.

## Changed

- **Noise cancellation in People blocks** (#1811). arugula.stream 0.4.0's voice and call bars have a **Noise** choice: Krisp (LiveKit Cloud's noise cancellation, the default), Standard (the browser's own suppression) or Off, switched without rejoining and kept in the browser. Krisp makes its audio worklet and worker from `blob:` URLs, which control's page didn't allow, so it fell back to Standard; with an arugula.stream app set, control's `script-src` now allows `blob:`. Without an app the policy doesn't change.
- **The desktop app's camera** (#1807) shipped in app 0.31.1: on macOS a People block's camera works in the app, and only Arugula's own pages get the camera and microphone.
- **CI** (#1802, #1803, #1804, #1808): chant 0.124.52 builds the newest commit; ci-red says at once when main's newest decided commit is red after its retry and names the bisect's culprit; doctests and control-smoke move to test-2, and testnet and desktop-xvfb start only on changes. Nothing changes for people.

## Note

- No migrations.
- A self-hosted control with an arugula.stream app gets `blob:` in its `script-src` (`docs/control-ops.md`).
