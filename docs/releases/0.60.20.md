# Arugula 0.60.20

Made as a deploy.

Chat catches up with Slack and Discord (#103, PR #1484), and team chat opens as a block beside your terminals.

## New

### Messages

- **Markdown** (#109): lists, quotes, tables, headings and code blocks in every room. Code blocks have colours and a copy button.
- **Emoji** (#105): `:joy:`-style shortcodes, `:` autocomplete and a picker. A message of only emoji draws large.
- **Reactions** (#104): react from a message's toolbar, or with a long-press on a phone. Chips show who reacted.
- **Reply threads** (#106): a thread opens beside the room (full screen on a phone), and a reply can also go to the channel. A Threads view lists the threads you follow.
- **Files and images** (#107): paste, drop or attach them, up to 10 MB each and 256 MB per team. Images show inline and open full size. Files are served only to people who can read the room.
- **Link previews** (#108): links to Arugula issues, runs, deploys and workspaces become live cards for those who can see them. Web links get a preview that control fetches, never your browser, and control won't fetch private addresses.
- **The hover toolbar** (#121): quick reactions, reply in thread and save, and a ⋯ menu with the rest. ↑ edits your last message, "↓ N new messages" brings you back down, and there's a compact layout.

### People

- **Avatars** (#112): GitHub pictures, served through control.
- **Presence** (#110): dots for active, away or offline. You can set yourself away or appear offline.
- **Typing** (#111): "alecraso is typing…".

### Notifications

- **Levels per room** (#113): all, mentions or nothing. DMs default to all and channels to mentions.
- **Do not disturb** (#115): snooze, or set quiet hours by weekday in your time zone. Others see a 🌙. A DM can "Notify anyway".
- **`@channel`, `@here` and keywords** (#114): `@here` reaches only people who are active now. A team can keep `@channel` and `@here` to its owners.
- **Mark as unread and saved for later** (#116), with optional reminders.

### Rooms

- **Private channels, a channel browser, join and leave** (#118). Existing channels stay public, with everyone in them.
- **Group DMs** (#117), with up to 8 people.
- **A topic you set, and pinned messages** (#119).
- **Starred rooms and your own sidebar sections** (#120), the same on every device.
- **The agents chip** (#122): one header chip lists whose agents can read or post, in place of the full-width lines, and a new grant says so once.

### In the multiplexer

- **Team chat as a block** (#123): open it from the block menu, with `arugula chat`, or with MCP's `show {kind: "chat"}`. It can sit beside a terminal, be pinned to one room, and quote a neighbouring pane in one click. Its tab shows unread.

### For agents

- **MCP `post_thread` and `read_thread`:** agents can react, attach files, pin, and reply in a thread, all within their grants. `read_thread` shows reactions, attachments, topics and pins.

## Fixed

- Menus and prompts opened from phone chat no longer sit under it.
- **Runs on hosted images built on loam start again** (#1470). The image now installs nftables, so a run's firewall loads instead of failing closed.

## Note

- Migration 0048 (channel members) isn't additive, so a rollback past this release is refused.
