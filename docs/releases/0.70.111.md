## Added

- **An issue agent's tab on the Overview, and every agent's rung** (#2867). An agent working on an issue opens, from its chip on a board's ticket or as the first tab at the ticket's own level, as that issue's thread above its last lines; a post in the thread prompts the agent leading it, and when another session leads the thread, or none does, the tab says so and keeps a box to write to the agent directly. Under every agent tab's head, which says where it runs and whether it's awake, a line gives its rung ("lead of Launch", "on #12 for Launch", or the chief of staff's project) and who started it, and the next its parent's newest post to it: the board lead's in the issue's thread, the chief of staff's in a lead's board threads. At a ticket's level the issue agent's panes come before its helpers'. An agent a Claude Code terminal starts through MCP now records that terminal's pane, so it can be named as the starter.

## Changed

- **Your chief of staff moves when you start it somewhere else** (#2871). There's one chief of staff per person per project. Starting one with `start_agent` and `recipe: chief-of-staff` where another already leads your staff room moves it: the new one takes the lead, and the old one stops. One on another machine is told in your staff room to stand down; it posts its place in the event log there and closes its block, and the new one carries on from that place (or starts from now and says so until it arrives). A hosted one is stopped, and the new one starts from now and says so. One on the same machine is closed, and the new one carries on from its notes. A chief of staff's block is now titled "Chief of staff · <project>", so one machine can run one for each of two projects.
