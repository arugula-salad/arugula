## Added

- **Deploys and run grants in the iPhone app** (#2831). A deploy waiting on you is first under Needs you, and its notification opens it: the project, what's deployed from where, who asked and the plan. **Approve or deny with passkey** opens control's approval in a browser sheet, where your passkey confirms it, and the app reads the deploy again when the sheet closes. The project menu's **Runs** shows its standing grant for runs, and allows, renews or revokes it the same way.

On the web, a project's settings now link straight to its run grant's dialog on your machines page (`#run-grant=`), which nothing opened since Settings moved into the app.
