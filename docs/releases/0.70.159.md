## Added

- **Tag a board's lead from any room; agents named by role** (#3018). Until now, `@<board>` tags only worked in #agents and a board's channel.
Now they also work in team channels, DMs, your chief of staff's room and
an issue's or a PR's thread. Type `@` and part of a board's name: the list
offers the boards of the room's project (in a team's channels and DMs, the
team's projects), and the post goes to that board's lead. When an agent
writes `@Design lift` in any of those rooms, it reaches the lead too.
`@dust` still means a machine's agents.

In chat, an agent now goes by what it is: a board's lead reads
"Design lift lead", the chief of staff reads "Chief of staff", and any other
agent shows its session's name. The `dust/…` prefix is gone. Hover a name
to see its machine and session. The `@` list, an agent post's receipt and
a thread's To chips use the same names.

Tagging a board outside #agents and board channels needs the server
release that comes with it. Until that release, other rooms work as they
did before.
- **A board's Steps view is back** (#3015). The switch sits in the board's title block (on a phone, a tab above the board).
Steps uses the same tiles as the lanes and the same order `board_read` gives,
and the board remembers your pick in this browser.

## Changed

- **The Overview is the chief of staff's view** (#3023). The Overview is now tabs of panes, as a board's desk is. Tab 1 is the title line over its own panes, Boards and Running. Needs you and the chief of staff's panel stay on the right, on every tab.

Your chief of staff can open a Doc, a PR, a run or #agents beside Boards and Running while you talk. Boards becomes a list and the right column narrows to a quarter. It never takes your focus or switches your tab, and if you were busy in the last 4 seconds its pane arrives quietly with a dot. Something that waits on you, such as a review or an agent's permission prompt, gets a tab of its own, and Needs you gets a row with "tab N" that opens it. A failing run can stand in place of Boards for a while, and ↺ Boards puts Boards back.

While tab 1 differs from the default, the header says "changed by chief of staff" and when, with Reset beside it. A pane you close stays closed until you Reset, and the ⋯ menu reopens what was closed. `1`–`4` pick a tab.

Cuts:
- On a phone, the Overview stays one column with no panes or tabs.
- A PR pane is a link to the PR, not the PR itself.
- **Your chief of staff puts what waits on you on the Overview** (#3024). Your chief of staff now arranges your Overview. While you talk with it, it opens the Doc or run you're discussing beside Boards and Running. Something that waits on you gets a tab of its own with a Needs you row pointing at it: a Doc ready for review, a PR to approve, or an agent that's blocked on a permission prompt while you're away. You answer the prompt in that tab, and the agent carries on. When main goes red it can show the failing run in place of Boards until it's green.

It never takes your focus or switches your tab. It leaves your own panes alone and doesn't reopen what you closed.
- **The Overview tidies itself and suggests asks** (#3025). The first time you open the Overview on a new day, it sends what your chief of staff opened before today, and you haven't touched, to the closed list. That covers its panes beside Boards and its tabs that no longer wait on you. A tab still waiting on you stays, such as an agent's permission prompt or a review you haven't answered. "↺ N closed overnight" at the end of the tab strip brings any of them back.

While nothing is open beside Boards and Running, the space below them shows what your chief of staff can put there, with a few asks to start from: "Show me what merged today", "Put the failing runs beside Boards", "Open the Docs that wait on me" and "Keep a standup Doc here each morning". Each one fills your chief of staff's message box for you to edit and send.

## Fixed

- **A renamed chief of staff still hears its room** (#3026). As a board's lead is since Arugula #1563, a session that leads a staff room or an issue's thread
(`post_thread` with `lead`) is now told of the room's posts by its Claude Code conversation, so a
rename doesn't leave them waiting unread in its machine's inbox.
- **"Who a post reaches" reads as a table again** (#3021). The panel beside #agents and a board's channel says who each kind of post
reaches. Its rows were drawn on top of one another, so the words overlapped.
Each rule now has its own row, with who posts on the left and whom it reaches
on the right, at any window width and in light and dark. A name in the list
above it that's too long to fit is cut short, and pointing at it shows it in
full.
