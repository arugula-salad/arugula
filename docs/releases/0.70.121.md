# Arugula 0.70.121

When no chief of staff leads your staff room, its tab on the Overview offers Start, with the same pick of your machines. A prompt queued behind a running agent turn now says how long that turn has run, as well as when it last showed output. A board's lead can read and close the issue agents it started on its own machine, in whatever tab they opened. Watching a machine's agents ask or go idle needs the sessions grant, not boards.read.

## Added

- **Start a chief of staff for a room with none** (#2889). A staff room can be left with no chief of staff leading it, and nothing hears what you write there. Its tab on the Overview now says so and offers **Start**, with the same pick of your online machines as the tab's other starts. Once it starts, it leads the room and the tab's head says where it runs.

## Changed

- **prompt_agent says how long the running turn has run** (#2890). When `prompt_agent` times out with your prompt queued behind a turn, the reply reads, for example, "its turn has run 12m, its last output was 9m ago", so a lead can tell a busy agent from a stuck one.

## Fixed

- **A board lead reads and closes the agents it started** (#2892). A lead that started an issue agent with `start_agent` (no `to`) was refused `read_output` and `close` on it, with "in another tab". An agent block's token now reaches the blocks it started, in any tab, as well as its own tab. What it didn't start in another tab stays out of reach.
- **Session watches ask for the sessions grant** (#2893). `watch` with `session.needs_input` or `session.idle` now takes the grant agents already have for sessions (`sessions.start`, `sessions.reply` or `threads.read`) instead of `boards.read`, and the event list says so.
