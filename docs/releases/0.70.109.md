## Added

- **Your chief of staff builds boards** (#2865). It files each issue with a "Done when" line, puts one group per phase of the Doc in its order, links an issue that blocks another only where the Doc says so, and puts the Doc in the board's Docs strip. Then it posts the board in your staff room and leaves it with no lead: it starts nothing, and you edit the board as you like. It changes a board it made only while nobody leads it, and on a board another lead runs it only adds a row you ask for.

## Changed

- **Working is yellow, quiet is grey** (#2870). A session's standing now reads the same everywhere the Overview shows it: its word in a row, a board's count (`2 working`) and a lead's dot. Working takes the yellow that quiet had, and quiet goes to the muted grey that working had.
- **Needs you takes a third, and you can resize it** (#2869). The column beside the list used to be a narrow strip with a wide empty middle. It now opens at a third of the page. Drag the divider between the list and the column (or press Left and Right on it) to make it wider or narrower, between 18rem and half the page; double-click it to go back to a third. Arugula remembers your width on this device. A phone and a narrow window keep the stacked layout, with no divider.
- **The apps open Settings in the app and show your picture** (#2866). Settings was the web's page in your browser, and Send feedback sent you there too. Now Settings opens inside the app, already signed in: the app hands its sign-in over once, and leaving Settings signs that page out again. As in the desktop app's window, adding a passkey, linking GitHub, changing your email and deleting your account stay in a browser. Send feedback sends from the sheet, and your picture replaces your initials.

## Fixed

- **An agent can always continue a Doc it opens** (#2875). Before, `doc open` handed back the block that was already open even when the agent couldn't write to it, so the next fill was refused and no one could carry on. Now the agent gets a new block beside its pane, and the reply names the other one. It also lists each section: filled, or pending with its intent.
- **A Codex block starts, or says why it didn't** (#2876). `arugula setup codex` no longer leaves out the Codex binary the adapter
runs when your machine has none, and a Codex block uses the `codex` on your
`PATH` or in `~/.local/bin` only if there is one. Before, either gap ended
in "The agent exited with code 1: throw er; // Unhandled 'error' event".
When an adapter does crash, the block's note is the error itself, such as
"Error: spawn codex ENOENT", not Node's `throw` line.
