# Arugula 0.51.0

Share a Doc with a link, like an artifact: anyone with the link can read it,
without an account, and control can't (#1064).

## Shared Docs (Labs, flag `docs`) (#1064)

- **Share from the Doc** (#1066). The Doc block's menu has *Share link…*:
  it uploads a snapshot of the Doc (its text, charts, drawings and
  pictures) and gives you a link to copy. *Update shared copy* puts up what the
  Doc says now, under the same link; *Unshare* takes it down. *Shared
  Docs…*, in control's account menu, lists every Doc you've shared, with when
  it was opened, and unshares any of them.
- **End to end.** The snapshot is encrypted on your machine. Its key lives
  only in the link, after the `#`, which browsers never send. Control
  stores and serves ciphertext it can't read.
- **The viewer** opens on any browser and phone, light or dark, from
  `docs.<your control's block domain>` (or `<control>/d/` without one). It
  runs nothing from the Doc, sets no cookies, and says only that a link is
  broken or no longer shared, never why.
- **An agent asks; you decide** (#1067). `doc share` puts a card on the
  Doc: "Share 'TITLE' with a link anyone can open?", with **Share** and
  **Not now**. Nothing is uploaded until you tap Share. The agent learns
  the outcome by asking again, and only after you've shared does it get
  the link, so it can hand it to you. A card nobody answers lapses after
  a day; it survives a restart until then.
- **Only the machine's owner shares.** Editors, viewers, a team's other
  owners and agents can't share, update, unshare or read a Doc's link, or
  answer its card, by any route.
- **Limits:** 48 MB a Doc, 200 MB and 100 shared Docs an account.

## For whoever runs control

- Deploy control with this release: it adds the `doc_links` and
  `doc_link_parts` tables and the viewer host. Extend the volume first
  (`docs/control-ops.md`, *Shared Docs*). Machines can share only once their
  control runs this release.
- Control refuses new uploads once its disk is over 80% full.
- The privacy notice (arugula.io/privacy) needs a line before the flag
  goes on for others: control keeps shared Docs encrypted and
  can't read them; it learns their size, your account and machine, when
  they were shared, updated and opened, and a rough open count.

## For contributors
