## Added

- **Take a Doc off a board** (#2945). A board's Doc tab has Take off this board in its ⋯, for whoever may change the board.

## Changed

- **All issues and Not on a board as rows** (#2946). All issues and Not on a board list each issue on one row (its state, number, title, labels and age), as the rest of Boards does.
- **A board's own asks and watched asks, as rows** (#2942). A Board block's tile draws its open ask as a Needs you row ("asks you" or "asks Jake"), with its options as buttons and a box when there are none. A question in a session you only watch now says "asks Jake", naming its machine's owner where Arugula knows them, instead of "asks an editor".
