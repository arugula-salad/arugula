## Added

- **Docs and prototypes in the iPhone and iPad app** (#2736). A Doc card in a thread opens the Doc's own page, signed in as the app: over
everything on an iPhone, and on an iPad in a third column beside the
conversation, with home's list folded away until you close it. Its prototypes
run, and a tap on a paragraph comments on it, as on the desktop. A link in it
to a board, an issue, a channel or another Doc opens in the app.

## Changed

- **The iPhone and iPad app keeps its tab bar** (#2742). Home, the conversations sorted by what needs you, is the first tab rather than
a screen in front of the tabs. Issues left the bar: a board's rows open their
issues, and the project's name at the top of Home, Boards and Chat is a menu
with the project's issues in it. A notification still opens its tab, and one
about an issue opens it beside Home.
