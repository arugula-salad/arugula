## Added

- **Agents hand you to-dos** (#2897). An agent adds a to-do with what, why and how. It's for you as the machine's owner, on the card the agent is working on, and it waits for you until you mark it Done or Can't. The agent can list its open to-dos and cancel one it no longer needs.
- **Your machines' agents ask in your Inbox** (#2900). Your machine tells control which of its panes wait on someone: what kind of ask it is, since when, whether only a person may answer, and the agent block that started it. It never sends the question itself. The app still reads that from the machine. An ask from an agent that another agent started goes to that agent first, and reaches you after ten minutes without an answer, or at once when only a person may give it (a gate, an invite, a Doc's share). A finished turn shows in your Inbox but isn't counted.
