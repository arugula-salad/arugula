# Arugula 0.70.69

Clicking a board's row on the Overview zooms to that board in place, and a ticket on it zooms to its work. Esc, the path or the level pill zooms back out. On the Overview, a board's lead chip opens that agent, where a message goes straight to it. The board's thread is the row's ↩ button again, and a line under the tabs says who you're talking to. Zoom into a card on the Overview to see every agent block and terminal working on it, live and side by side. You can type into each one, and an agent's question shows above them.

## Added

- **Zoom into a board from the Overview** (#2759). Each level has its own address, so a link, a reload and Back land on it. The talk panel keeps its tabs as you zoom, and brings the one in charge to the front: your chief of staff at the project, the board's lead at a board, the card's agent at its work. A Needs you card's title zooms to the work that's asking. The board and work views are early: their tickets and sessions, with fuller views to come. A board's chevron still opens its cards in place.
- **A card's terminals, live on the Overview** (#2760). Up to four panes sit side by side, and the rest wait as tabs. The pane whose agent you're talking to is outlined, and each pane has Talk and Open in Panes. Terminals are read from their machines through your own connection, not through Arugula cloud. A teammate's session is shown read-only. A cloud workspace's session shows its box's terminal while the box runs, or its last answer and a reply box while the box is paused. A card no agent works on says so and offers to ask its board's lead.

## Changed

- **A lead's name opens that agent** (#2758). A reply to a post in a board's thread now names the session that answered ("geek/launch lead answered"), not only its machine. A lead this page can't reach opens as where it runs, read-only.
