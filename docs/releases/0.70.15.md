## Added

- **Silence and Stop your agent from a thread** (#2569). They act on the agent's pane through your page's own connection to its machine, so nothing new goes through Arugula cloud. A mention of a silenced session shows as not delivered. On app.arugula.io, open the session on your machine to do the same.
