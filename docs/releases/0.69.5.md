## Changed

- **Steps is a chart** (#2307). The Steps view shows each row as one line: its title on the left and a bar in the column of the step it waits for, with done rows in ✓. Hover a line, or tap it on a phone, to see the arrows of its links. Drag the title column's right edge to change its width, or focus it and use the arrow keys; double-click puts it back. Each device remembers its width.
- **A long menu has a filter row** (#2304). A menu of 13 rows or more starts with a filter: type to narrow the rows, use the arrow keys and Enter over what is left, and Escape clears the text before it closes the menu. On a phone the filter doesn't take the focus, so the keyboard stays down. A board's Label and Person menus now hang under their buttons, and right-aligned to them near the window's edge.
- **Issues and Inbox show less text** (#2309). An open issue's row drops "Open" and "opened … ago by" for its number, age and
who. The two-places banner is its two counts, and the list's footer about where
new issues go is gone (the project's settings still have it). An issue's page
no longer says who opened it under the title (the first post does), an empty
description or label list says nothing, and no comments yet is "No comments
yet". In the Inbox, a card under "Reviews requested", "Changes requested",
"Checks failed" or "Runs failed" keeps only who, a thread's card reads "3 new ·
mentions you", and the Seen button is gone: opening a card marks it seen.
- **Boards shows less text** (#2310). The stats row under the goal, "Led by" and "Made by", the "Docs" label, the "working" word on an agent in Doing, "No description." in the drawer, the Steps view's "N done" line and the breadcrumb above a board's title are gone. A board's progress reads "2 of 8 done" (the full words stay in the bar's label), "not proven yet" is "no proof", and a Done row's "proven" tag and proof link are one "Proof" link. An agent chip shows the agent's name, not "agent".
- **Settings, Runs and Deploys show less text** (#2311). The lists, switches, counts and dates stay. Each column of the agents table
still says what it gives in its heading's tooltip, and the pause switch is
now "Pause everything, not just chat" (hover it for what else waits). The
empty Deploys and Runs pages say one line and offer the button.
- **Chat shows less text** (#2312). Chat shows less text: the room header drops the team name, a thread's agents show a name and state, session cards drop "agent session" and "Pushed · N commits", and editing a message drops its key hints.
- **Agent blocks show less text** (#2314). An agent block waiting on you no longer repeats "Needs you" in its bar: the
pane header's dot and word say it. A question from an agent shows as its
card alone while it waits, without a tool row above it that said the same, and without
the small caps label (the label is in the question's tooltip). Once
answered, its row stays in the transcript, as before. A running
agent says "Working…" without naming the tool, whose own row shows it.
The cost beside the context strip is just "≈ $0.01", and the conversations
list no longer says "≈ costs at API prices" (the tooltip still explains
the figure). The install strip is the Install button.
- **Docs shows less text** (#2313). A complete Doc drops its "3 of 3 sections written" line (a Doc still waiting
on sections says "2 of 3"). A project Doc's byline reads "name · time", a
copy reads "Copy from machine · time", and the "Acme's Docs" link, the
"Made from…" line, the preview's "updated · sections" facts and the machine
Docs page's "on machine" and word count are gone. The Docs list has no project
header when there is one project. In the share dialogs, the explanations the
buttons already give are gone, the link line reads "Anyone with the link can
open it", and when the link is out of date the "Shared copy is older · Update"
chip carries when it was shared.
- **Projects and Home show less text** (#2315). Projects and the project page drop lines the page already says: the team
explainer under People (owners keep a Manage team link), "you're the owner" on
a project card (still in its tooltip), "linked repos, environments" after
Project settings, "3 of 3 written" under each Doc, and the longer wording of
the runs beta and environment lines.
