## Added

- **A board's lead on its Overview row** (#2751). A lead on a teammate's machine is named with them ("Aaron's chat"). The dot is there when this page or the hub can tell how the session stands, and plain otherwise. A board with no lead shows nothing yet, and keeps its thread button. While chat is off the chip is a label.
- **Your chief of staff's room on the Overview** (#2753). Once your staff room exists, it is the talk panel's first tab, pinned, and says which session hears what you write there.
