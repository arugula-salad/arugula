# Arugula 0.70.29

Settings' You tab now holds Phones and browsers, Appearance and Preferences, and opens your machines page right at sign-in, devices or teams. Help moved to the command palette. Settings' This machine shows a machine's access, permission rules, reports, guest SSH, developer settings and name, and what its agents may do, which you now change there, one machine at a time. On Docs, Agents, Recipes and Design, ‹ or the [ key folds the list to a thin strip so the open item gets the room. Each page remembers it. Deleting a team Doc shows Undo, and deleted Docs wait 30 days in a folded Deleted group at the list's foot, where Restore brings one back. The Trash link and the Docs header's extra line are gone.

## Added

- **Lists fold to a strip** (#2640). `‹` in the list's header, or `[`, folds the list to a 34px strip with the
page's name; the strip, or `[` again, opens it. Each page remembers whether
its list is folded, as it remembers the split. ↑ and ↓ still step through
the rows while it's folded. Where the page is too narrow for the list and an
item side by side, nothing changes.

With less than about 900px for the item, what sits to its right (a Doc's
outline) now goes under it instead of squeezing the text.

## Changed

- **Settings, You: your devices and preferences in one place** (#2623). You is in three groups: Notifications, Look and feel, and Account. Preferences (Tips, Big tips, and which editor Open in editor uses) are set right there, and they follow your account to every device. Appearance is its own section, and it's the only place it appears.

Some things are signed by one of your devices: sign-in and passkeys, your devices, adding a phone or browser, teams and shared Docs. They still live on your machines page, but each has its own row in You marked ↗, and the row opens that page with the right dialog showing. Turning notifications on for a device works the same way. On your machines page, This device and All your devices are now one section, Phones and browsers. The "On your machines page" list of links is gone.

Getting started, What's new and nvim help are in the command palette under Help. They're no longer part of Settings.
- **A machine's settings, and its agents' grants, in Settings** (#2624). Pick a machine at the top of This machine. Its own settings are read from the machine itself, so a machine the app can't reach, or one that's offline, says so in each section's summary. What its agents may do on Arugula cloud is one section per machine, with a switch per project and for your DMs. It replaces both *Agents on your machines* under You and the read-only *Agents' grants…* in the machine's menu, which now opens this section. An old link to `/settings#agents` lands here.
- **The last fields with a look of their own take the kit's** (#2636). A new style that draws its own field (a border, fill, height or focus ring on a text box or select) is refused by `web/scripts/check-fields.mjs`; boxes that sit inside a field (the chat composer, the room switcher, the palette's search, a join code's secret) are listed there.
- **Deleted Docs live at the foot of the list** (#2641). The Docs page's header says only its title: the line under it and the Docs count beside it are gone, since the list's groups carry counts.

Deleting a team Doc shows a toast with **Undo**. After that, the Doc waits for 30 days in a **Deleted** group at the foot of the Docs list, folded, and only there when something is deleted. A deleted Doc opens beside the list with who deleted it, when, the days it has left and **Restore** (owners, editors and its maker). The header's Trash link and its card list are gone.

## Fixed

- **A card's sessions update live on its board row** (#2644). A board row's sessions, and those in its drawer, show a cloud workspace
session's state as it changes: when it starts waiting on you, its row says
**needs input** within a few seconds, without reloading the page. A
session on a computer still updates about once a minute while the board is
open.
