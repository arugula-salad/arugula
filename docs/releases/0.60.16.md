# Arugula 0.60.16

Made as a deploy.

## New

- **Control vouches for the Mac app's passkeys** (#1460). Control serves
  `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association`, naming the desktop app
  (`HNDPMQPF22.io.arugula.desktop`) for `webcredentials`, so macOS lets
  the app's window use your passkeys on control's page. A self-hosted
  control serves it only when `--apple-team-id` is set. The app side comes
  in app 0.30.1.
- **A live stream of your notices for the desktop app** (#1461). Control
  can now deliver what it pushes (chat, panes on any machine, inbox,
  deploys, runs, workspaces, account notices) to the desktop app over a
  stream, as well as to Web Push, filtered by the same Push settings.
  Each device listens at one stream with its own token, which no other
  account or device can take over. An account holds at most 50 push
  subscriptions, and its pushes are capped per hour. The app side comes
  in app 0.30.1.
