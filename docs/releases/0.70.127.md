## Changed

- **iPhone and iPad show the one Inbox and its count** (#2905). The bell's number is the server's count, the same as on the web. It no longer adds deploys waiting or threads with news, and it updates as things change instead of every minute. The app icon's badge shows the same count. The Inbox groups what needs you as Decide, Names you and Chat. A teammate's asks fold under one row, such as "2 asks for Jake ›". "Ready, merged or closed" is gone. Anything the app doesn't recognise still shows as a plain row. You can answer in the row itself:
- An agent's card with its own buttons, such as Start or Not now.
- A to-do with Done or Can't, and an optional note.
- A board's ask with its options or your own words.
- An agent's question or permission prompt, as Needs you answers it.

Dismiss still clears an item. A deploy waiting for you opens its approval. When an item clears, its notification goes away. `arugula://inbox/<item>` opens the Inbox at that item and marks it seen, as on Android.
- **Android shows the one Inbox** (#2904). The bell's number is the Inbox's count as the server says it, across your projects, and it moves as soon as the Inbox changes: the app no longer adds waiting deploys or thread news of its own. The sheet lists what needs you or names you in three groups (Decide, Names you, Chat), without the "Ready, merged or closed" group. An agent's question or permission on one of your machines is answered under its row, an agent's card with its options, and a to-do with Done or Can't and an optional note; Dismiss puts any item away. Asks that wait on a teammate fold under "2 asks for Jo ›" and aren't counted for you. The Overview's Needs you now lists the project's deploys waiting for your approval, as the iPhone's does.
