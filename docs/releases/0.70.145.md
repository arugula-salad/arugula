## Changed

- **A Doc's page is its paper, with its contents beside it** (#2948). A project Doc's page draws the Doc as a sheet of paper. Beside it, On this page lists its written sections, marking the one you're reading and the ones with a figure (◧); a click scrolls to it. Under that, Linked lists the boards it's on, which used to be a line under the Doc. Comments stay in the margin beside their paragraph, now highlighted in the Doc's own blue. In the app, the agent that wrote the Doc is a tab of the agent panel, folded to a strip: `\` opens it. Folding it there doesn't change your panel on other pages.
- **The Inbox lists every project's items, answered in place** (#2953). The Inbox now lists what wants you in all your projects, under each project's name, with the project you're in first. Asks, permissions, approvals and to-dos come first and answer in place, as on the Overview's Needs you. Reviews, mentions, chat and failed checks on your PRs follow, and each can be dismissed. Press `↵` to go to an item, in whatever project, and `esc` to come back. A teammate's asks fold into one line ("2 asks for Jo ›").

The rail's Inbox badge is the server's count, and each project's mark shows that project's share. The Inbox no longer has an All projects toggle or deploy cards (a deploy's row opens its page).
- **A few colors follow the roles** (#2951). A merged change shows as done, and the few teal, peach and pink marks take the review and needs-you colors, so every color on the page comes from your Look.
