Not on a board is pinned at the top of the Boards list with its count; pick it and its issues fill the middle, with Order and the labels beside them.

## Changed

- **A board's controls are on the right** (#2651). On the Boards page, the board keeps the middle to itself. Its controls are in a column at the right under small headings: **View** (Lanes or Steps), **Find** (the search, with the Label and Person menus, and a **Group** menu on a board with groups), **Docs**, **Add** (Add row, Add a group, New issue) and **Board** (Workers and ⋯).

A row's details open in that column, in place of the controls, so the board stays in sight beside them. **‹ Board controls** or Esc goes back. On a narrower screen the column moves under the board and a row's details open over the page, as before. A board in a workspace pane keeps its one column.
- **arugula login signs in from a browser** (#2653). Since browsers stopped holding device keys, a new account had nothing to approve the code `arugula login` showed. Now `arugula login` signs in the way `--browser` did, and Arugula cloud gives the CLI a session of its own kind: it reaches your machines and does what the CLI does, but can't approve sign-ins, devices, joins or deploys, add passkeys or change a team's members, since an agent on that machine can read its file. The Allow page says whether the CLI or the desktop app is asking. `arugula login --device` keeps the old way, and an Arugula cloud too old for a CLI's session gets it too, with a message saying so. `--browser` still works.
- **Settings is in one place** (#2645). Your machines page's Settings now opens the app's Settings. Old `#settings` links land on the matching section: Appearance, Preferences and Account under You, and a machine's own settings under This machine. Remote sessions, the list of who reached a machine through Arugula cloud, is now a section of This machine.

Phones and browsers stays on your machines page, because a device turns its notifications on with its own keys. The ↗ rows in Settings still open it there.

On your machines page, a machine's menu lists its settings again (Access, Permission rules and the rest). In the app, the menu opens This machine in Settings. A machine's own page, served by its daemon with no Arugula cloud, keeps its whole Settings page.
- **Not on a board is the first row of Boards** (#2655). **Not on a board** is now the first row of a project's Boards list, with how many open issues are on no board, and it stays there whatever you search for. Pick it and its issues fill the middle. Order and the labels, each with its count, are in the column on the right, and a label filters the issues.

Drag an issue, or a selection of them, onto a board's row in the list, or press ＋ to pick a board. The Not on a board link from every project's Boards selects this row. On a phone it's the first row too, and its issues open in the list's place.

## Fixed

- **No hang talking to a restarted daemon on macOS** (#2654). A pane's program and the process that keeps it alive across restarts could
take a copy of a socket the daemon was opening at the same moment (macOS
can't make a socket close-on-exec as it's made). They now close anything
like that as they start.
