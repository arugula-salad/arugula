## New

### People

For everyone in a team; there's no Labs flag.

- **A People block** (#1792). Right-click a pane, then *Open beside → People*, and pick one of your teams. The block is the team's room on [arugula.stream](https://arugula.stream), drawn by its own elements: a tile per person there, with their avatar (a character that follows their face) or outline while their camera is on, reactions and waves. The room has a drop-in voice channel (join muted, unmute, start video), and a wave can turn into a private call, where real video stands in for the avatar. Control signs your room token as you (`POST /api/chat/presence/token`, a team's members only, named as its roster names them) and keeps nothing of the room. The page joins the room only while the block is shown.
- **Speaker view** (#1792). In voice or a call, a *Grid / Speaker* toggle: Speaker shows whoever's talking big (their video, or their character), everyone else in it in a strip, and the room under them. Click someone to pin them big, and again to follow whoever's talking. The block remembers your choice.

### Boards

Still behind the `boards` Labs flag.

- **Boards on the rail** (#1799, native #338). The left rail reads Panes, Swarm, Chat, Boards, Docs, Agents; Boards opens the platform's projects, each with its boards. The places bar and the phone sheet follow the same order.
- **A row an agent added says so** (#1798, native #336): a dim `agent · <machine>` label in the row.

## Changed

- **Say "board", not "status board"** (#1798, native #337): in the tools' titles, grants, the `show` kind, the Labs flag's description and the CLI help.
- **The newest deploy request wins** (#1795, native #334). Asking for a deploy marks the environment's older open requests stale; they drop out of the inbox and can't be approved or started. The deploy page and Control's modal say a newer deploy was asked for, and link it. A release of a commit that's already released is refused when it's planned ("b1faaa04 is already in v0.63.8").
- **`just release` stops when it would leave merges out** (#1769, #1679). It lists the newer commits on main that aren't green yet and exits; `--older` releases without them.
- **One outline bell everywhere** (#1800). Chat's room bell is the header's outline bell (struck through at "nothing") instead of an emoji.
- **For contributors:** e2e's allow-app step waits on the app becoming a device (#1772, #1674), and the sessions test expects "Chrome on Mac" on macOS (#1773).

## Fixed

- **Needs you opens an item under its row** (#1800). The open item's detail used to sit after the whole list, as if the bottom row were open; now it opens under the row you picked and scrolls into view, and Up/Down move between rows only.

## Note

- No migrations.
- People blocks need control to have an arugula.stream app: `ARUGULA_PRESENCE_APP` and `ARUGULA_PRESENCE_SECRET` on the platform, and the app id on the hub, whose page then lets scripts load from the relay (`docs/control-ops.md`). Hosted control has one ("Arugula People"); a self-hosted control without one shows a People block saying so, and its CSP doesn't change.
- With a People block open, arugula.stream's relay gets your account id, your name on the team's roster and the team's id, and while your camera is on, your avatar's movements or your outline (never a picture). Voice and video go through LiveKit Cloud. See "Who else sees it" in `docs/control-e2e.md`.
