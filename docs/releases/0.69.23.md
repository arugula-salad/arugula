## Changed

- **Projects grouped by owner, and team pages** (#2475). Archived projects wait behind "Show archived". On a phone the owners come first, then one owner's projects. Moving a project to another owner revokes the grants over it.

## Fixed

- **A Doc's prototype runs after you switch to it** (#2474). A Doc's prototype no longer sometimes says "This prototype tried to leave the page" right after you switch to the Doc, on iPhone, Android or the desktop app. It runs.
- **Copy link works on a Doc in a pane** (#2414). Copy link asks the machine for the Doc's link, and WebKit refused a clipboard write that came after that wait. The copy now starts with the click and fills in when the link arrives. Share's confirm and the share dialog's copy work the same way.
