## Added

- **Put a session on a card** (#2571). On the project's Sessions page, a computer's session in Not on a card has **Put on a card**, and one on a card has **Move to another card** when its row is open. Both pick from the project's open cards, and the session moves at once; if the machine can't take it, it moves back and says why. **Not on a card** in the same list takes it back to the card its branch gives (a `feat/N-…` branch's `#N`), or to none. In the app, a card's drawer offers **Change card** for the session it shows, the same picker the pane chip's **Put on a card** uses.

The whole session moves, and every pane in it that has no card of its own follows, as when its branch puts it on one. The card is kept on the machine, so it survives a restart, and a branch never moves a session someone put on a card. This needs a machine on this release and an Arugula cloud that forwards the change to it; until then the page says it can't move sessions yet.
