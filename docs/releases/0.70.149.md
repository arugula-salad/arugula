## Added

- **Chat has an Agents section** (#2966). A project's Chat has an **Agents** section under Direct messages, which folds like the others. It lists:

- your **chief of staff** (no longer among Direct messages);
- **#agents**, for work across boards such as deploys, merge order and holds, once the project has it;
- a **#** channel for each open board, with a dot when the board has a lead. Closed boards fold under **archived**.

Issue comments aren't listed. Beside #agents or a board's channel, a column says who's in it and who a post reaches. A board channel's column also has Open the board. A thread there opens in the channel's own column, and `esc` or "‹ #channel" goes back. In Chat, `j` and `k` move through the Agents rows and `g a` opens #agents.
- **Tags in #agents and board channels** (#2967). In #agents and a board's channel, typing `@` lists `@channel`, `@here`, `@cos` (your chief of staff) and each board's lead. In #agents, the delivery rule is written under the list. A tag is sent with its board's id, so renaming a board doesn't break it. Tags show as pills.

The line under the box you write in says who a post reaches: "Every lead gets this, no tag needed" in #agents, and "Its lead gets this · @here adds active workers" in a board's channel. Once Arugula cloud reports it, each post shows who it reached, such as "✓ reached: Design lift, A2P" or "✓ reached: no lead · it tagged none".

## Changed

- **A prompt beside a board shows its terminal** (#2974). A prompt pane, an agent's pending permission or question that its lead put beside a board or in a tab for you, now shows the question at the top, answered in place, with the agent's terminal under it. Before, it showed the question alone.
- **A run beside a board shows the run** (#2977). A run opened beside a board, by you or by its lead, now shows the run's own page in the pane: its state, what started it, its timeline and its log, kept current while it runs. The pane's × closes it; the run's page is still at its address.
- **Running, every live process in one drawer** (#2978). The top bar now says how many processes are running across your machines. Clicking it opens **Running**, a drawer at the right: the project's red branches first ("main has been red for 3 hours"), then each machine's live agents, servers and shells, then the project's CI runs. Up and Down move through it, and a process's last lines show under the list. On a board, **Pin to desk** puts the process beside the board. Esc closes it.

## Fixed

- **An approved start's tab moves to its issue's project** (#2976). An approved start's tab moves to its issue's project even when a same-name tab was in another project.
