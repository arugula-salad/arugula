# Code of conduct

Arugula's issues, pull requests, Discussions and any other place we talk
about it should be somewhere anyone can ask a question, report a bug or
send a change without being made to feel small.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep it on topic, and take no for an answer.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content, threats, or personal
  attacks, and no publishing someone's private information.
- No spam or self-promotion that isn't about Arugula.

This applies to everyone, maintainers included, in the project's spaces
and wherever someone is speaking for the project.

## Reporting

Email **hello@arugula.io** with what happened, where (links help) and who
was involved. A maintainer reads it, and we keep who reported it private.
If the report is about a maintainer, say so and someone else handles it.

## What happens

Depending on what happened and whether it keeps happening, a maintainer
may:

1. tell the person privately what was wrong and ask them to stop;
2. hide or delete the comments, issues or pull requests;
3. ban them for a while; or
4. ban them for good.

Harassment and threats can go straight to a ban.

This is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
shortened.
