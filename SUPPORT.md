# Getting help

- **A question** (how do I…, is this expected…): ask in
  [Discussions, Q&A](https://github.com/arugula-salad/arugula/discussions/categories/q-a).
  Answers there stay findable for the next person.
- **Something's broken:** check the
  [known issues](https://docs.arugula.io/known-issues/) first, then
  [open a bug](https://github.com/arugula-salad/arugula/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
  with `arugula --version`, your OS and the steps that show it.
- **An idea:** post it in
  [Discussions, Ideas](https://github.com/arugula-salad/arugula/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **A security problem:** report it privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
  says. Please don't open an issue.

## When you'll hear back

During the launch weeks, within one working day. A person answers; agents
may draft replies and sort issues, but a person reads and posts them.

There's no Discord or chat server yet. Please keep to the
[code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
